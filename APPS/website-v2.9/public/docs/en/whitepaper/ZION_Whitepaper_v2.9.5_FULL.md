## 1. Vision & mission

ZION TerraNova is positioned as a next-generation **Layer 1** combining:

- **Proof-of-work** for verifiable security  
- **Consciousness mining** — gamified rewards tied to participation / “consciousness” framing  
- **Humanitarian tithe** — automatic protocol routing of a significant block-reward share to humanitarian ends (figures in the full doc: historically stated up to ~10–25 % depending on section/era)  
- **AI Native** — AI integrated into the ecosystem narrative and tooling  

The project contrasts itself with “speculation-only” coins by emphasizing **real-world impact** and **community governance**.
