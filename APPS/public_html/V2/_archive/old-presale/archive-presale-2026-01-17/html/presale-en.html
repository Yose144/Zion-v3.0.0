What is the ZION token?

ZION is a next-generation cryptocurrency anchored in the concept of "consciousness mining" that blends ASIC-resistant algorithms with humanitarian funding.

We target the ZION mainnet for December 31, 2027. Before that, ZION 2.9 (core + P2P) must pass the functional public testnet and security audits. The presale will go live only after that checkpoint so funding aligns with a production-ready stack.

What is a QR wallet?

A QR wallet is a unique QR code that stores your tokens. Once the mainnet goes live you scan it inside the ZION wallet app to receive the tokens on-chain.

Are payments secure?

Yes. Stripe, the global leader in online payments, processes every card transaction. Your payment data is encrypted and never touches our servers.
