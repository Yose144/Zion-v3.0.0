## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Language Guidelines

✅ **Use:**
- "Core contributors"
- "Guardians"
- "Community"
- "Protocol"

❌ **Never use:**
- "Company"
- "CEO"
- "Roadmap guarantees"
- "Investment opportunity"

### Public Communication

Always include disclaimer:
> "ZION is an experimental open-source protocol. Not an investment. No guarantees. Use at your own risk."
