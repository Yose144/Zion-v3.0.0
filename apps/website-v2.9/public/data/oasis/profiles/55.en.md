## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Hunahpú** and **Xbalanqué** are a **pair** — the heroes of the **Popol Vuh** who descend into **Xibalba** and win by **cunning and recognition**, not by brute force.

---

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Location:** Xibalba — a nine-layered underworld (dungeon meta).
- **Mode:** **co-op** (not a solo avatar — the pair as one quest slot).
- **Teacher:** **CL3–CL6** — strategy instead of brutal force.

---

## 📜 QUEST LINE

**"Nine Houses of Xibalba"** — logic puzzles in each house; the boss **Hun-Camé** is defeated by **cunning**.

---

## ⚡ ABILITY

**Gemstone Fall** — a narrative debuff of a strong enemy by exposing pride (*see the appendix*).
