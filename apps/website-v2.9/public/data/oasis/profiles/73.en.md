## 🌟 Who is MILAREPA?

Milarepa (Mila Thöpa Gawa, "Mila, Radiant in Hearing") is a Tibetan yogi, poet and saint who attained enlightenment in a single lifetime despite a terrible past. He first learned black magic to avenge his mother, but later underwent extreme asceticism under the master Marpa. In ZION OASIS he embodies the possibility of transformation — from murderer to saint. He is the patron of those who carry heavy karma.

As an avatar he wears a nettle cloak (turned green by the plants) and sings dohas (dohā, spiritual songs) that destroy illusions and heal wounded hearts.

---

## 📖 Origin and Story

He was born in Gungthang as a merchant's son. After his father's death, relatives seized the property and abused his mother. Mila learned black magic and sent a hailstorm that killed 35 relatives. The horror of the deed led him to Marpa Lotsawa, who tested him by having him build towers and then destroy them. Mila nearly poisoned himself in despair, but after receiving the real teachings he spent years in caves — Lapchi, Tisé (Kailash) and Drin. His songs became classics.

> *In the cave of Drin it was so cold that Mila had to meditate in a nettle cloak. When hunters came, they were frightened by his green body. "Are you a demon?" they asked. Mila laughed and sang: "I am a man who ate nettles and drank water. My misery taught me that there is no difference between pain and bliss for one who knows their own mind." The hunters fell to their knees.*

## 🧭 Spiritual Teachings

**1. Karma is inexorable, but not unchangeable**
Milarepa proved that even heavy violent karma can be overcome by sincere repentant effort (ngöndro).
> *In ZION: A "forgiveness" mechanic — the player can reset negative reputation with factions through a demanding but possible quest chain.*

**2. Renunciation as strength (Tsül)**
Not comfort, but extreme simplicity frees the mind from attachment. Milarepa's nettle cloak is the symbol.
> *In ZION: An "asceticism buff" — a player with minimal equipment gains bonuses to meditation and mana.*

**3. Natural mind (Rigpa)**
As a singer of dohas Milarepa taught that truth needs no complicated theories. Direct recognition is available to all.
> *In ZION: "Cave Song" — the player can activate places of power by simple singing, without runes and keys.*

## 🎮 Role in ZION OASIS

**🏔️ Location: The Cave of Lapchi**
A snow cave with ice formations where time runs slower. Players meditate for temporary buffs, but must feed on nettles (an in-game item).

**⚙️ Mechanic: Doha Resonance**
Milarepa's songs create sound waves that destroy illusory walls and harm beings based on deception (shadow creatures).

**📜 Quest concept: Marpa's Tower**
Players build symbolic towers (block puzzles) under the supervision of Marpa's NPC. Each tower unlocks another line of Milarepa's teachings.

---

## 📜 Quest Line: The Saga of the Nettle Cloak

**Phase 1: The Hailstorm of Karma**
*Task:* Confront Mila in the form of the "black magician" and persuade him to set aside revenge.
*Lore:* The player finds themselves in Mila's past and changes the NPC's decision through empathy, not force.
*Reward:* Item "Stone of Regret" — allows averting a fatal blow once per day.

**Phase 2: Towers and Ruins**
*Task:* Build 3 stone towers in the zone of Marpa's court. Each must be destroyed and built again.
*Lore:* A test of patience; frustration is part of the training.
*Reward:* Title "Stone Bearer" and +20% resistance to stun effects.

**Phase 3: Nettles and Water**
*Task:* Survive 3 days in the cave with minimal resources, collect nettles for crafting the cloak.
*Lore:* Physical hardship purifies the ego. The player discovers that survival is possible even without comfort.
*Reward:* Skin "Nettle Yogi" and the ability to survive in extreme biomes.

**Phase 4: Song beneath Kailash**
*Task:* Sing a doha in the temple beneath Mount Tisé (Kailash), summon Milarepa's final aspect.
*Lore:* The song must be composed by the player — the system uses a rhythm mini-game.
*Reward:* Unique spell "Voice of Emptiness" — strong AoE against illusions and powerful mental attacks.

## 🔗 Connections to Other Avatars

**Avatar 72 — PADMASAMBHAVA**
Milarepa draws on terma hidden by Padmasambhava. In ZION their connection is a "secret passage" between Samye monastery and the cave of Lapchi, where players carry teachings.

**Avatar 74 — TSONGKHAPA**
Tsongkhapa criticised extreme asceticism without an ethical foundation; Milarepa defended it with the heart. In ZION their dialogues unlock a quest about the balance between discipline and inspiration.

**Avatar 50 — NĀROPA**
Nāropa was the teacher of Marpa, thus the "great-great-grandfather" of Milarepa's teaching. In the ZION system they form the "Kagyu lineage", which players can "unlock" gradually.
