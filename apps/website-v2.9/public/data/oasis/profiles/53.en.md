## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Ptesáŋ Wíŋ** is a **mythic being**, not a historical person — a **sacred messenger** who gives the **Sacred Pipe** and teaches the **seven sacred rites**. In OASIS she is a **mentor of crisis** and transformation.

---

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Location:** the Plains — an open horizon, a temple that is **not fixed** (it appears and departs).
- **Ability:** **Transformation** — colours white → black → red → yellow (see the appendix).
- **Teacher:** all players; **strengthening in crisis** (e.g. low HP → an encounter on the plain).

---

## 📜 QUEST LINE

**"Seven Sacred Rites"** — seven ritual steps (Inípi, Hanbléčeyapi, … per the appendix); the final understanding of **one family**.

---

## ⚡ ABILITY

**Sacred Pipe Blessing** — a narrative encounter in the hardest moments; **no monetized ceremonies** (see the ethics in the appendix).
