## 🌟 Who is MAUI?

MAUI is a demigod of Hawaiian and wider Polynesian mythology, son of Hina and a maker (creator) of heavenly and earthly wonders. Born as a kupua — a being with superior powers yet still bound to human passions. In ZION OASIS he represents flexible courage, inventiveness and a deep connection to both ocean and sky. He is a guide for those who must face unimaginable tasks with small means. MAUI is not a perfect hero; he is a trickster who teaches through his mistakes.

As an avatar in ZION he carries his magic hook Manaiakalani and a rope of pohutukawa fibre. Players meet him at the boundary of fire and water, where he always draws the line between night and day.

---

## 📖 Origin and Story

Maui was thrown into the ocean as a premature child, but the goddess Hina saved him. The god Tāne gave him a magic hook and taught him the language of birds. With that hook Maui pulled the Hawaiian islands out of the depths of the Pacific, tethered to his canoe. When people suffered because the sun Lā raced across the sky too fast, Maui traded his net for a rope and on the volcano Haleakalā tethered the sun, forcing it to slow down.

> *Maui stood on the caldera. In his hand he held a rope of sacred tree fibre. "Slow down, Lā!" he cried and threw the noose. The sun caught, flames licked the sky, but Maui held. "Will you give me light enough time to live?" The sun nodded. And since then the day lasts long enough for fish to dry and children to grow.*

## 🧭 Spiritual Teachings

**1. Inventiveness (Hoʻomaikaʻi)**
Maui does not fight with straightforward force, but with cunning and tools. He teaches that limitations are an invitation to a surprising solution.
> *In ZION: A player with low stats can overcome bosses through the environment and timing, not only raw strength.*

**2. Connection with Kai (sea) and ʻĀina (land)**
Every move of Maui's hook joins sky, land and sea. He teaches respect for all elements as allies.
> *In ZION: An "elemental affinity" system — the player gains bonuses when they respect the local ecosystem.*

**3. Responsibility for the gift**
When Maui stole fire for humanity, he burned his fingers too. Every gift has a price and must be used for the community.
> *In ZION: Abilities stolen from enemies carry a "karmic price" — using them without judgement harms the user too.*

## 🎮 Role in ZION OASIS

**🏝️ Location: The Haleakalā Chain**
Volcanic islands with active calderas, where the sun changes speed by the day. Players collect "sun ash" (Lā-poho) here for crafting.

**⚙️ Mechanic: Time Manipulation (Kālai)**
Maui can slow time in a local bubble for 15 seconds of real time. This allows solving environmental puzzles with fast-moving platforms.

**📜 Quest concept: Fishing for islands**
Players help Maui pull "lost data islands" from the depths of the server ocean each season. Success expands the map.

## 📜 Quest Line: The Saga of the Sun Hook

**Phase 1: A Noose of Pohutukawa**
*Task:* Collect 7 fibres from sacred trees on the coast.
*Lore:* Maui needs a new rope; the old one is fraying under the pressure of time.
*Reward:* Access to the Haleakalā mountain trail.

**Phase 2: Climbing the House of the Sun**
*Task:* Pass the wind scarecrows (ʻAʻā) on the caldera ridge without fighting, only by hiding.
*Lore:* Violence on the sacred mountain wakes Pélé. Cunning is better than a sword.
*Reward:* Talisman "Tāne's Breathing" (+30% stealth in wind).

**Phase 3: Hook into Lā**
*Task:* Cast Maui's hook into the solar portal at the exact time (noon).
*Lore:* The sun resists; the player must read its movement in the shadows.
*Reward:* Ability "Bonds of Lā" — 15 s time slow, 5 min cooldown.

**Phase 4: The Agreement of the Day**
*Task:* Negotiate with the Sun avatar (NPC Lā) a lasting peace between night and day.
*Lore:* Maui must admit that even he needs rest. Negotiation via dialogue trees.
*Reward:* Title "Kupua" and access to night fishing with unique loots.

## 🔗 Connections to Other Avatars

**Avatar 42 — TANGAROA (God of the Ocean)**
Maui and Tangaroa are rivals and allies. Tangaroa gives him access to the deep zones, but Maui must pay a "fishing tax" — every third loot goes to the sea.

**Avatar 58 — PÉLÉ (Goddess of Fire)**
Pélé inhabits the volcano on which Maui lassoed the sun. They respect each other, but players often have to balance their conflicting quests (fire vs. water).

**Avatar 69 — KĀNE (God of Life)**
Kāne gave Maui strength, but takes it away when the demigod behaves selfishly. Their relationship is mentor-student with regular tests of character.
