*K'iche' / Yucatec / Classic Maya | Tortuguero Monument 6 | Transition, not an end*

---

> *"The thirteenth bak'tun will be completed. There will come the display of Bolon-Yokte' — in a great investiture."*
> — Tortuguero Monument 6, block O4–P6 (transcription Houston & Stuart 1996, rev. Gronemeyer & MacLeod 2010)

---

## 🌟 ESSENCE

**B'olon Yokte' K'uh** is an **over-pantheon god** — older than individual cities, broader than one lineage. The name means **"Nine Steps"** or **"Nine Supports"**. He appears at the **ends of great cycles** — not as a destroyer, but as a **god of transitions, of changing clothes, of new beginnings**.

- **War** — the rope around the neck symbolises a captive, but also **bonds that must be broken**
- **Underworld** — he comes from Xibalba, but brings no ruin; he brings **transformation**
- **Time** — from 250 AD to 1800 AD; depicted on a 7th-century balance and in colonial texts
- **Prophecy** — on Tortuguero Monument 6 there is the **only** known hieroglyphic text that explicitly refers to the end of the 13th bak'tun (2012)

> **This is not an apocalypse.** The epigraphic community (Stuart, Houston, Gronemeyer, MacLeod) long ago refuted that Monument 6 predicts the "end of the world". The text speaks of an **investiture** (joyaj) — a ceremony of changing clothes, of display, of a festive beginning. Bolon Yokte "descends" (yem) just as the god K'awiil "descended" at Palenque in 931 449 BCE — as a **positive, inaugural act**.

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Location:** Tortuguero Ruins — a temple complex in virtual Tabasco, where Monument 6 is located.
- **Mode:** **Solo avatar** with the option of **dual transformation** — the player can switch between a "shadow" (underworld) and "light" (inaugural) form after completing the quest.
- **Teacher:** **CL7–CL9** — accepting the end of a cycle as the condition for a new beginning. The hardest lesson: to accept that some things **must end** so that others can begin.
- **Special mechanic:** **Bak'tun Reset** — once per game season, a player with Bolon Yokte can "reset" their skill tree without losing overall CL (a cosmetic+mechanic hybrid).

## 📜 QUEST LINE

**"Nine Steps of Transition"**

1. **Step 1: Recognition** — find Tortuguero Monument 6 in the rainforest; read the eroded glyphs.
2. **Step 2: Bonds** — uncover the rope symbol: who is the captive, and who the guard?
3. **Step 3: Xibalba** — descend into the nine levels of the underworld (a parallel to the Hunahpú & Xbalanqué quest #55).
4. **Step 4: Tree** — find the Ceiba that grows on the boundary of three worlds.
5. **Step 5: Time** — understand the difference between linear and cyclical time (a calendar puzzle).
6. **Step 6: Death as creation** — a cinematic flashback: the scene from *The Fountain* where Tommy accepts Izzi's death.
7. **Step 7: Investiture** — the ceremony of changing clothes: the player sets aside the old avatar skin and takes a new one.
8. **Step 8: Sun** — birth a new cycle: activate a Genesis block in the temple courtyard.
9. **Step 9: Display** — Bolon Yokte "displays" himself in full form; the player gains a permanent ability.

---

## ⚡ ABILITY

**Bak'tun Shift** — a one-time ability that **"resets" the local game state** in a given zone (e.g. cancels all active buffs/debuffs of NPCs and players) and generates a new random loot pool. In lore: the ceremony of ending and beginning. In gameplay: a high-risk, high-reward tactical reset.

*Cooldown: once per game month (or a real-time event).*
