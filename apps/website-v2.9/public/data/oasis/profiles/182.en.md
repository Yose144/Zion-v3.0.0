## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Valhalla** in the eddic field is the hall of Óðinn's **einherjar** — a day of battle, a night of feasting. In OASIS this archetype **promises no** posthumous reward; it teaches **team recovery**, **ring rules** and an **anti-burnout** rhythm between "raid" and regeneration.

---

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Quest:** "Einherjar Etiquette Table" — the player sets a **codex** for internal trials (testnet duels) without dehumanizing opponents.
- **Ability:** **Roast-Rest Cycle** — a short **cooldown buff** after an intense sequence (anti-grind).
