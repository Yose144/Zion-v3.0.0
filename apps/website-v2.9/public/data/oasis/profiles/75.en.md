## 🌟 Who is ATIŠA?

Atiśa Dīpaṃkara Śrījñāna (980–1054) was an Indian Buddhist master from Bengal who restored Buddhism in Tibet and wrote the *Bodhipathapradīpa* (Lamp for the Path to Enlightenment). Founder of the Kadampa tradition, teacher of lojong (mind training) and master of the gradual path. In ZION OASIS he is a guide for those who have lost themselves in haste — a saint of small steps and great patience.

As an avatar he carries a real lamp whose flame never goes out as long as the bearer thinks of others. His presence calms chaotic zones and reveals true paths in labyrinths.

---

## 📖 Origin and Story

Atiśa was born as a prince in Bengal, but gave up the throne and studied under Dharmakīrti and Dharmarakṣita at the university of Vikramaśīla. He travelled 13 months to the Golden Isle (Suvarnadvipa, Sumatra), where he studied lojong with the elder master Dharmakīrti. In 1042 he came to Tibet at the invitation of King Jangchub Ö and, with the translator Rinchen Sangpo, restored the pure teaching. He died in Nethang in 1054.

> *In the Nethang valley Atiśa sat with a lamp in his hand. Tibetan disciples asked him: "Why did you come so far?" He answered: "I came because I hear that your lamas have forgotten the foundation. A lamp is useless if you put it in a draught. You must build a glass — the glass of ethics, the glass of patience." Then he lit a candle and added: "And then you will see that the flame was always in you."*

## 🧭 Spiritual Teachings

**1. The gradual path (Bodhipathapradīpa)**
Enlightenment is not a leap but a staircase. Atiśa systematically ordered practice from ethics through compassion to the wisdom of emptiness.
> *In ZION: A "gradual development system" — players cannot skip the basic quest lines; each level unlocks a new one, but the old ones remain relevant.*

**2. Mind training (Lojong)**
Thoughts are like a wild horse — they must be trained, not beaten. The motto "Think of others" is the core of the practice.
> *In ZION: "Exchange of self for others" — the player can temporarily take an ally's debuff onto themselves and gain karmic buffs for it.*

**3. Tonglen — the breath of compassion**
Inhale the suffering of others, exhale healing. Atiśa taught that self-sacrifice is the fastest path.
> *In ZION: A "pain transfer" mechanic — a healer can absorb party damage and transform it into AoE healing.*

## 🎮 Role in ZION OASIS

**🏛️ Location: The Ruins of Vikramaśīla**
A restored university ruin with libraries where book bosses ("evil interpretations") spawn. Players study old texts here.

**⚙️ Mechanic: Lamp of Wisdom**
Atiśa's lamp creates a 20m aura that reveals the correct path in mazes and removes illusions. In dark zones it is the only source of light.

**📜 Quest concept: Relighting the Lamp**
Players carry Atiśa's lamp through dark zones and light incense on altars. Each lit altar permanently illuminates part of the map for all players on the server.

---

## 📜 Quest Line: The Saga of the Bodhipathapradīpa

**Phase 1: The Prince's Departure**
*Task:* Visit the Bengali palace and persuade the young Atiśa to give up the throne.
*Lore:* The player becomes the "voice of destiny" and learns that power without wisdom is a prison.
*Reward:* Item "Prince's Ring" — reduces trade prices by 15% (wisdom over property).

**Phase 2: The Journey to the Golden Isle**
*Task:* Cross the ocean by ship and survive 3 storms through decisions based on ethics, not force.
*Lore:* The path to learning requires sacrifice. The player must throw cargo overboard to save the crew.
*Reward:* Title "Pilgrim" and the ability to survive on water without a mount.

**Phase 3: Teaching in Tibet**
*Task:* Translate 5 concepts from Sanskrit into Tibetan (mini-game) and teach them to NPC monks.
*Lore:* The transmission of teaching is itself an act of sanctity. Language is a bridge.
*Reward:* Potion "Languages of the Dharma" — temporary understanding of all NPC dialects.

**Phase 4: The Lamp in Nethang**
*Task:* Protect Atiśa in the last valley from the "shadow inquisition" and light 108 lamps.
*Lore:* The master's death is not an end but the passing of the flame. The player lights lamps for future generations.
*Reward:* Final ability "Lamp on the Path" and the title "Kadampa".

## 🔗 Connections to Other Avatars

**Avatar 74 — TSONGKHAPA**
Tsongkhapa is the direct spiritual grandson of Atiśa's Kadampa line. In ZION they jointly lead the quest "Lamp and Hat" about the transmission of teaching from India through Tibet.

**Avatar 72 — PADMASAMBHAVA**
Padmasambhava and Atiśa are two pillars of Tibetan Buddhism — tantra and the gradual path. Their meeting in ZION unlocks a "conversation of the century" with bonus lore.

**Avatar 68 — DHARMAKĪRTI**
Dharmakīrti was Atiśa's teacher at Vikramaśīla. In the ZION system they form the "Indian line" together with Nāgārjuna; players must "pass through" this line to unlock Atiśa.
