# Glossary — Ekam Deeksha

*An extended vocabulary of terms*

---

## A

**Advaita Vedānta** (अद्वैत वेदान्त)
A philosophical school of Hinduism whose central thesis is *advaita* — "non-duality". Reality is one indivisible consciousness (Brahman). All apparent multiplicity is a manifestation of the same. Key representatives: Ādi Śaṅkarācārya (8th century), Ramana Maharshi (20th century), Nisargadatta Maharaj (20th century).

**Agni** (अग्नि)
"Fire" in Sanskrit; the Vedic deity of fire, the mediator between people and the gods. In the ritual of *yajña* (the sacrificial fire) Agni carries the offerings to the higher powers. In the Ekam line a metaphor for transformative Deeksha: the fire of consciousness that consumes charges.

**Amma** (Sri Amma)
Sri Padmavathi Amma, b. 15 August 1954. Co-founder of the Ekam line and Oneness University together with Bhagavan. She symbolises the feminine principle — love, Shakti, immediate unconditional grace. Her physical transmission is Sparsha Deeksha — touch.

---

## B

**Beautiful State**
A term introduced into the modern terminology of the Ekam line especially by Krishnaji and Preethaji (*Four Sacred Secrets*, 2019). It denotes the natural inner state of consciousness without chronic reactivity: calm, openness, presence, joy not bound to conditions. The counterpart of the Suffering State.

**Bhagavan** (Sri Kalki Bhagavan)
Sri Vijaykumar Naidu, b. 7 March 1949 in Nathur, Andhra Pradesh. Co-founder of Oneness University and the Ekam line. The honorific *Kalki* refers to the tenth avatar of Vishnu — a being whose coming means the transition from Kaliyuga into a new era. He symbolises the masculine principle — insight, Shiva, conscious awakening.

**Bindu** (बिन्दु)
"Point" in Sanskrit; in the geometry of the Sri Chakra it denotes the central point from which the whole complex structure of the mandala proceeds. In meditation *bindu* is the place of concentration behind physical sight. In the Shaiva-tantric tradition Bindu is the condensed form of consciousness from which the created universe unfolds.

**Brahma** (ब्रह्मा)
The personal creative aspect of God in the Hindu triad (Brahma-Vishnu-Shiva). While Brahman is the impersonal absolute, Brahma is "the one who creates multiplicity". In the Cosmic Harmony v3 algorithm: phase 1, the expansion of the seed into a 256 KiB working space — Brahma as the unfolding of oneness into multiplicity.

**Brahman** (ब्रह्मन्)
The impersonal absolute reality in Advaita Vedānta. It is not God in the theistic sense — it is consciousness as such, without boundaries, without attributes, the basis of everything. The identification of the individual *Ātman* with Brahman is the goal of Advaita.

---

## C

**Charges**
Bhagavan's term for hard emotional blocks encoded in the nervous system. They arise from undigested past experiences — childhood wounds, repeated patterns, traumas. Charges are not merely memories — they are patterns of reactivity that are activated in situations similar to the original experience. Work with charges is a central part of the Oneness University programmes.

**Chit** (चित्)
"Consciousness" in Sanskrit; the second aspect of the triad *Sat-Chit-Ānanda* (Being-Consciousness-Bliss). Chit is consciousness as the capacity to know and to be aware. In Cosmic Harmony v3: phase 4, the integration hash mingling the previous state with the epochal weights — consciousness as integrating capacity.

**Cosmic Harmony v3**
The proof-of-work (PoW) algorithm of the ZION TerraNova network. A six-phase pipeline with Vedic names for the phases: Hiranyagarbha, Brahma, Yantra, Karma, Chit, Samadhi. Memory hardness (a 256 KiB scratchpad, ASIC Tier 1) and epochal adaptivity (NPU weights every 100 blocks, ASIC Tier 2) are architecturally built in as barriers to the monopolistic centralisation of mining.

---

## D

**Deeksha** (दीक्षा)
A transmissive act of consciousness — physical, energetic or intentional — from giver to receiver. Literally: "consecration, leading into". In the Ekam line there are five main forms: Sparsha (transmission by touch), Smarana (transmission through an object), Oneness Meditation / Ekam Meditation (group transmission without physical contact), Jnana Deeksha (verbal transmission of insight), Paduka Deeksha (transmission through the guru's sandals). Formerly called "Oneness Blessing" or *diksha*.

**Dharma** (धर्म)
One of the richest terms of Sanskrit, ambiguous: *cosmic order, natural justice, duty, path, law*. In the Yuga Cycle (the cycle of ages) dharma decreases from Satyayuga (full dharma) to Kaliyuga (dharma at one quarter). On the personal level: dharma as natural vocation and a manner of acting in accordance with cosmic order.

**DMN (Default Mode Network)**
A network of brain activity active in the resting state — especially the medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate and precuneus. It is connected with self-referential thinking, mind-wandering, planning and autobiographical memory. Research from 2004–2008 (EEG/fMRI on participants of Oneness University and other contemplative programmes) showed a reduction of DMN activity during deep meditation and Deeksha transmissions.

## E

**Ekam** (एकम्)
"One" or "Oneness" in Sanskrit. The name of the temple and of the line: the Ekam temple (opened 2018, Varadaiahpalem, Andhra Pradesh) and the organisation *Ekam* as the successor entity to Oneness University. The name carries a paradox: "one" as a designation for an experience that transcends any single name.

**Ekam World Peace Festival**
An annual global meditation event organised by Ekam. It combines physical presence (10,000–50,000+ meditators in the temple) with a global online stream and local satellite groups. Working hypothesis: synchronised meditation in a sufficient measure contributes to a change in the collective field of consciousness.

---

## F

**Fana** (فناء)
"Extinction" in Sufism — the mystical death of the ego self in the presence of God. Analogous notions: the Buddhist *śūnyatā* (emptiness), the Advaita Vedānta *samādhi*, the Hindu *nirvikalpa samādhi*. In the context of Ekam: the phase of breakthrough of the separateness of consciousness beyond the boundary of the individual ego.

---

## G

**Golden Age**
See *Satyayuga*. In the Ekam line: not a historical epoch, but an emergent state of collective consciousness — the average of awakened individual consciousnesses crossing a critical mass.

**Golden Orb**
The golden sphere in the Sanctum of the Ekam temple. A symbol of **Hiranyagarbha** — the golden germ. At the same time a physical meditative object (yantra) facilitating the transition from the analytic-obsessive mind to open presence. In ZION TerraNova: a metaphor for the seed of the computation at the beginning of each block.

---

## H

**Hiranyagarbha** (हिरण्यगर्भ)
"The golden germ" or "the golden womb" in Sanskrit. A key term of the Rigveda (X.121): the cosmological image of the golden egg from which the universe is born. Brahma as creator proceeds from Hiranyagarbha. In Advaita Vedānta: an aspect of Brahman as causal consciousness, the basis of the manifested world. In Cosmic Harmony v3: phase 0 of the algorithm — the Blake2b seed from the block header.

---

## J

**Jeevashram** (Jīvāśram)
"The dwelling of the spirit" or "the ashram of the living being" (jīva = individual soul; āśram = refuge, place of practice). The school and ashram founded by Amma and Bhagavan in 1984 in Varadaiahpalem, Chittoor district, Andhra Pradesh. The germ of the whole Ekam line, philosophically and physically.

**Jnana Deeksha** (Jñāna Dīkṣā)
"The transmission of knowledge/insight": a form of Deeksha mediated verbally — through a specific statement, story or question that interrupts automatic thought patterns and opens space for direct experience. Analogous to the Zen Buddhist *kōan*.

---

## K

**Kaliyuga** (कलियुग)
"The age of strife/conflict": the lowest of the four ages in the Hindu cosmological periodisation. It lasts 432,000 years (or 4,320,000 in the "full" calculation). It is characterised by a decline of dharma to one quarter, by the rule of deception, egoism and conflict. Bhagavan interprets Kaliyuga not as a fatality, but as an average of consciousness — one that can be changed.

**Karma** (कर्म)
"Deed" or "the consequence of a deed". In the dharma systems the general law of cause and effect on the level of consciousness: every action, thought or intention leaves an imprint (samskāra) that manifests in future experience. In the Ekam line: karma is a psychological pattern — a charge created by past actions that determines present reactions. In Cosmic Harmony v3: phase 3, the application of epochal weights — the present state of the computation is modified by historical context.

**Krishnaji** (Sri Krishnaji)
Born approximately 1986; the son of Amma and Bhagavan. Together with his wife Preethaji he leads the *O&O Academy* (One and Only) and continues the Ekam line in the 21st century. Author of *The Way of the Awakened* (2019) and *Four Sacred Secrets* (co-author with Preethaji, 2019). Focus: meditative philosophy in a modern context.

**Kripa** (कृपा)
"Grace": in the Ekam line the external condition of a breakthrough of consciousness — energy coming through the giver of Deeksha or through a strong group field. Complementary to *yogyatā* (the readiness of the receiver). The breakthrough occurs at the intersection of both: kripa + yogyatā.

---

## M

**Mahāsabhā**
An assembly; in the Ekam line a designation for larger group sittings with direct Deeksha from Amma or Bhagavan.

**Manasika Mouna** (मानसिक मौन)
"The silence of the mind": inner silence transcending physical silence (*vāk mouna*) — the halting of chronic inner commentary and evaluation. A central practice recommended by the Ekam line.

**Mandala** (मण्डल)
"Circle" or "sacred circle": a geometric composition used in the Indian and Tibetan tradition both as a meditative object and as a cosmological map. The Sri Chakra is the most complex mandala of the Vedic tradition.

**Mouna** (मौन)
See *Manasika Mouna* and *Vāk Mouna*.

## N

**NPU (Neural Processing Unit)**
A specialised hardware chip for matrix operations (weight tables, float16 matrix multiplication). In ASIC Tier 2 of Cosmic Harmony v3: every mining hardware must have a co-processor capable of applying the current epochal NPU weights (rotation every 100 blocks). This makes mining adaptive — a hard-programmed ASIC without an NPU cannot follow the changes of the epoch.

## O

**O&O Academy** (One and Only Academy)
The successor organisation to Oneness University, led by Krishnaji and Preethaji. Focused on meditation processes, retreats, online programmes and books adapted to the global audience of the 21st century without the need to travel to India.

**Oneness University**
An educational and spiritual institution established by Amma and Bhagavan in 2003 in Varadaiahpalem, Andhra Pradesh (near Satyaloka). The venue of 21-day transformation processes, where research studies took place (EEG, fMRI 2004–2008). In 2018 renamed and evolved into the form of the Ekam temple and the O&O Academy.

---

## P

**Paduka Deeksha** (Pādukā Dīkṣā)
*Pādukā* = the guru's sandals. Deeksha mediated through the sandals of Amma or Bhagavan — physical objects charged with the guru's presence. The tradition of the guru's sandals is old in the Indian context (Rama carries Bharata's sandals as a symbol of his brother-king's presence). In the Ekam line: Paduka Deeksha mediates the transmission for those who have no direct access to the guru.

**Parental Blessing Process**
A specific Oneness University process focused on releasing charges encoded in the primary relationship with parents. Bhagavan claims: ~80 % of the psychological suffering of an adult has its root in the childhood relationship with parents — not necessarily in traumatic abuse, but in the subtle dynamic of conditional love, rejection or abandonment. The process includes contemplation, group sharing and directive Deeksha transmission.

**Preethaji** (Sri Preethaji)
Born approximately 1981; the daughter-in-law of Amma and Bhagavan (the wife of Krishnaji). With Krishnaji she leads the O&O Academy. Author of *Four Sacred Secrets* (co-author with Krishnaji, 2019). Focused on spirituality in the context of partnership, family and psychology.

**PoW (Proof-of-Work)**
A consensus mechanism in a blockchain: to add a new block a miner must prove that they have performed a defined computational work. The function prevents Sybil attacks (forging the identity of nodes) and calibrates the pace of block creation through *difficulty adjustment*. Cosmic Harmony v3 is a memory-hard, epochally adaptive PoW designed for ZION TerraNova.

## R

**Rigveda** (ṚgVeda)
The oldest of the four Vedas; dated approximately 1700–1100 BCE (the oral tradition is even older). 1,028 hymns in ten Mandalas. The foundational text and the Hiranyagarbha hymn (X.121), the Purusha hymn (X.90), the hymn Nāsadīya-sūkta (X.129, "then there was neither being nor non-being"). The basis of Vedic theology and cosmology.

---

## S

**Samadhi** (समाधि)
"Unification" or "perfect absorption". The highest state of meditation in Patañjali's Yoga Sutras (c. 400 CE): the vanishing of the boundary between the meditator, the act of meditation and the object. In Advaita: undifferentiated consciousness without subject-object duality. In Cosmic Harmony v3: phase 5, the final BLAKE2b → comparison with the difficulty target. A valid block = digital Samadhi.

**Śaktipāt** (शक्तिपात)
"The descent of Shakti": the transmission of energetic grace by a guru to a disciple — physical (touch), visual (look), verbal (mantra) or intentional. A direct analogy to Deeksha in the Ekam line, although Ekam does not use Śaktipāt as a term.

**Satyayuga** (सत्ययुग)
"The age of truth/the golden age": the first and most exalted of the four ages of Vedic cosmology. Dharma (justice, truth, order) is in full measure. People live in harmony, long-lived, without illness and wars. It lasts 1,728,000 years. In the Ekam line: Satyayuga not as a distant past, but as an emergent future state of collective consciousness.

**Satyaloka**
The second physical space of the Ekam line, developed between Jeevashram (1984) and Oneness University (2003). It served as a centre for the early transformation processes. The name: satya = truth; loka = world/sphere. "The sphere of truth".

**Scratchpad (256 KiB)**
The working memory space allocated for each block in Cosmic Harmony v3 (the Brahma phase). It must be physically present in the RAM of the mining device — it cannot be precomputed or shared. The ASIC Tier 1 barrier.

**Smarana Deeksha** (Smaraṇa Dīkṣā)
*Smaraṇa* = "recollection, remembering". Deeksha transmitted through an object that was in the guru's presence or in a strong sacred field. An analogy with a relic in Christianity or *yadgar* in Islam — a physical object as a medium of presence.

**Sparsha Deeksha** (Sparśa Dīkṣā)
*Sparśa* = "touch". The most frequent form of Deeksha: transmission by placing the hands on the crown of the receiver's head for 1–2 minutes. A physically mediated transmission that was described in EEG/fMRI research. Sparsha Deeksha is typically given by Amma, Bhagavan or trained "Oneness Blessing Givers" (Deeksha givers).

**Sri Chakra** (श्रीचक्र)
See *Sri Yantra*. The most complex geometric mandala of the Vedic tradition: nine interpenetrating triangles (four Shakti-downward + five Shiva-upward) around a central bindu, forming 43 smaller triangles, bounded by two rings of lotus petals and an outer square. The basic geometric principle of the Ekam temple.

**Sri Yantra**
See *Sri Chakra*.

## T

**Turīya** (तुरीय)
"The fourth": the fourth state of consciousness beside waking (*jāgrat*), dreaming (*svapna*) and deep sleep (*suṣupti*). In the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad: the basis of the other states — pure consciousness, not a state in the sense of content, but the condition of the possibility of any experience.

---

## V

**Vastu Shastra** (वास्तु शास्त्र)
The Vedic architectural science; it codifies the relation of space, orientation, proportions and materials to living systems and energetic fields. Its basic principles are applied in the design of the Ekam temple.

**Vāk Mouna** (वाक् मौन)
"The silence of the voice": physical abstention from words. Different from *Manasika Mouna* (the silence of the mind). Both types of mouna are practices of the Ekam line and of other Indian traditions.

## Y

**Yantra** (यन्त्र)
"Tool, mechanism, mandala". In the tantric context: the geometric form of divine energy — a physical yantra as a meditative object. In Cosmic Harmony v3: phase 2, the traversal of 256 × 4 pseudorandom addresses through the scratchpad — the "mandala" of the access pattern.

**Yogyatā** (योग्यता)
"Readiness, capability": the inner condition of a breakthrough of consciousness — as distinct from the outer *kripa* (grace). Yogyatā includes the sincerity of seeking, the willingness to see charges, work on relationships, regular practice. The breakthrough occurs at the intersection of yogyatā + kripa.
