# ZION — The Salt of This Earth
## Episode 11: The Gate of the First Player — The Choice of Path in Oasis

*You do not enter as a player who must win. You enter as a person who has decided to build something.*

> *"You do not choose a name to elevate yourself. You choose the first way you will be of use in the world."*

---

# I. The Gate That Requires No Faith

The path from the Valley of the First Step led between two cedars. No guardian with a sword waited there, no priest with a form, no merchant offering a "favorable entry."

There stood only a gate of dark wood. At its center was a circle divided into seven paths. Beneath it, a single sentence:

> **"Before you enter, verify where you are entering. Then choose what you wish to serve."**

A traveler who had come from the river stopped. In one hand she held a phone, in the other a slip of paper with the address of her new ZION wallet.

"Do I have to choose an avatar?" she asked.

Rádha stood beside the gate. She was not there as a sovereign, and no one knelt before her. She simply pointed to the surface of a shallow pool in which the newcomer's face was reflected.

"First you choose your own form," she said. "A name, a face, a color, a garment. That is only a dress, so you can be recognized in the garden. Then you choose a **path**. Not a cage. A path can be developed further, learned from others, and reconsidered over time."

"And Rama? Hanuman? You? Neo? Are they avatars too?"

"They are characters and guides of the story," Rádha replied. "Some spring from spiritual traditions, others from literature or pop culture. You are not to wear them as a foreign mask or pretend to be them. You are to meet them, pass through their questions, and take from them only what holds up in your own life: truthfulness, care, courage, curiosity, or service."

The gate opened.

---

# II. First the Person, Then the Character

Beyond the gate there was no screen full of rare armor. There was a quiet circle with simple tools: a mirror, a canvas, a set of colors, and a blank notebook.

In the notebook it read:

1. **Connect your address.** That is the technical signature of your identity in Oasis — not proof that you are better than others.
2. **Choose a display name.** It can be plain. It need not be heroic.
3. **Create your appearance.** Face, garment, and small marks serve recognition, not the creation of hierarchy among people.
4. **Choose your first path.** It will not determine your worth. It will only determine the first task the world offers you.

Morpheus, leaning against the cedar wall, added: "Entry is not a reward. It is the beginning of responsibility. Every new character starts at the first level — no throne, no guild, no territory, and no privilege."

The traveler wrote the name **Lumen** in the notebook.

Then seven paths lit up before her.

---

# III. Seven Paths — Not Seven Castes

The archival design of Oasis describes seven game paths. In the First World, they are not religious or cultural identities of obligation; they are game archetypes that give the first step a different tone. Every person should have the option to enter also as an ordinary **Pilgrim**, without taking on a name, tradition, or symbol that is not their own.

## 1. The Path of Knowledge — Cosmic Engineer

On the first path waited **Sarasvatí**, guardian of the library, beside her **Vishvakarma**, builder of maps, bridges, and precise instruments.

"Before you begin to fix the world," said Sarasvatí, "learn to read its manuals."

This path belongs to those who wish to understand code, nodes, security, mathematics, and architecture. The first task is not to defeat a monster, but to read the explanation of a transaction, to find the difference between a key and an address, and to pass a basic quiz without guessing.

- **First guides:** Sarasvatí, Vishvakarma, later Thoth the Scribe.
- **First task:** "What does a signature actually confirm?"
- **Gift of the path:** curiosity, auditability, the ability to say "I don't know — I'll verify it."

## 2. The Path of Service — Caretaker

On the second path stood **Hanuman**. He was not lifting a mountain or brandishing a weapon. He was carrying a crate of water to people who had just arrived.

"Strength that does not help the weaker is just noise," he said.

This path belongs to people who wish to help newcomers, support community projects, understand the humanitarian tithe, and turn good will into a verifiable deed.

- **First guides:** Hanuman, Shanti, Lady Nada.
- **First task:** choose one area of aid — water, food, shelter, environment, health, education, or crisis relief — and find out what can be verified about it.
- **Gift of the path:** service without the need for reward, and care without pressure.

## 3. The Path of Courage — Protector

On the third path lay a bridge across a deep ravine. At the middle of the bridge stood **Rama**, and not far from him kept watch Issobela, guardian of the city.

"Courage is not the desire to dominate," said Rama. "It is the ability to bear the consequences of your own decision and to protect the space in which others may also live."

This path belongs to node operators, community moderators, guild administrators, and those who wish to protect the system from fraud, chaos, or abuse.

- **First guides:** Rama, Issobela Guardian, later Arjuna.
- **First task:** recognize a security warning, verify the source of software, and report suspicious behavior without public humiliation.
- **Gift of the path:** responsibility, safety, and protection without control over others.

## 4. The Path of the Heart — Weaver of Relationships

On the fourth path Rádha played the flute, and beside her a rose garden grew. It was not a zone for romantic promises. It was a school of attention.

"A network without relationships is cabling," said Rádha. "And a relationship without freedom is a cage."

This path belongs to people who wish to create a safe social space, translate, welcome new members, lead dialogue, create art, or help a group overcome conflict.

- **First guides:** Rádha, Sítá, Mother Yashoda, later Tara.
- **First task:** write or create something that eases a newcomer's entry, without promising them yield, exclusivity, or salvation.
- **Gift of the path:** joy, empathy, and the ability to connect people without manipulation.

## 5. The Path of Stillness — Pilgrim

The fifth path did not lead to a temple. It led into an ordinary quiet grove. The Pilgrim Sádhu sat there and had no riddle for the newcomer.

"Not every gap must be filled with a message," he said after a while.

This path belongs to people who wish to give the game world rhythm, not merely speed: meditation, focus, regular participation, and care for one's own limits.

- **First guides:** Pilgrim Sádhu, later Avalokiteshvara and Vajrasattva.
- **First task:** complete a short guided meditation, or consciously refuse a quick reward if it would violate the community's rules.
- **Gift of the path:** patience, attention, and the ability not to confuse XP with human worth.

## 6. The Path of Transformation — Alchemist

On the sixth path burned a violet fire. It did not set the forest alight; it illuminated things that otherwise hid in shadow: errors, mistakes, broken ideas, and contradictions between words and deeds.

"A mistake is not an identity," said Saint Germain. "It is material you can work with, if you admit it."

This path belongs to creators, experimenters, tool developers, and people who can turn failure into a fix, a patch, or a new skill.

- **First guides:** Saint Germain, Hilarion, later Ptah Mason.
- **First task:** find a contradictory statement in the public documentation, describe it factually, and propose a verifiable correction.
- **Gift of the path:** humility before reality and the courage to repair rather than conceal.

## 7. The Path of Awakening — Signal Seeker

The seventh path was formed of points of light. Neo and Trinity sat at its beginning, but they did not say that anyone must be "the chosen one."

"Everyone wants the red pill," said Neo. "Few want to read the terms, verify the source, and bear responsibility for the decision that follows."

Trinity added: "To connect worlds is not the same as to own them."

This path belongs to people drawn to technology, interoperability, digital culture, and the question of how to remain free even within a complex system.

- **First guides:** Neo, Trinity, Morpheus.
- **First task:** choose one technical boundary — a wallet, a bridge, a signature, or a hash — and explain it to another newcomer without jargon.
- **Gift of the path:** critical thinking, digital literacy, and freedom joined to responsibility.

# IV. Lumen's First Four Steps

Lumen looked at the seven paths for a long time. In the end, she did not choose the strongest weapon or the rarest color. She chose the Path of Knowledge, because she wanted first to understand where she had placed her address.

Sarasvatí opened the first page of the Library for her. There was no sacred command on it. Only a question:

> **"How do you know that information about the network is true?"**

Lumen looked at four answers. One spoke of a promise. The second of price. The third of what an influential person had said. The fourth said: *"I will find the source, check it, and admit what does not follow from it."*

She chose the fourth.

No legend flashed on her bracelet, no NFT. Only **30 XP** appeared, and the name of the first completed task: *Crypto Knowledge — Beginner*.

"That's all?" she asked.

"That is enough," said Rádha. "The First World does not begin with a great treasure. It begins with a person who can take a small honest step."

Lumen then saw further options: ten minutes of silence, another quiz, first aid to someone at the gate, or simply returning tomorrow. None was mandatory. None said that without it she had no value.

In the distance, above the Temple of Consciousness, eight territories lit up: Mount Zion, Cedar Forest, Negev Desert, Sea of Galilee, Masada Forge, Crystal Mines of Solomon, Temple of Consciousness, and Babel Nexus.

They were possibilities, not possessions. First a person must learn to enter the world. Only much later might they wish to govern a piece of it.

---

# V. What Is Not Sold at the Gate

Rádha turned to Lumen one last time before she set out on her path.

"Remember four things:

1. **An avatar is not a soul.** It is a game interface and a narrative language.
2. **XP is not a measure of human worth.** It is a technical record of participation in a specific system.
3. **A reward is not a promise.** Neither Oasis nor ZION is meant to guarantee yield, price, or future status.
4. **The choice of a path is not an oath.** When you find you wish to serve differently, you can learn from another path."

And then she added: "A new civilization is not known by the number of its legendary characters. It is known by whether even the first person at the gate understands what is happening to them, where they can go, and why they may freely leave."

## Design and Ethical Boundaries

Oasis must work with traditions as context for story, not as a license to appropriate living faiths, real persons, or others' fictional works. Before public release, every avatar must pass a curatorial, community, legal, and IP review. In particular, characters drawn from modern copyrighted works are for now only internal design references, not confirmed public content.
