# ZION — The Salt of the Earth
## Episode 11: The Gate of the First Player — choosing a path in Oasis

*You do not enter as a player who has to win. You enter as a person who has decided to build something.*

> *"You are not choosing a name meant to elevate you. You are choosing the first way in which you will be useful in the world."*

---

# I. A gate that does not require faith

The path out of the Valley of the First Step led between two cedars. No guard with a sword waited there, no priest with a form, no salesman with an offer of a "favourable entry".

There was only a gate of dark wood. In its centre was a circle divided into seven paths. Beneath it was a simple sentence:

> **"Before you enter, verify where you are entering. Then choose what you want to serve."**

The pilgrim who had come from the river stopped. In one hand she held a phone, in the other a slip of paper with the address of her new ZION wallet.

"Do I have to choose an avatar?" she asked.

Radha stood beside the gate. She was not there as a ruler and no one knelt before her. She only pointed to the surface of a shallow pond in which the newcomer's face was reflected.

"First you choose your own form," she said. "Name, face, colour, garment. That is only clothing, so that you are recognizable in the garden. Then you choose a **path**. Not a cage. You can develop the path further, learn from others and, in time, reconsider it."

"And Rama? Hanuman? You? Neo? Are they avatars too?"

"They are characters and guides of the story," Radha answered. "Some come from spiritual traditions, others from literature or pop culture. You are not to put them on as someone else's mask, nor pretend to be them. You are to meet them, pass through their questions and take from them only what stands up in your own life: truthfulness, care, courage, curiosity or service."

The gate opened.

---

# II. First the person, then the character

Behind the gate was not a screen full of rare armour. There was a quiet circle with simple tools: a mirror, a canvas, a set of colours and an empty notebook.

In the notebook it said:

1. **Connect your address.** That is the technical signature of your identity in Oasis — not proof that you are better than others.
2. **Choose a display name.** It can be simple. It does not have to be heroic.
3. **Create your form.** Face, garment and small marks should help recognition, not create a hierarchy between people.
4. **Choose your first path.** It does not determine your value. It only determines the first task the world offers you.

Morpheus, leaning against a wall of cedar wood, added: "Entry is not a reward. It is the beginning of responsibility. Every new character starts at the first level — without a throne, without a guild, without a territory and without privilege."

The pilgrim wrote the name **Lumen** in the notebook.

Then seven paths lit up before her.

---

# III. Seven paths — not seven castes

The archival Oasis design describes seven game paths. In the First World they are not religious or cultural identities imposed as a duty; they are game archetypes that give the first step a different tone. Every person must also be able to enter as an ordinary **Pilgrim**, without taking on a name, tradition or symbol that is not their own.

## 1. The Path of Knowledge — the Cosmic Engineer

On the first path waited **Sarasvati**, guardian of the library, beside her **Vishvakarma**, builder of maps, bridges and precise tools.

"Before you start repairing the world," said Sarasvati, "learn to read its instructions."

This path belongs to those who want to understand code, nodes, security, mathematics and architecture. The first task is not to defeat a monster, but to read the explanation of a transaction, find the difference between a key and an address, and pass a basic quiz without guessing.

- **First guides:** Sarasvati, Vishvakarma, later Thoth the Scribe.
- **First task:** "What does a signature actually confirm?"
- **Gift of the path:** curiosity, auditability, the ability to say "I don't know, I'll verify it".

## 2. The Path of Service — the Caregiver

On the second path stood **Hanuman**. He was not lifting a mountain nor waving a weapon. He was carrying a crate of water to the people who had just arrived.

"Strength that does not help the weaker is only noise," he said.

This path belongs to people who want to help newcomers, support community projects, understand the humanitarian tithe and turn good will into a traceable deed.

- **First guides:** Hanuman, Shanti, Lady Nada.
- **First task:** choose one area of help — water, food, shelter, environment, health, education or crisis relief — and find out what can be verified about it.
- **Gift of the path:** service without the need for reward, and care without coercion.

## 3. The Path of Courage — the Guardian

On the third path lay a bridge over a deep ravine. In the middle of the bridge stood **Rama**, and not far from him watched Issobela, guardian of the city.

"Courage is not the desire to control," said Rama. "It is the ability to bear the consequences of one's own decision and to protect the space in which others can live too."

This path belongs to node operators, community moderators, guild stewards and people who want to protect the system from fraud, chaos or abuse.

- **First guides:** Rama, the Issobela Guardian, later Arjuna.
- **First task:** recognize a security warning, verify the source of software and report suspicious behaviour without public humiliation.
- **Gift of the path:** responsibility, security and protection without control over others.

## 4. The Path of the Heart — the Weaver of relationships

On the fourth path Radha played the flute and a rose garden grew beside her. It was not a zone for romantic promises. It was a school of attention.

"A network without relationships is cabling," said Radha. "And a relationship without freedom is a cage."

This path belongs to people who want to create a safe social space, translate, welcome new members, lead dialogue, make art or help a group overcome conflict.

- **First guides:** Radha, Sita, Mother Yashoda, later Tara.
- **First task:** write or create something that makes entry easier for a new person without promising them yield, exceptionality or salvation.
- **Gift of the path:** joy, empathy and the ability to connect people without manipulation.

## 5. The Path of Stillness — the Pilgrim

The fifth path did not lead to a temple. It led to an ordinary quiet grove. The Pilgrim Sadhu sat there and had no riddle for the newcomer.

"Not every gap has to be filled with a message," he said after a while.

This path belongs to people who want to give the game world rhythm rather than only speed: meditation, focus, regular participation and care for one's own limits.

- **First guides:** the Pilgrim Sadhu, later Avalokiteshvara and Vajrasattva.
- **First task:** complete a short guided meditation, or consciously refuse a quick reward if it would break the community's rules.
- **Gift of the path:** patience, attention and the ability not to mistake XP for human worth.

## 6. The Path of Transformation — the Alchemist

On the sixth path a violet fire burned. It did not set the forest alight; it illuminated things that otherwise hid in shadow: mistakes, errors, ideas that did not work, and contradictions between words and deeds.

"A mistake is not an identity," said Saint Germain. "It is material you can work with, if you admit it."

This path belongs to creators, experimenters, tool developers and people who can turn failure into a repair, a patch or a new skill.

- **First guides:** Saint Germain, Hilarion, later Ptah Mason.
- **First task:** find a contradictory claim in the public documentation, describe it factually and propose a verifiable fix.
- **Gift of the path:** humility before reality and the courage to repair instead of conceal.

## 7. The Path of Awakening — the Signal Seeker

The seventh path was made of points of light. Neo and Trinity sat at its beginning, but they did not say that anyone had to be "the chosen one".

"Everyone wants the red pill," said Neo. "Few want to read the terms, verify the source and bear responsibility for the decision after it."

Trinity added: "Connecting worlds is not the same as appropriating them."

This path belongs to people drawn to technology, interoperability, digital culture and the question of how to stay free even inside a system that is complex.

- **First guides:** Neo, Trinity, Morpheus.
- **First task:** choose one technical boundary — wallet, bridge, signature or hash — and explain it to another newcomer without jargon.
- **Gift of the path:** critical thinking, digital literacy and freedom joined with responsibility.

---

# IV. Lumen's first four steps

Lumen looked at the seven paths for a long time. In the end she did not choose the strongest weapon or the rarest colour. She chose the Path of Knowledge, because she wanted first to understand where she had placed her address.

Sarasvati opened the first page of the Library for her. There was no holy command on it. Only a question:

> **"How do you know that information about the network is true?"**

Lumen looked at four answers. One spoke of a promise. The second of price. The third of the fact that an influential person had said it. The fourth said: *"I find the source, check it, and admit what does not follow from it."*

She chose the fourth.

No legend or NFT glittered on her bracelet. Only **30 XP** was added, and the name of the first completed task: *Crypto Knowledge — Beginner*.

"That's all?" she asked.

"That is enough," said Radha. "The First World does not begin with a great treasure. It begins with a person who can take a small honest step."

Lumen then saw further possibilities: ten minutes of silence, another quiz, first help to someone at the gate, or simply to return tomorrow. None was obligatory. None said that without it she had no value.

In the distance, above the Temple of Consciousness, eight territories lit up: Mount Zion, Cedar Forest, Negev Desert, Sea of Galilee, Masada Forge, Crystal Mines of Solomon, Temple of Consciousness and Babel Nexus.

They were possibilities, not possessions. First a person must learn to enter the world. Only much later may they want to steward a piece of it.

---

# V. What is not for sale at the entrance

Radha turned to Lumen one last time before she set out on her path.

"Remember four things:

1. **An avatar is not a soul.** It is a game interface and a story language.
2. **XP is not a measure of human worth.** It is a technical record of participation in a particular system.
3. **A reward is not a promise.** Neither Oasis nor ZION guarantees yield, price or future status.
4. **Choosing a path is not an oath.** When you find you want to serve differently, you can learn from another path."

And then she added: "A new civilization is not recognized by the number of its legendary characters. It is recognized by whether even the first person at the gate understands what is happening to them, where they can go and why they are free to leave."

## Design and ethical boundary

Oasis should work with traditions as context for a story, not as a licence to appropriate living faiths, real persons or others' fictional works. Before public release every avatar must pass curatorial, community, legal and IP review. In particular, characters taken from modern copyrighted works are for now only internal design references, not confirmed public content.
