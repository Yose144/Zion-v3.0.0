# Episode 6 — The Compass and the Invitation to Oasis

*The compass will not tell you exactly where you will arrive. It only tells you that the direction you are walking at least does not lead in a circle.*

> *"Whoever asks 'how do I sign up' is asking for a map. Whoever asks 'why should this matter to me' is asking for a compass."*

---

## The Story

The morning was different from all the ones before. The ark stood on the bank of the dried river with the open ribs of its hull — not finished yet, but already large enough to show how many people it could hold. The fountain channel that Hanuman had spent years clearing of stones was clean. And for the first time since Tasteless had forgotten taste, more people came to the fire than the old circle could hold.

The carpenter, the pilgrim with the bowl, the shepherd with the flute, Rama, Sita, Hanuman and the man who was building the boat sat in a circle together with the whole town — the water carrier, the soldier, the woman, the farmer, the blacksmith, the mother with her child, and dozens of others who had come just to see what was being built.

"We have salt," said the carpenter. "We have dissolution," said the pilgrim with the bowl. "We have the taste of water," said the shepherd. "We have a road without a map and a bridge," said Rama, Sita and Hanuman together. "We have a boat," said the man who was building it.

"What are we to do with all of it now?" asked the woman who had stopped counting what was left, and who over those weeks had become the voice the whole of Tasteless listened to.

The quiet knot-maker, who had sat by the fire all that time and only listened, finally stood up. He took the stone he had carried since the first night and set it in the middle of the circle — not at the bottom of a pot as before, but where everyone could see it.

"I have nothing extra to give you," he said. "You received salt from the carpenter. Dissolution from the pilgrim. The taste of water from the shepherd. A road and a bridge from Rama, Sita and Hanuman. A boat from the builder. What I have is only one thing: a **compass**."

He turned the stone over, and on its underside four marks were carved, one on each side.

"North is the question *why*," he said, pointing to the first mark. "East is the question of what is broken in what we are leaving. South is the question of who I am when I set out on the road. West is the question of where that road is actually heading. None of you needs to know the whole map. But every one of you can know which way north is."

The farmer, who had not spoken the whole time, suddenly spoke up: "And what if I want more than just to know the direction? What if I want to see where the road actually leads — not as a question, but as a place I can enter?"

The knot-maker smiled — for the first time since that first night under the fig tree. "Then you do not have to wait for the end of the ark," he said. "Beyond this fire, a little further on, we are building one more thing. Not a boat for water. A boat for play, for story, for a world where what you have done — the water you carried, the stone you cleared, the salt you gave a neighbour — counts and grows with you. We call it **Oasis**."

## An invitation to Oasis

The compass shows the direction. But the compass alone will not carry you anywhere — you have to do that, with your own step. **Oasis** is the place that step leads to: a game and community layer above the ZION network, where work, care and participation are reflected in your own story.

It is not a replacement for mining or for consensus — mining remains pure Proof-of-Work, regardless of what happens in Oasis. It is a **layer on top**, where a different rule applies: **what you do counts.**

### Nine levels of consciousness — a path like Rama's, not a leap in a forest without a map

In Oasis there is no single "level up" button. There are nine levels — from **Physical** (first contact with the network) to **On The Star** (full integration) — inspired by the kabbalistic Tree of Life, where each degree corresponds to a different quality of participation, not merely a different number on an account.

- **Physical** — the first step, like the water carrier who first drinks what he carries to others.
- **Tiferet (Intuitional)** — the balance between giving and receiving, like the woman who gave salt to her neighbour without expectation.
- **Da'at (Unity)** — the knot that bridges the network, like Hanuman, who built a bridge without anyone asking him to.
- **Keter (On The Star)** — full integration, the stone in the foundation that carries the whole structure.

You earn them through real activity — a valid share in mining, a found block, voting in the DAO, fixing a bug, running a node, documentation, long-term loyalty to the network (activity has its weight, but also its decay during long inactivity — just like salt, which loses meaning without use).

### Avatars and quests — the story you write yourself

In Oasis you are not an anonymous number. You have an avatar — one of dozens of characters, each with their own quest, their own path, their own reason for being here. Not because you have to play a role. Because the story you have just finished — salt, dissolution, the taste of water, a road without a map, a bridge, the ark — does not end at the fire. It continues where you decide to enter.

### The Golden Egg — a treasure scattered across the whole world of ZION

And for those who ask what is at the end of the road, there is one concrete, tangible goal: the **Golden Egg** — a massive treasure hunt scattered across the entire ZION ecosystem. 108 cryptic clues hidden in the blockchain, in smart contracts, in the TerraNova books, and in physical places. Whoever solves them all receives a share of the genesis reserve set aside precisely for this purpose.

This is not a promise of profit without work. It is **Hanuman's bridge turned into a game**: a road everyone must walk themselves, step by step, riddle by riddle — but with a goal visible and verifiable from the first moment.

> **A note on honesty:** the Golden Egg is a planned feature (launch planned for 2027; the avatar system is in active development today), not a treasure already handed out. The compass shows the direction — no one knows exactly the date the water arrives, but the direction it is raining from can already be seen.
