# I. Morning mist over the Valley of the First Step

After the night when Radha brought joy to the fire and the tone of a bamboo flute, the sun rose above the rocks.

When the mist dissolved, the land beneath the overhang no longer looked like an ordinary forest. Before the pilgrims opened an enormous, radiant space — **the First World of Oasis**, known in ancient texts as the **Garden of Hiranyagarbha (The Realm of the Golden Womb)**.

At the centre of the whole land rose the **Ancient Tree of Life**, its roots drinking water from the L1 blockchain and its branches forming nine heavenly storeys (9 levels of consciousness from Malkuth to Keter). Around the Tree lay **8 Genesis Territories**, each holding one of the key elements of the new civilization.

"This is not a virtual escape," said Morpheus, pointing toward the valley. "This is a **training ground for a new reality**. Here we learn to manage land, found guilds, distribute resources and search for the 108 clues of the Golden Egg without destroying the world around us."

# III. The geography of the First World of Oasis — 8 Genesis Territories

The First World of Oasis is not an endless wasteland. It is structured precisely into **8 Genesis Territories**, each with its own meaning, its own rule and its own reward pool:

```
                         [ 09. KETER — Stellar Horizon ]
                                       │
                         [ 05–08. Fifth to Eighth Storey ]
                                       │
               ╭───────────────────────┴───────────────────────╮
               │       01. MALKUTH — Valley of the First Step   │
               ╰───────────────────────┬───────────────────────╯
                                       │
     ╭─────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────╮
     │                                 │                                 │
[ 02. Code & Mining Zone ]   [ 03. Garden of Service (Humanitarian) ]  [ 04. Guild City ]
 (Neo / Trinity / Miners)          (Hanuman / Sita)                     (Morpheus / Guilds)
```

1. **Valley of the First Step (Malkuth Gateway):** the entry zone for all newcomers (Level 1 — Dormant / Physical). The first onboarding, wallet verification and acquisition of the first Avatar happen here.
2. **Crystal Mines (Mining & Code Realm):** the territory reserved for miners (Stream 1, 2, 3) and developers. Mining uptime and code contributions generate bonus XP here.
3. **Garden of Service (Humanitarian Sanctuary):** the place where the 5% Humanitarian Tithe from every block visibly gathers. Players with the Giver Avatar decide here on concrete grants and help.
4. **Guild Citadel (Guild Keep):** the zone where the first Guilds arise (max 100 members per guild). Guilds compete here for the stewardship of territories through fair auction and contributions to the network (10,000 ZION claim cost).
5. **Temple of Hiranyagarbha (The Golden Womb Temple):** the central temple at the roots of the Tree of Life, where the first of the **108 clues of the Golden Egg** lies.

# IV. The mechanics of growth: from XP to the Golden Egg

How does life work in the First World of Oasis?

1. **Earn XP for real actions:**
   - Starting a node or miner → **+500 XP / day**
   - Reporting a bug or helping with documentation → **+1,000 XP**
   - Joining a Guild and taking part in a community task → **+2,000 XP**
   - Contributing to a humanitarian project → **+1,500 XP**
2. **Advance through the 9 Levels of Consciousness:**
   - From *Level 1 (Physical)* through *Level 5 (Cosmic)* to *Level 9 (OnTheStar)*.
   - At a higher level your Avatar gains a new appearance, access to deeper quests and the right to vote in DAO governance.
3. **Search for the 108 clues of the Golden Egg:**
   - 4.95 billion ZION from the genesis reserve is locked in 5 reward pools (Player Pool, Guild Pool, Territory Pool, Golden Egg Pool, Winners Pool). *(Slots 4 and 5 — 3.3 billion ZION — repurposed to L5 Free World Projects.)*
   - The clues are hidden in L1 chain blocks, in smart contracts on Base/EVM, in the TerraNova books and in real geographic locations.
