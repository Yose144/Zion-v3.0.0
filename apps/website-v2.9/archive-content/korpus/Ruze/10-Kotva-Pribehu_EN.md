# THE ROSE STORY — X. The anchor of the story
## What in this book is story, what is a repository fact — and everything the book is not

> *"A story may be beautiful, the evidence must be precise."*

---

## 1. The whole plot is MYTH

The Rose Story is a literary fable. **The whole plot** — Sarah's descent from the cave to the gate, the handing over of the empty alabaster vessel to Lumi, the seed in its crack, the planting by the river, the speaking Rose, the visitor in gloves, the gardener of mirrors, the merchant with a scent, the garden of many roses, the drought, Lumi's departure and her return, the epilogue planting of the second seed in the Genesis Garden by the Atlantic — **all characters, dialogues and scenes are MYTH** according to [`../MiseAmenti/01-Kanon-a-Ustava.md`](../MiseAmenti/01-Kanon-a-Ustava.md). None of it happened; the book presents none of it as an event, a testimony or a revelation.

## 2. Documented continuity — the existence of texts, not historical truth

The only thing the book may claim as a repository fact is that these texts and lines exist in the repository:

- [`../Miriam/13-Kotva-Pravdy-a-Hranice.md`](../Miriam/13-Kotva-Pravdy-a-Hranice.md) ends with old Miriam handing Sarah an empty alabaster vessel to carry onward.
- [`../Miriam/12-Reka-a-Dite-s-Lucernou.md`](../Miriam/12-Reka-a-Dite-s-Lucernou.md) lets Miriam and the child with the lantern meet on the bank of a river — and labels that scene itself as "a dream this book weaves on its own" (MYTH).
- [`../Lumi/12-Brana-Zustava-Otevrena.md`](../Lumi/12-Brana-Zustava-Otevrena.md) — Lumi carries the lantern, the gate stays open, the last page of her book is blank.
- [`../SulZeme/11-Brana-Prvniho-Hrace-a-Volba-Cesty.md`](../SulZeme/11-Brana-Prvniho-Hrace-a-Volba-Cesty.md) — on the path of the Heart stands a rose garden and Radha says literally: **"a relationship without freedom is a cage"**.
- In the [`` `internal repository` ``](../../../interní%20repozitář) there exists an avatar `id: 18` with the name **Meriam Rose**, `location: Jerusalem Garden`, `teaching: "True devotion sees beyond form"`.
- [`../BodhiGaia/03-Zahrada-Genesis.md`](../BodhiGaia/03-Zahrada-Genesis.md) describes the Genesis Garden (Sabacheira, Tomar) and the figure of Ana; [`../BodhiGaia/12-Ruze-v-Zahrade-Genesis.md`](../BodhiGaia/12-Ruze-v-Zahrade-Genesis.md) is the follow-up chapter of the Book of the Earth; in `apps/OasisWeb/src/domain/config/worlds.ts` there is a world record `GENESIS_GARDEN` with explicitly labelled literary Rose lore.

The existence of these lines is a **repository fact** — verifiable by simply opening the files. It is not historical or spiritual truth. "The text exists and says X" is LIVE; "X happened or is valid" remains MYTH.

## 3. Relation to the older document `18_MERIAM_ROSE.md`

In the repository there is an older esoteric text [`../../docs2.9/ZION_OASIS/SACRED_TRINITY/18_MERIAM_ROSE.md`](../../../docs/docs2.9/ZION_OASIS/SACRED_TRINITY/18_MERIAM_ROSE.md). **This book does not confirm its content.** Its claims — "twin flame", a secret union or marriage, the grail as a womb, secret codes and teachings, a future school, council, funds or allocations — are read according to their nature as **MYTH / UNSUBSTANTIATED / HORIZON / INVALID CLAIM**, not as facts; the specific label depends on the specific claim. The new story shares no characters, scenes or claims with it; the Rose in this book is not Miriam, Meriam nor avatar no. 18 — it is a literary flower grown from the seed of a story. Where the claims overlap with what [`../Miriam/13-Kotva-Pravdy-a-Hranice.md`](../Miriam/13-Kotva-Pravdy-a-Hranice.md) addresses, this book takes over its historical boundaries; part of the old document is not addressed there and remains unverified.

## 4. Literary relation to *The Little Prince*

*The Little Prince* (Antoine de Saint-Exupéry) is for this book an acknowledged distant inspiration **in form only** — a short philosophical parable, the simple questions of a child and the child's relationship to a rose. It is not a source nor a continuation: the book takes over no character, scene or sentence from it. It poses its own question, whether a relationship can be unique without being exclusive, and answers with mutuality, consent and freedom.

## 5. What this book is not

- **It is not a historical account.** Nothing in it happened; its characters are not real people nor foretold persons.
- **It is not therapeutic advice.** The images of boundaries, care and departure are literary; they are not a guide to managing real relationships.
- **It is not a religious initiation.** It initiates no one, preaches nothing and claims no spiritual authority; it adds no new religious figure.
- **It is not a relational command.** It describes one possible ethic of care; it prescribes to no one how to live.
- **It is not a financial or technical document.** It makes no claim on the network, funds, tokens, governance or operations. Operational truth remains in [`StatusV3.md`](../../3.2/StatusV3.md), the [`` `internal repository` ``](../../../interní%20repozitář) and [`../MiseAmenti/07-Registr-Dukazu.md`](../MiseAmenti/07-Registr-Dukazu.md).
