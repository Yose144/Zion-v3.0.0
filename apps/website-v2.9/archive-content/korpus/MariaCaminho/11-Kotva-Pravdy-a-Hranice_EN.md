## The Story

In the last tent — there where the way is written down — the Pilgrim laid the credencial on the table beside a table above which was written: *"This is where we come when the story collides with a fact."*

Only-that did not raise his head. He only pointed at the rows.

*"Three Marys,"* he said. *"Three ships. Eleven stamps. Each of them has its row — and each row has the truth it carries, not the one we invent."*

*"And what if I get it wrong?"* asked the Pilgrim.

*"Then we repair the row,"* said Only-that. *"Not you. We repair you only if you lie — and you have never lied yet. You were only mistaken — and a mistake is repaired, a lie is written down."*

The Pilgrim looked at the credencial — at the three Marys who had visited him, at the three ships that carried them, at the eleven stamps that had been pressed — and he knew: **everything he holds, he holds because it holds to the truth.**

## What it means

**The Book of the Way needs its own register, because a way is itself a claim — and a claim that is not submitted is not a way but advertising.** This chapter does not rewrite [`MiseAmenti/07-Registr-Dukazu.md`](../MiseAmenti/07-Registr-Dukazu.md) nor the registers of the layers ([`BodhiGaia/09`](../BodhiGaia/09-Kotva-Pravdy-a-Hranice.md) for L5, [`Lumi/13`](../Lumi/13-Kotva-Pravdy-a-Hranice.md)). It records three things that are new only in this book: **every revelation as a layer, every hull as a state and every stamp as a rule.** In a conflict, the canonical register, `StatusV3.md` and the live repository prevail.

### §4 What this book is not

1. **It is not a biography.** The Pilgrim is an archetype — a character the reader puts on and takes off. The sovereign model [`genesis/09.5`](../../docs2.9/genesis/09.5-CHAPTER-9-Three-Marian-Apparitions.md) tells a private story; this book retells it as "the Pilgrim" and removes the private as its first discipline.
2. **It is not theology.** The three Marys are archetypes and documented folk traditions; the book asserts no revelation as fact, preaches nothing, initiates no one and does not build a Marian cult.
3. **It is not a fleet plan passed off as vessels.** Hulls I and II are BUILDING; hull III is HORIZON — and the book holds the precision that "the ship that is not yet" is a ship that waits.
4. **It is not a financial promise.** No chapter states a price, a return or "why to start early". The 300M reserve is a registry fact, not a claim.
5. **It is not a replacement for the registers.** Where it differs from `StatusV3.md`, `MiseAmenti/07`, `BodhiGaia/09` or `Lumi/13`, they prevail.
6. **It is not an identification of "the Marys of the way = the Marys of Provence".** Miriam (Magdalene) is the axis of time; the Marys of the way are the axis of space — and the book holds them apart, the way two rivers are held apart that never mingle but both flow to the sea.
