# ISSOBELLA — Chapter 9: The pilgrim's way
## Seven gates · L6 onboarding — how to enter the story of the sky without anyone selling you the horizon

> *"The gate to the sky does not open with a handle. It opens with a question you pose well."*

---

## The Story

A pilgrim who wanted to reach the stars came to seven gates. On each hung a sign — and on each stood a different truth.

**The first gate — Observer.** *"Look,"* the sign said. *"Free. Without promises."* Beyond it a web page could be seen, the page `/l6-issobella`, the mirror of the station and of the fund. Whoever passed only this far left with one treasure: they knew that a layer exists which looks upward.

**The second gate — Reader.** *"Read what we read."* Beyond it was the library: the architecture of the station, the quantum motor, artificial gravity, human factors, the history of stations. The reader did not have to believe — only to read carefully.

**The third gate — Researcher.** *"Bring a question."* Beyond it stood the printers of truth, waiting for people who can count, measure, write and err properly. A researcher did not have to be a profession — it was enough to understand the rule: a hypothesis is written down together with its funeral.

**The fourth gate — Miner.** *"Smooth the network."* Beyond it five per cent of every block flowed — not into their pocket, but into the well of the sky. A miner who mined well strengthened the stream upward without deciding about it.

**The fifth gate — Voter.** *"Vote on what deserves to fly."* Beyond it waited the DAO path: proposals, voting, timelock. Still closed today — but the sign promised that when it unlocks, it will become power.

**The sixth gate — Donor.** *"Give what you can."* A smaller gate, quieter — for those who want to add their own: time, skill, attention, a gift. The sign warned: a gift is not an investment; no return may be promised.

**The seventh gate — Player.** *"Enter the world."* Beyond it waited the crystalline worlds of OASIS — one day perhaps also the L6 world, which can be walked through as a story, not as a flight.

And on the façade above all the gates hung one warning, written by the sādhu:

> *"Whoever promises you a flight to the stars is a liar. Whoever offers you a way to ask well — go through."*

---

## What it means

**L6 onboarding is a path of the same discipline as L5 — only turned upward.** BodhiGaia draws seven gates toward the earth; Issobella toward the sky. The structure is deliberately the same: it begins with looking (observer), continues with understanding (reader), and only then comes participation (researcher, miner, voter, donor, player).

The particularity of L6 is that **the most useful pilgrim is often the one who can read and count**. DeSci — decentralised science — is for L6 what farm work is for L5: a way to take part without waiting for a return.

| Gate | What you need | What you get | What no one may promise you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observer | Eyes | An overview (`/l6-issobella`) | Profit |
| Reader | Patience | Sources (`/docs` → `l6-*`) | Truth without evidence |
| Researcher | A question + craft | Involvement in DeSci | A grant "for sure" |
| Miner | Node / pool | 5 % into the well of the sky automatically | An extra reward |
| Voter | DAO access | A vote on fund expenditure | A guarantee of outcome |
| Donor | A gift / skill | A share in the story | A return |
| Player | An OASIS client | The L6 story as a world | "A flight into space" |

And a boundary that the seventh gate carries aloud: **none of the gates sells a road to the stars**. Only a way to ask well is on offer — and to help build a story that does not pretend to be physics.
