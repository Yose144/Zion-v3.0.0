## The Story

The first thing a newcomer on the wheel learns is not how to float. It is how to **breathe with the others**.

An old navigator — they called him Korma — led new people around the ring and showed them a curious schedule of the day, hung where elsewhere a menu hangs. Not a duty roster, but a **roster of the body**:

*"Half an hour against resistance, half an hour running, twenty minutes in the small wheel, a quarter of an hour stretching. Every day. Not because you are to be strong. Because your bones forget that they are meant to be bones."*

He pointed to the greenhouse by the wall of the ring — a long band of green behind glass. *"And this is not food. Well — a little it is. But mainly it is proof that something other than us can grow. On the old stations they said the cosmonauts talked to the plants more than to mission control. And they knew why: a plant never asks when the next ship arrives."*

In the evening the crew gathered in the common zone — and there a rule held that surprised newcomers: **no one was allowed to be fine all the time.**

*"Elsewhere they say a crew must hold together,"* said Korma. *"We say it differently: a crew must have somewhere to step back. Whoever has nowhere to go alone takes it out on the others. That is why everyone has a cabin that others open only when called — and the privacy of the mind is sacred. What you think is yours. What you say is the crew's. What you do is the ship's."*

And then there was one more rule, quieter: **rituals**. Shared meals. A week counted even where there is no Sunday. Small celebrations that no one skipped, because in a closed world time without celebration dissolves like salt in water.

*"A long mission is not like a journey,"* said Korma. *"It is like a marriage to the ship and to everyone on it. And as in a marriage — it is not survived by those who love each other, but by those who have learned to talk."*

## What it means

**The crew of Issobella is not a complement — it is a micro-society.** The research bundle `L6data/Lidske_Faktory.md` joins medicine, psychology and sociology into a single insight: in a closed world, technology and mind translate into one another. Broken communication kills as reliably as a broken valve.

From the bundle comes a concrete discipline:

- **Body:** a daily exercise regimen (resistance 30 min, walking/running 30 min, centrifuge 20 min, yoga 15 min) — the price for bones that thin in weightlessness.
- **Mind:** privacy as infrastructure — a cabin that others open only on invitation; psychological data are protected ("mental health privacy").
- **Community:** rituals and shared time; analogue missions (Mars-500, HI-SEAS) showed that isolation breaks people first in small things — in noise, in smell, in the lack of space for solitude.
- **Green:** plants as a technology of mood — they clean the air, feed, and hold the psyche; in the narrative of the series they connect directly to the L5 Hanuman missions (trees as the memory of the planet).

And one principle the book takes as a motto: **a crew is not a selection of the perfect — it is an agreement of the perfectible**. People are chosen by how they can repair, not by how they can avoid erring.
