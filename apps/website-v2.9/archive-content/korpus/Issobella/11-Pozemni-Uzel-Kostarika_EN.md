## The Story

When the pilgrims first raised their eyes from the sea to the stars, they did not see a station — they saw a **gap**. Somewhere up there, in an orbit they could so far calculate only on paper, a home named Issobella was one day to circle. But a station in orbit is like a kite without a string: a beautiful idea that means nothing without the ground.

*"Every station has two halves,"* said Only-that, and drew a circle in the sand with a single point beneath it. *"The one that flies — and the one that listens. No crew lifts off until it has somewhere to speak to. And that second half is not built into the sky. It is built into the clay."*

They looked for a place for that second half. Not where land is most expensive — where it is **most precise**: on the narrow bridge between two continents, under the sky of the ninth parallel, in a land that traded an army for schools and where someone once built an engine meant to fly to Mars.

The pilgrim remembered the words of the old woman of the circle of elders — *listen first, then build* — and understood that a ground node is not built only into the land, but into a relationship. On the same plot held by the Garden of the New America, an antenna was one day to grow beside a cacao tree: **L5 underfoot, L6 overhead.**

*"Here,"* said Only-that, and drew a circle around the point in the sand, *"isotropy touches a concrete place. The universe has no centre — but communication does. And it needs a home."*

## What it means

**The Costa Rica ground node is the first physical trace of L6 on Earth — a research campus and the future ground segment of the Issobella station.** Where the orbital station is a HORIZON decades away, the ground node is the way to begin building L6 **today**: a laboratory, an antenna field, an education centre and an observatory — on land shared with the L5 node LUMI (New America).

### Why Costa Rica — the technical logic

| Factor | Fact | Significance for L6 |
|---|---|---|
| **Latitude** | ~9–10° N | Proximity to the equator → more passes for low-inclination and equatorial orbits; longer contact windows for equatorial tracking |
| **Two ocean fronts** | Pacific + Caribbean in one territory | Average coverage for TT&C and optical/radio observation; redundancy of data paths |
| **Space ecosystem** | Agencia Espacial Costarricense (2021); the Irazú CubeSat (2018); Ad Astra Rocket Company in Liberia (VASIMR plasma engine, Franklin Chang Díaz) | A country with real space research — partnerships, personnel and know-how within reach |
| **Energy** | ~98 % renewable grid | Clean energy for both the laboratory and the antenna field |
| **Stability** | No army, a stable democracy, cheap rural land | A feasible land purchase and long-term operation |
| **Climate** | Tropical, two seasons | Limitation: humidity baffles, tropical corrosion; lightning protection is a design element, not a detail |

### What the ground node means architecturally

Every orbital station has three segments: **space** (the station), **user** (missions and science) and **ground** (TT&C — telemetry, tracking, command; data downlink; an operations centre). Issobella today exists as a fund, a tracker and a research bundle — the ground node is the first segment that can be built without a rocket:

| Segment | State in L6 | The Costa Rica ground node |
|---|---|---|
| Space | HORIZON (2040+) | Preparation of communication interfaces |
| User | BUILDING (DeSci, the research bundle) | Laboratories, residencies, education |
| **Ground** | **this chapter — HORIZON** | **TT&C antennas, data centre, observatory** |

### Campus programmes

| Programme | Content | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| **TT&C ground station** | S/X-band antennas, LEO CubeSat tracking, connection to the amateur satellite network (SatNOGS) | 2030+ |
| **Research laboratory** | Plasma/small propulsion energetics (inspired by VASIMR/Ad Astra in Guanacaste), materials, ECLSS prototypes | 2029+ |
| **Observatory** | Optical and radio observation; a SETI data pipeline | 2030+ |
| **DeSci centre** | Residencies for researchers, open-science publication, partnerships with universities in CR and Polynesia | 2028+ |
| **Education** | Programmes for local communities and indigenous territories — science as a bridge, not as an export | from the start |
| **Connection to L5** | Land shared with New America: LoRa mesh, Medical Table, seed library, water/energy | shared |
