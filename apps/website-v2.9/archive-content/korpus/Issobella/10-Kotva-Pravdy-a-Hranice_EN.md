## The Story

At the end of the road of the sky the pilgrim came to the last tent of the camp — the largest and quietest. On its wall hung no pictures and no maps. There hung **a single table** — and above it was written: *"This is where we come when the story collides with a fact."*

Beside the table sat Only-that, keeping records. *"This is a register,"* he said. *"Every image in the book has its row. When someone comes and says: this is not true — we do not argue. We go to the row, read the anchor and repair what is needed. Not the story for the sake of a feeling — the story for the sake of truth."*

He pointed to three columns. *"LIVE — what you can check in the code and on the chain. BUILDING and HORIZON — what is being done and what is still ahead of us. MYTH — what is beautiful and true as an image, but not as an event."*

The pilgrim asked: *"And what if a myth and a fact collide?"*

*"Then we repair the myth,"* said Only-that simply. *"Not the fact. Never the fact."*

Under the table there were three more lists: **what Issobella is not** (the sale of space, a promise of flight, a physical theory, militarisation, a replacement for the canon), **known discrepancies** (places where the stories and the documents have not yet met) and **open questions** (where the truth has not yet grown).

The pilgrim was silent for a long time. Then he asked: *"And why is all this done? Why keep a book so strictly?"*

Only-that looked up at the sky, where the first stars were beginning to appear above the camp. *"Because one day — perhaps in fifteen years, perhaps in thirty — someone will look up and ask: *is it flying up there, or not?* And we want to be able to answer with the truth — not with a story we grew fond of."*

### What Issobella is not

- **It is not a sale of space** — no shares, no tokenised orbital real estate.
- **It is not a promise that it will fly** — the roadmap 2030–2050 is a horizon, not a guarantee.
- **It is not a physical theory** — quantum fire is a question, not a conclusion.
- **It is not militarisation** — L6 does not engage in armament; safety gates are part of the design.
- **It is not a replacement for `StatusV3.md` / `MiseAmenti/07` / `L6data/`** — where they differ, the canon prevails.
- **It is not a successor to the ISS, NASA, ESA or any other programme** — it draws lessons from history, not a heritage.

### Known discrepancies and open questions

- **`nirvana/11` gives the "5 % L6 fund" at the address `zion1z4s3a5...`** — correctly as the *subsidy stream*, but premine slot 6 (`zion1f5h5k6t8...`) is a separate entity; this book distinguishes both (ch. 6).
- **`worlds.ts` does not yet have an L6 world** — OASIS holds L4; L6 is reflected only as a story/direction.
- **A public portal of the fund is missing** — the dashboard is an operator one; API-key enforcement is not enforced.
- **The DeSci programme is not formalised** — no board, no public call.
- **What is the correct etymology of "Issobella"?** — the canonical mythology says *iso + bella*; the historical origin of the name in the git history is not documented (see the `Issabela1stSkelet` render from `5ec065b81`).
