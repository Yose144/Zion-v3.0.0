## The Story

Anyone who lasted a week in the Genesis Garden and was still restless was shown by Ana to the south, where an island rose above the sea with its summit lost in the clouds. *"There. But do not go there for the view. Go when you have so much noise in your head that you can no longer hear your own breath."*

The island was greener than anything the pilgrims had ever seen. The soil from volcano ash was black, warm and so fertile that trees grew as though someone were pulling them towards the sky. Water ran down the slopes. At night the sky was so dark that the Milky Way cast a shadow.

And on the slope halfway between the sea and the summit, at the height where the sunny south meets the rainy north, stood **a temple that was not of marble**.

The pilgrims knew the temples of the old world: of stone, heavy, with gold and guards at the door. This one was different. Seven domes of wood, bamboo and glass — one large in the middle and six smaller in a circle around it — joined by covered walkways, between which gardens grew and the water of a pond gleamed. On the roofs of the domes lay solar panels like scales. The domes did not look built; they looked **grown** — as though someone had planted seven seeds of geometry and let them sprout.

In the middle of the main dome there was no altar. There was **a tree**.

A living tree that grew through the floor and whose crown touched the apex of the vault, where there was glass so that light would fall on it. Around the trunk was a circle of basalt, and on it people sat — in silence.

A man with a simple staff came to them, whom the pilgrims knew from Salt of This Earth as **Sadhu**, the Pilgrim of Silence. He did not speak at once. He waited until the breath of those arriving slowed.

*"We call that tree Bodhi Gaia,"* he said at last. *"Bodhi is awakening. Gaia is the Earth. Beneath the Bodhi tree a man once sat until he awoke. Here we sit beneath a tree until the Earth awakes — that is: until we awake to it. Not to the sky. To the soil the tree drinks from."*

*"Why seven domes?"* asked the carpenter, who could not stop counting beams.

*"Because seven is wholeness. The one in the middle is for silence. The six around it are for learning: about the earth, about knowledge, about geometry, about craft, about nature and about the future. Whoever comes passes through them all. A child and an old person. Little Buddhas and those who do not remember being Buddhas."*

*"And that structure — two interwoven pyramids I see in the ground plan?"*

*"Merkaba. Sky and earth interwoven, a chariot of light. But do not let words deceive you. It is only geometry that holds a roof with the least amount of wood. What is sacred about it is not that it is a triangle. What is sacred is that it does not fall down."*

The pilgrims stayed. Some seven days, some fourteen, some twenty-one. In the morning they worked in the syntropic garden on the slope — planting bananas between avocados, papayas between coffee, cutting biomass and leaving it lying so that it would feed the soil. In the afternoon they were silent. At night they walked to the ridge to look at the volcano that had burned years before, and at the stars that shone without competition.

When they left, each received a seed of a local variety and a small wooden tablet with a carved sign. *"Not as a trophy,"* said Sadhu. *"As a reminder that you went back different. And that this can be forgotten."*

## What it means

**Dharma Temple is the spiritual and educational node of the Terra Nova network — the Sanctuary, the Trunk of L5 Free World. A place where physical self-sufficiency and inner practice grow from the same root.** While the Genesis Garden is a gate (movement, work, ocean), Dharma Temple is a deeper stopping place for those who need more than a farm. Both projects are nodes of the same network: a different energy, the same intent.

### La Palma — La Isla Bonita

The greenest of the Canary Islands, a UNESCO biosphere reserve, the Caldera de Taburiente national park, 700–1,500 mm of rain per year, volcanic soil that holds both warmth and moisture, and nights so dark that one of the best observatories in the world stands there (Roque de los Muchachos, 2,396 m). Recommended position: the western side, 400–800 m — a balance of sun and rain. And also **a real risk**: Cumbre Vieja erupted in 2021.

### The architecture of the New Bodhi Gaia

| Element | Description | Symbolism (an image, not a doctrine) |
|---|---|---|
| **1 main dome** | The temple of awakening, the main hall, meditation, rites | The centre, silence |
| **6 smaller domes** | Museums and temples of knowing in a circle: *Earth* (soil, ecology, permaculture), *Knowledge* (library, archive), *Cognition / Geometry* (sacred geometry, mathematics, philosophy), *Nature and craft* (traditional techniques, local culture), *Future and technology* (free energy, DAO governance, ZION), + a meditation space | Seven parts of being, seven planets, seven chakras |
| **The Tree of Life — Bodhi Gaia** | The living axis of the temple precinct, the point of connection with nature | Awakening to the Earth |
| **Merkaba** | The ground-plan geometry of two interwoven tetrahedra | The union of sky and earth, a chariot of light |
| **Covered walkways, gardens, a pond, a perimeter walk** | Connection of the domes, a water element, a protective circle | Unity, cycle |

**Construction and materials:** geodesic domes (light, strong, economical), wood, bamboo, glass, stone; triple / ETFE foil as a translucent envelope; solar panels integrated into the roofs; collection and filtration of rainwater; passive ventilation; a circular stone foundation; compost; gardens of medicinal plants as part of the programme.

### Silence as a protocol

Dharma Temple differs from the other nodes in that **silence is part of governance**:

- Every keeper completes a **21-day silent retreat** before taking on a role.
- In the Dharma Circle there is **no electronics**; major decisions have **48 hours of reflection**.
- A new keeper goes through a 30-day trial retreat before entry into the DAO.
- Circles: *Practice* (morning practice, retreats), *Earth* (syntropic garden, infrastructure), *Finance*, *Community* (admission, conflict, protocols of silence), *Knowledge* (documentation, seed library).
