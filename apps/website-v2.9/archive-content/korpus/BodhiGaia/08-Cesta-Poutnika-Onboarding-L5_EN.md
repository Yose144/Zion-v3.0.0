## The Story

On the post by the gate of the Genesis Garden a board had hung for some time. It bore no rules. It bore **seven doors** carved side by side, each of a different size, and beneath them a single sentence: *Choose the door according to what your life can carry — not according to which one sounds best.*

A young man came with a backpack and a telephone in his hand. *"I want to help the earth. Where do I sign up? How much does it cost? What do I get for it?"*

Ana set down the hoe. *"Three questions, three wrong ones. There is no sign-up. It costs nothing — and if someone tells you it costs something, run. And you will get nothing for it that could be sold. But let us try it differently. How much time do you have?"*

*"A week."*

*"Then the first door. Do not travel anywhere. Sit down and read. Look at the explorer, find the address of the fund of the Earth and count how much has flowed into it since midnight. Compare it with what the web says. If it does not agree, write to us — that is the first service to the earth: to watch that the mirror does not lie."*

*"I can do that in an evening. What next?"*

*"The second door: come. Sleep in a tent, eat what we cook, dig in the morning, surf or keep silent in the evening. You pay like any other guest, or you work twenty-five hours a week and stay for free. You will leave with calluses. That is all I promise you."*

*"And if I wanted to stay?"*

*"The third door is narrow. Six months on site. Or twelve in the temple — and there twenty-one days of silence before we give you the key to anything. Four gates: you write who you are and why you are coming; you sit with the circle; you try it for a month; and then the circle says yes or no. No one will test you on faith. They will test you on whether a tidy kitchen remains behind you."*

The man looked at his telephone. *"I… I am more of that sort. Networks, servers."*

*"The fourth door. A technical keeper need not be here. They look after the node, the mesh, the solar remotely, and come once a quarter. But careful — the node does not **stand** here yet. Whoever comes saying they want to 'operate a Guardian node in the Garden' will get the answer: 'Help us build it. Phase two.'"*

*"The fifth?"*

*"To vote. When the fund starts distributing — and for now it does not — it will be voted on. Whoever wants to vote must know what they are voting on. For now that is learned by reading proposals, not by clicking."*

*"The sixth?"*

*"To give. Money, seeds, tools, hours of work on documentation. But listen carefully: **none of it is an investment**. You are not buying a share, land or future yield. You give to the garden because you want it to grow. Whoever promises you the opposite is not from this garden."*

*"And the seventh?"*

Ana smiled. *"The seventh is the door into the mirror. OASIS. Take the avatar Sita or Hanuman, walk in the Garden of Hiranyagarbha, look for traces of the Golden Egg. It is a game and it is a school. Only do not forget that points from the mirror are not exchanged for clay."*

The man stood before the board for a long time. Then he pointed at the first door. *"I will start here."*

*"Good,"* said Ana and picked up the hoe. *"That is the only right beginning."*

## What it means

**Onboarding into L5 is not a funnel. It is Hanuman's bridge from `MiseAmenti/02`: simple enough for a newcomer to cross, honest enough that they do not lose their bearings on it.** The seven gates correspond to seven real ways of entering the L5 layer today — and for each it is said what is available **now** and what is a horizon.

### What no one in L5 may ask of you

- **KYC, a personnel reference, a geographical block.** Entry into a community is the consent of the circle, not an identity document. (Legal entities naturally comply with local law — that is a different matter from blockchain identity.)
- **The purchase of ZION.** No gate requires holding a token. A Guardian node is a voluntary project of the community, not a condition of membership.
- **Faith.** Bodhi, Dharma, piko, Merkaba are images. The test is a tidy kitchen, not a doctrine.
- **Silence about risks.** Fire in central Portugal, a volcano on La Palma, a cyclone on Raiatea, a flood in Bohemia, a monsoon in Sri Lanka, evacuation, insurance, volatility — all of it is in the community documents and must appear in every booking flow too.

### What no one in L5 may promise you

- **A yield.** Nothing in this book is an investment.
- **Impact.** Until a project has an impact packet, no one may say "your stay / your gift paid for the well".
- **Paradise.** See Te Pīko Ora: reality is better than paradise — and reality does not stand yet.
- **Finished doors.** Genesis: a tent and a bed (Phase 1). Dharma Temple: a concept and a curriculum (Phase 0). Te Pīko Ora: a map and questions (Phase 0 from 2026–2027). Golden Republic Bohemia: the Golden Republic protocol and a search for a circle (Phase 0). Bodhi Lanka: the Bhakti protocol and a search for a partner (Phase 0 from 2027–2028).

### The safe format of every invitation (from `MiseAmenti/05` §6, translated for L5)

```text
INTENT:        What you want to do in the garden (host / work / keep / help remotely / give).
PLAN:          When, where, how long, under what conditions.
EVIDENCE:      A link to the community document and the phase state (LIVE / BUILDING / HORIZON).
RISKS:         Travel, health, weather, legal form, insurance, volatility.
ALTERNATIVES:  A shorter stay, another node, help from here.
ACTION:        A concrete first step — and the option to leave at any time.
APPROVAL:      Who in the circle confirms (for keepers: the four gates).
```
