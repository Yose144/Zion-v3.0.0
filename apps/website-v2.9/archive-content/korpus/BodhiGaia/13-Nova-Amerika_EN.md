## Bridge & Memory · Costa Rica — the last L5 node, the land of the first peoples and the geographical centre between two continents

> *"A bridge does not join two shores in order to change them. It joins them so that they can tell each other stories."*

## The Story

The pilgrims stood at the end of the map they had drawn themselves. The root in the Nabão valley near Tomar. The trunk on La Palma. The crown on Raiatea. The heart in Bohemia. The space in Lanka. Five nodes, five elements, five answers. And yet — when they bent over the map for the last time — one place remained on it to which no line led, and yet all of them led through it.

*"Between the North and the South,"* said Sita, laying her palm on the intersection of two oceans, *"lies a land so narrow that you can hardly see it on a map. But without it America would be two halves that never met."*

They sailed west. This time not for a tree or a lagoon — for **a bridge no one had built**. Costa Rica. The land bridge between two continents, narrow as a neck, wide as a door. A country that abolished its army and has teachers instead of soldiers; where six percent of the planet's biodiversity lives on three hundredths of a percent of its dry land.

On the beach where the Pacific opened wide and calm, neither a temple nor a farm awaited them. **A circle of elders** awaited them — people who had sat there before any of the pilgrims knew that a circle exists in which one sits. Bribri. Cabécar. Boruca. Ngäbe. Maleku. Huetar. Chorotega. Térraba. The first peoples of a land the pilgrims had imagined as *new* — and which was older than all their books together.

The elders did not speak of projects. They spoke of a land that remembers. Of the cacao that Sibö gave to the people so that they would have bread and the sacred in one bean. Of the Talamanca mountains where the weather was born. Of rivers that know the way to both oceans — because a land that is a bridge can never be only one shore.

The pilgrim who remembered what it means to hold a knife did not raise a knife this time. He sat down. He listened. And after a long silence he understood the last lesson of the Book of the Earth:

*"A garden does not begin with planting. It begins by asking the land what it remembers — and by asking the people who were here before anyone who is allowed to say no."*

The elders looked at one another. Then one of them — an old woman with palms like cacao bark — laid before the pilgrims the single question that carried weight in that circle:

*"Have you come to build — or have you come to listen?"*

And the carpenter answered as one answers all decisive questions in the Book of the Earth: truthfully.

*"First to listen. Then — if you permit — to build with you."*

The old woman nodded. *"That is the right order. Most people swap it — and then wonder why nothing grows for them."*

Above them, in the evening sky, the first stars began to appear. And somewhere beyond the horizon, on the same plot, a plan for a second building was already waiting — one that was not to grow into clay but to speak to the sky. Earth and sky. L5 and L6. In one place that is the centre of nothing — and therefore the centre of everything.

## What it means

**New America is the sixth and last L5 node — a project for the native cultures of the Americas, located in Costa Rica.** Where the first five nodes form a pentagram of elements (Earth, Fire, Water, Air, Akasha), New America does not stand in line as a sixth element — it stands **outside it, as bridge and memory**: a geographical centre that joins North and South, and a cultural anchor that joins the present with the first peoples. The project prototypes the **Bridge protocol** — a way of building an L5 node *with* the first peoples, not *beside* them.

### Why Costa Rica — the geographical centre

| Property | Fact | Meaning for L5/L6 |
|---|---|---|
| **Geography** | A land bridge between North and South America; Pacific + Caribbean within reach | The literal centre of the Americas — a node joining both halves of the continent |
| **Latitude** | ~9–10° N | Proximity to the equator — advantageous for the L6 ground segment (LEO tracking, TT&C) |
| **Stability** | No army since 1948, a stable democracy | A safe haven for long-term fund projects |
| **Biodiversity** | ~6 % of world biodiversity on 0.03 % of dry land | A living library of native crops and medicine |
| **Land** | Cheap rural land outside protected territories | Physically feasible acquisition even for a smaller fund |
| **First peoples** | Bribri, Cabécar, Boruca, Ngäbe, Maleku, Huetar, Chorotega, Térraba; 24 territories, Ley Indígena 6172 (1977) | Partnership, not colonisation — the territories are legally protected |
| **Space footprint** | Agencia Espacial Costarricense (2021); the first satellite Irazú (2018); Ad Astra Rocket Company (Liberia) — Franklin Chang Díaz's VASIMR plasma engine | An L6 research context directly in the country — plasma energetics next door |

### The Bridge protocol — how to build with the first peoples

| Principle | Description | Counterpart |
|---|---|---|
| **FPIC** | Free, Prior and Informed Consent — before any step touching traditions, languages or territory | Consent decision-making, translated to the intercultural plane |
| **Circle of elders** | Indigenous governance as a partner in decision-making, not as folklore | A council of elders beside sociocratic circles |
| **No commodification** | No sale of "native spirituality", no tokenised traditions, no NFT of ceremonies | An anti-grift safeguard; protection of the integrity of the project and of the partners |
| **First to listen** | Advisers: listen → consent → only then build | Consciousness Admission, extended by culture, not only by person |
| **Memory as infrastructure** | A living library of languages, seeds and knowledge — documented only with the consent of the owners of the tradition | Knowledge commons; a seed library with native varieties |

**The legal framework:** Ley Indígena 6172 (1977) reserves ownership of land in indigenous territories to the original communities — which is why New America does not stand *on* a territory but **beside it**: land outside the territories + a formal MOU with partners. The model is the reverse of what history knows: not "we build it and they may come", but "they decide what from their world is carried into the garden".

### A shared plot with L6 Issobella

New America shares its location with the **Issobella ground station** (`L6data/Pozemni_Stanice_Kostarika.md`, `Issobella/11`). One plot, two layers: L5 cares for the land and the people, L6 builds the ground segment and the research campus. The administrator of the L6 fund is **Aelan Vaast** — a Polynesian woman who runs the fund independently until Sarah Hubalková's 18th birthday, and afterwards cooperates with her. The joining of both projects in one place is intent, not coincidence: *Bodhi Gaia cares for the earth beneath us, Issobella watches the sky above us — in Costa Rica they hold hands.*
