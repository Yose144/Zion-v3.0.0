## What is Bodhi Gaia?

**Bodhi** (Sanskrit बोधि) means *awakening*. **Gaia** is the Greek name for Earth as a living whole. *New Bodhi Gaia* is the image that first sounded in the Dharma Temple concept on La Palma — "a place of enlightenment and nirvana for little Buddhas and all beings of light", with the **Tree of Life** as the axis of the whole precinct. This book extends that image to the whole **L5 Free World** layer:

> **Bodhi Gaia is not another planet. It is this Earth, and someone has begun to care for it differently.**

The Nirvana book tells how the pilgrims landed on the New Earth after the flood, built the House of Lumi, spanned the abyss with WARP and opened the springs of Free World. This book is about **what happens when the pilgrims take soil into their hands**. It is a book about six nodes in six real places — the Atlantic coast of Portugal, the volcanic island of La Palma, the lagoon of Raiatea, the heart of Bohemia, the island of Sri Lanka and the land bridge of the Americas in Costa Rica — and about how an abstract 5 % of the L1 coinbase stream becomes a well, a tree, a dome, a canoe, a circle, a bridge and the space that holds them all.

It is also the strictest book of the whole series. **Soil does not know marketing.** A tree either grows or it does not. A well either gives water or it does not. That is why every chapter carries a longer truth anchor than anywhere else: it says exactly what of L5 runs on the network today, what is being built, what is only a horizon and what is merely a beautiful image.

## The five states of claim this book uses

| Tag | Meaning in the Book of Earth |
|---|---|
| **LIVE** | Running on Edge, or in the L1 consensus, and verifiable today (address, service, page, file). |
| **BUILDING** | Code, a document, a team or land under negotiation exists; production or independent proof is missing. |
| **HORIZON** | A legitimate direction (dome, canoe, mesh, portal) that nobody today has the right to present as finished. |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | A claim that can be refuted (for example, that the community model works economically). |
| **MYTH** | An archetype and an image — Sita, Hanuman, Rama, the Tree of Life, the Merkaba, Sri Maha Bodhi. Inspiration, not doctrine. |

> **No photograph of a well, a tree or a child is proof of impact without consent, provenance and independent verification.** ([`MiseAmenti/04`, M5](../MiseAmenti/04-Exekucni-Charta-3.3.md))

## What this book is not

- **It is not a sale of land or shares.** No garden here offers an investment, a return or "tokenized soil". The communities are legally independent and economically autonomous.
- **It is not a religion.** Bodhi, Dharma, Merkaba, *piko* and the Tree of Life are archetypes borrowed with respect; the book claims no spiritual authority and no lineage.
- **It is not a claim of impact.** Until there is a public *impact packet* with a recipient, a budget, an on-chain reference and independent verification, no project in this book **is** "verified impact".
- **It is not a replacement for the whitepaper or `StatusV3.md`.** Wherever the story speaks about a number, the canonical source wins.
