# BODHI GAIA — Chapter 3: The Genesis Garden
## Base Camp · Sabacheira, Tomar, Portugal — a garden between Fátima and the Templar town, and the first tree planted as a rite

> *"Planting trees is not a PR activity. It is a rite of taking root. Every tree that grows here will still be here when this team is long gone."*

---

## The Story

The first to come down from the slope below the house of Lumi all the way to the sea was a young woman who on the ark did what no one else wanted — she emptied the buckets of water from the hold. They called her **Ana**. She could not read a map, could not preach and could not build bridges. She could do two things: work with her hands and swim in cold water.

She came to the place where the clay ended and the sand began. The wind blew there from the west without ceasing, and at night you could hear the waves breaking on the cliffs. The soil was poor, salty, full of stones. Anyone else would have gone on.

Ana knelt, took a handful of earth in her palms — as Sita had done — and felt that it was warm. *"Here,"* she said to no one.

Within a week she had her first bed. Within a month three people came from the house of Lumi, because they had heard that "down by the sea someone is growing tomatoes and surfs in the evening". Within half a year there stood on the slope above the bay the first canvas tent, beside it a barrel of water, and around it a circle of stones one could sit on.

Ana did not call the place a temple or a sanctuary. She called it **the Garden** — and because it was the first garden of the New Earth, the others added the word **Genesis**.

When the carpenter came to look, he expected a farm. He found something else: a place where people met at six in the morning by the circle of stones, divided the work — who digs today, who cooks, who fetches water, who repairs the fence — and in the evening, when the sun set into the Atlantic, sat on the same circle and spoke of what had failed.

*"This is not a farm,"* said the carpenter.

*"No,"* answered Ana. *"This is a gate. The farm is only the way of keeping it open. Whoever comes here need not believe in anything. They only have to be able to put a hand to the work and withstand the wind. If they withstand it for a week, they may go up into the mountains for the silence or on to the water for the canoe. And if not — they go home with palms full of calluses and the smell of rosemary. Even that is enough."*

That evening they planted the first tree together — an olive, because it withstands drought, wind and salt. Ana said only one sentence, which then became the rule of the Garden:

*"This tree will give oil when none of us is here. We plant in biological time, not in the time of accountants."*

The carpenter carved three signs into the post by the gate: a wave, a hoe and an olive leaf. Beneath them he wrote: **Base Camp**.

Many stories later — in another book of this series — a different pilgrim came to this bay: a child with a lantern, carrying an alabaster vessel with a second seed. What happened at the edge of the bed is told by the [epilogue of *The Story of the Rose*](./12-Ruze-v-Zahrade-Genesis.md) and by [Chapter 12](./12-Ruze-v-Zahrade-Genesis.md) of this book — and the whole scene is **MYTH**, not a record of a real event.

## What it means

**The Genesis Garden is the first physical node of Terra Nova in Europe — an eco-farm in the freguesia of Sabacheira near Tomar, in the Nabão valley, where organic farming, surfing, off-grid technology and community life intersect. Its role in L5 Free World is the Root: Base Camp, the entrance gate, the place one can come to for the first time.**

The intention is not perfection. The intention is **real operation open to people** — a farm that grows together with people, not without them.

### The character of the place: a border of two worlds

The Genesis Garden stands on the border between the quiet inland of the farm and the wild Atlantic coast. This duality — the silence of the soil and the energy of the ocean — is deliberate. The project seeks people who can work in clay and surf waves. Farmers and surfers. Builders and meditators.

### What the Garden does (Phase 1 — Roots)

| Area | Phase 1 plan | Phase 2 plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy** | 5 kWp PV + 10 kWh LiFePO4 battery (~20 kWh/day in summer), a DC microgrid for lights, solar water heating | 15 kWp + 30 kWh + grid fallback (~60 kWh/day) |
| **Water** | A borehole of 80–120 m, a 5 m³ tank, sand filter + UV, a reed-bed greywater treatment, composting toilets | Borehole + rainwater, a 20 m³ underground cistern, a biodigester |
| **Food** | 0.5 ha organic + agroforestry; 20 % caloric self-sufficiency; chickens, bees | 2 ha syntropic agroforestry + keyline; 50 %; goats, ducks, aquaponics |
| **Accommodation** | 8–12 guests: safari tents, the core team in a renovated building | 20–30 guests: wooden eco-cabins, a community yurt |
| **Trees** | Fig, pomegranate, citrus, olive; cover crops | Olive oil, wine, dried herbs, seedlings as market crops |
| **Seed library** | A catalogue of local varieties | Exchange with Dharma Temple and other L5 nodes |

### An economy that does not lie

The Garden is **economically autonomous and protocol-aligned**. It does not live from the L5 fund; it lives from what it produces:

| Income | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glamping / eco-tourism | 70 % | 50 % | 35 % |
| Farm products (market, CSA) | 10 % | 20 % | 25 % |
| Workshops / retreats | 10 % | 15 % | 20 % |
| ZION Guardian node rewards | 5 % | 10 % | 15 % |
| Grants / crowdfunding | 5 % | 5 % | 5 % |

Phase 1 target: 50 000 EUR/year, break-even at 120 nights/month. Work exchange (25 h/week = accommodation + food) is limited to 2 working guests per 4 paying ones, so that the farm has both hands and income.

### The link to ZION

- **Guardian Node** (Phase 2, target 2027): a mini-PC (Intel N100 / Ryzen embedded), 15–25 W — it fits into the solar budget; Starlink + 4G failover + a LoRa relay. Reward split **90 % operator / 10 % community treasury**.
- **Treasury:** a 3-of-5 multisig (operations 2-of-3, cold reserve 3-of-5, tithe auto-forwarded monthly, seed library fund).
- **Governance:** sociocratic circles (General → Operations / Finance / Community / Expansion), deciding by consent ("no reasoned objection"), double-linking. Expenditure > 5 000 EUR goes to an on-chain proposal (7 days, 60 %). Admission of a new keeper: 14 days, consent.
- **Keeper roles:** Farm, Hospitality, Tech (remote OK), Finance (remote OK), Community — at least 6 months on site for on-site roles.

### Risks that are not kept quiet

Drought and lack of water (borehole + rain + greywater), **summer fires** (firebreaks, water reserves, an evacuation plan, insurance), the departure of a key keeper (cross-training, documentation), the volatility of ZION (treasury in stablecoins + ZION), crop failure (polyculture, seed diversity), injury to a guest (insurance, first aid).
