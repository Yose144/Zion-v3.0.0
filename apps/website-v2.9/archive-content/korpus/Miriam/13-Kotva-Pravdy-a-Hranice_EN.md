## The Story — the last evening in the cave

On the last evening Miriam did not tell a story. She took the alabaster vessel, turned it to the fire one last time, and handed it to Sarah.

*"You carry it onward,"* she said. *"It is empty — and you will carry it further. When someone on the way asks you for the story, tell it as I gave it to you: stone as stone, clay as clay, dream as dream. And when they try to sell you stone that they know to be a dream — ask them whom it serves."*

*"And if someone asks me whether you saw the true Miriam — tell the truth: you saw a woman who held a testimony that was not for sale. The rest is in the records — and anyone can open the records for themselves."*

Sarah went down to the town at dawn, the vessel under her cloak. Miriam stayed in the cave above the sea — there where the legend always wanted her — and watched the wave that broke on the rock and returned, like every story that is handed on.

And the sea carried onward everything brought to it — a testimony that holds by being handed on.

## What it means — the book's covenant with the reader

This book is a **living story** — and a living story survives only when the reader knows which part of it is stone, which is clay and which is a dream. Chapter 13 is not an appendix — it is part of the story: Miriam hands over the vessel and the reader takes over the duty of distinguishing.

**How to read the book:**

1. **Stone** you can verify — gospels, manuscripts, records, historical documents. When the book says "a record", open it.
2. **Clay** you honour as culture — legends, traditions, images that people carry. You do not give them the status of a record, and you do not deny them the status of beauty.
3. **A dream** you read as poetry — the connections the book weaves itself (Miriam and Lumi, the rose and the tree, the vessel and the chalice). They are true about the one who weaves them — not about what happened.
4. **UNSUBSTANTIATED** means: it is claimed, but it has not been established. It does not mean "they are lying" — it means "so far unverified, not for sale".
5. **INVALID CLAIM** means: the record runs against the facts — a forgery, a conflation with a documented truth, or a claim obtained through a lie.

## What this book is not

- **It is not a biography.** Miriam speaks in the first person — and yet the book itself says that her life from the records ends at the tomb. The voice is a literary instrument, not a biographical claim.
- **It is not theology.** The book reads Christian texts with reverence and at the same time keeps them on the table — it does not decide what is true of faith; it decides what is true of the documents.
- **It is not hidden history.** No secret line, no cover-up order, no "truth the church kept from you". Where the book mentions a secret, it names it — and shows how forgeries are made.
- **It is not a prophecy about ZION.** Revelation is a map of images here — dragon, wilderness, city, river — not a calendar of events. The corpus borrows the image; it never appropriates it.
- **It is not a financial document.** Nothing in the book promises a return, pushes a purchase, or defends an investment with sacred figures. Where the fund is mentioned, it is architecture that is discussed, not returns.
- **It is not a replacement for the registers.** `StatusV3.md`, `L2contracts.md`, the `internal repository` and `MiseAmenti/07-Registr-Dukazu.md` remain the only sources of operational truth. The book is a story — and a story never overrides a record.

## How to read Revelation in the context of ZION — the boundary

In this book Revelation is a **map**, not a **prediction**:

- **The dragon** = the system that swallows — not the name of one enemy. Where a system consumes witnesses, the dragon is waging war.
- **The wilderness with a place made ready** = a garden tended before anyone needs it.
- **The city that descends** = what is built elsewhere and prepared — not a utopia people claim.
- **The river and the tree** = what nourishes everyone and cannot be put up for sale.
- **"Come"** = an open invitation — a gate that does not close.

**We never say:** ZION fulfils Revelation; 144 billion is a prophecy; the city is our network. An image inspires architecture — it does not claim it. `INVALID CLAIM` is the correct label for any reading that turns a parable into a forecast of the network.
