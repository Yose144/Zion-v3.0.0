# MIRIAM — Chapter 10: The wedding at the end of the book
## Stone · Revelation 19 and 21 — a new heaven and a new earth, a city descending like a bride, a garment woven from deeds, and an invitation that is not inherited

> *"The wedding the record does not know, people dreamt up for themselves — and the wedding the record does know, people did not notice. The bride is not a woman beside one man. The bride is a city."*

---

## The Story

*"Now I will tell you about a wedding — and I will tell you exactly what the records say, because it is more beautiful than all the legends they tell about me,"* said Miriam.

*"His first sign happened at a wedding — at Cana, when the wine ran out and he turned water into wine so that the feast could go on. His last sign in the book is a wedding too — the whole bible ends with a feast. Listen to what John saw:*

*'I heard what seemed to be the voice of a great multitude — like the sound of many waters and like the sound of mighty thunders: Hallelujah! For the Lord our God the Almighty has begun his reign. Let us rejoice and exult and give him the glory — for the marriage of the Lamb has come, and his bride has made herself ready. And she was given to clothe herself in fine linen, bright and pure — the fine linen is the righteous deeds of the saints.'*

*And then, chapters later: a new heaven and a new earth — the first heaven and the first earth had passed away — and the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband. And a voice from the throne: 'Behold, the dwelling of God is with people — he will dwell with them and they shall be his people, and God himself will be with them and wipe every tear from their eyes. And death shall be no more, neither mourning, nor crying, nor pain any more — the first things have passed away. Behold, I make all things new.'"*

---

*"And now pay attention, Sarah — here the whole lie people told about me becomes visible at once.*

*The legends wanted me to be the bride of one man — a secret wedding, secret blood, a secret dynasty. And the record says something else and better: the bride is not a woman — the bride is a city. The wedding at the end of the book is not the union of two people — it is the union of everything alive with the one who made it. And the invitation is not inherited through blood — it is given to everyone who is invited, and that is everyone.*

*'Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb.' Invited — not born into the right family. Called — not chosen into a secret line. And the bride 'made herself ready' — she was not inherited; she put on the fine linen that was given to her — and that linen is righteous deeds. A garment sewn from deeds, not from blood. The genealogy you wear is woven from what you did — not from whom you were born to.*

*For the whole twentieth century people looked for a secret wedding — while an open one lay before them: a wedding to which everyone is invited, where garments are sewn from good works and where tears are wiped away. Do you know why they did not notice it? Because an open invitation cannot be sold. A secret one can."*

---

*"And one last thing about the Carpenter — because he is there too. He once said: 'In my Father's house there are many dwellings — I go to prepare a place for you.' The Carpenter, who built houses and gates all his life, promised to build a dwelling — and at the end of the book a city descends that no one was seen building. Built elsewhere, prepared beforehand — and descending when the time comes. The whole Earth was the workshop; the city arrives finished.*

*And me? I am not there as a secret wife in a bloody genealogy. I am there as the woman who saw first — and who now sees a city no one could have imagined: gates that never close, because there is no night there. And the rose I carried from the lake will meet there all the roses that ever bloomed — and no one will pick them any more to prove the truth with them. They grow. That is enough."*

---

**Stone, clay and dream.**

*"Stone: Revelation 19:6–9 — the voice of the multitude, the marriage of the Lamb, fine linen = righteous deeds, 'blessed are those invited'. Revelation 21:1–5 — a new heaven and a new earth, the city as a bride, the dwelling with people, tears wiped away, 'I make all things new'. Cana in John 2 — the first sign at a wedding. 'Many dwellings' in John 14 — the carpenter who builds.*

*Clay: the bride = the church/people of God — a documented reading, beautiful and open; the mystical marriages of saints — a tradition that lives in the orders.*

*Dream: that I was that bride — the record says a city, not a woman; that the wedding at Cana was mine — no record says so; that the wedding at the end means my dynastic marriage — a dream sold as stone, and we already know how that is done."*

---

## What it means

**The wedding at the end of the book is the open counterpart of all secret weddings: instead of the union of two people, the union of everything with the Source; instead of a blood claim, an invitation for everyone; instead of a crown, a garment sewn from deeds.**

1. **The bride = a city.** The book's greatest reversal: secret history sold the wedding of two people; Revelation offers the wedding of a city with the Source. The whole Christ-line question closes here: *they looked for the wedding in a bedroom — it was in a city.* A community as bride = the woman clothed with the sun grown into a city.
2. **An invitation instead of a genealogy.** "Blessed are those invited" — entry is given by calling and answering, not by blood. The exact architecture of the corpus's gate: "the gate no one has to unlock" ([`Lumi/05`](../Lumi/05-Brana-a-Dve-Knezky.md)) — anyone may enter, and the only thing that decides is whether you answer the call. Onboarding = an invitation, not a registration of lineage.
3. **A garment of deeds.** Fine linen = righteous deeds — a wardrobe you do not weave from property but from acts. The third trace of proof-of-work in the corpus: "calluses are proven" → "deeds are woven" → "what you did, you wear" ([`Lumi/12`](../Lumi/12-Brana-Zustava-Otevrena.md) — "what will remain of you").
4. **The Carpenter's dwelling.** "I go to prepare a place" — the city descends finished, built elsewhere. The corpus knows exactly this shape: a garden prepared before you need it ([`BodhiGaia/`](/maitreya/bodhigaia/cist)); a prepared place in the wilderness (Rev 12); an ark built in dry weather ([`nirvana/`](/maitreya/nirvana/cist)). *It is built where no one lives yet — and it descends when the time comes.*
5. **"I make all things new."** The sentence the corpus carries as the working name of the world it builds for: Terra Nova — a new earth — is not a new map, but a new counting. Rev 21:5 is the sentence that makes sense of everything before it: we do not recycle the old — we make all things new.
