# 01 — Canon and constitution of Mise Amenti
## Rules that hold the story, the code, human freedom and the long horizon together

> **Normative status:** This document is the binding editorial and planning constitution of the `MiseAmenti/` corpus.  
> **Technical limit:** It does not introduce a consensus change, does not replace security policy and does not give any person, agent or document authority over live code.

---

## 1. The basic principle

Mise Amenti stands on a simple sentence:

> **Technology is good only when it expands people's ability to verify, to decide freely, to care for one another and to correct mistakes without concealment.**

Five inseparable commitments follow from this sentence. They are derived from the five values formulated in `Road to Zion` — **truth, freedom, unity, love and service** — but they are written as testable principles for real software and a real community.

| Commitment | How it shows in code, operation and communication |
|---|---|
| **Truth** | Every technical claim has a source, a commit, a test, an on-chain proof, or is marked as a plan. A mistake is admitted and corrected. |
| **Freedom** | No pressure to buy, hold, believe, take on an identity, be politically loyal, or stay in the network. A user may leave at any time with their keys and their data. |
| **Unity** | Interoperability before lock-in, cooperation before a personality cult, more independent nodes before a single centre of power. |
| **Care** | Security, accessibility, comprehensible onboarding, incident response and transparent funds are not side features; they are part of the definition of finished work. |
| **Service** | Computation, AI and finance serve users and communities. They are never designed to maximise dependency, manipulation or hidden extraction. |

---

## 2. The five mandatory states of every claim

Every important claim about ZION 3.3, and especially every claim published outside internal planning, must carry one of these labels:

| Label | Meaning | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **LIVE** | The feature or service is deployed and verifiable at the same time. | A link to a live endpoint/on-chain data + relevant code or deployment evidence. |
| **BUILDING** | The work exists in the repository or is actively being realised, but is not yet finished in production. | A link to a branch/commit, an issue, a test or a specific acceptance criterion. |
| **HORIZON** | A long-term direction; not a binding date, nor a promised product. | A clearly marked goal, dependencies and the limits of the claim. |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | A research question that requires independent testing and can be refuted. | Methodology, a falsification criterion, open data or a clearly named absence of evidence. |
| **MYTH** | A literary, cultural or philosophical archetype. | A contextual disclaimer so that it is not confused with history, science or technical fact. |

### Examples of correct use

- "the primary node is **LIVE** on Edge" — if the current `StatusV3.md` and a live probe confirm it.
- "ZIS Passkeys are **BUILDING**" — if an implementation issue/plan exists; they **must not** be presented as working until there is code and an E2E test.
- "the Unreal Engine 5.7 client is **HORIZON**" — until a real client and a deploy exist in the source tree.
- "quantum propulsion is a **HYPOTHESIS**" — until there are reproducible experiments; never an "invention" or an "energy solution".
- "the Halls of Amenti are **MYTH**" — a literary image, not an Egyptological document nor proof of historical technology.

## 3. The interpretation of "Mise Amenti"

Mise Amenti is not a mission to control humanity, a fight against people, nor a project that can be carried out "for the good of people" without their consent.

It is a voluntary commitment to create an **Ark of record and care**:

1. **Record** — open source, an auditable history of decisions, technical documentation and transparent incident reports.
2. **Resilience** — multiple nodes, backups, disaster recovery, reproducible builds and the ability to continue even after the failure of an individual or a server.
3. **Free access** — a comprehensible path for the observer, the player, the builder and the operator, without the need to accept an ideology.
4. **Care in the architecture** — measurable, auditable humanitarian and scientific flows; no "trust us" funds.
5. **Handing on** — documentation, open protocols and a legal/technical architecture of such quality that the project outlives all its current authors.

"Completing Mise Amenti" is therefore **not a victory over an adversary**. It is a state in which verifiability, security, freedom of choice and shared care are no longer the rare experiment of one community but the ordinary way people handle technology.

---

## 4. Boundaries against abuse

The following things are incompatible with this canon, even if they bring growth, money or popularity in the short term:

- manipulative "last chance" appeals, profit guarantees, price predictions or investment promises;
- dark patterns, addictive UX, data collection without necessity and clear consent;
- automatic transfers of funds, changes to production, the firewall, deployment or governance without the relevant human approval;
- unverified medical, psychological, physical or spiritual claims;
- cultural appropriation, pretending to a Tibetan Buddhist lineage, or presenting the Amenti myth as history;
- communication that labels a critic an enemy or a challenge as betrayal;
- presenting AI as a being with demonstrable consciousness, spiritual authority or the right to decide for a person.

Detailed security and autonomy rules are in [`05-Autonomie-a-Bezpecnost.md`](./05-Autonomie-a-Bezpecnost.md); cultural and narrative boundaries in [`02-Pribeh-a-Architektura.md`](./02-Pribeh-a-Architektura.md).

## 5. Version and legal/communication discipline

- **"3.3 Nirvana" is, in this corpus, a planning and integration label.** It must not by itself be interpreted as a claim that the running binary already issues protocol version 3.3.
- The actual version of the product, release, binary or chain is taken **exclusively** from the current release, `StatusV3.md`, the `internal repository` and the code.
- Public materials must be clear, fair, not misleading and consistent with the technical whitepaper and applicable legal requirements. No mythological metaphor may weaken a security, financial or legal disclaimer.
- Every translation is an editorial change with its own review; a translation must not strengthen a certainty that is not in the original.

---

## 6. How a dispute is resolved

1. **Evidence has priority.** The code, the test, the live endpoint or on-chain data is checked.
2. **The discrepancy is recorded.** In [`07-Registr-Dukazu.md`](./07-Registr-Dukazu.md) or in an issue, the claim, source, evidence, decision and date are stated.
3. **The narrative is corrected, not the other way round.** If the story got ahead of reality, the text is corrected and a changelog is kept.
4. **A disputed claim is reduced by a state.** From `LIVE` to `BUILDING`, from `BUILDING` to `HORIZON`, or from `HYPOTHESIS` to "unsupported", until evidence arrives.
5. **Safety wins over speed.** In doubt, nothing is published, deployed or automated.

---

## 7. Canonical conclusion

> **Mise Amenti protects the possibility of the future, not an idea of who has the right to own it.**  
> **The story may be beautiful. The evidence must be precise. The person must remain free.**
