# LUMI — Chapter 11: A hundred years of the lantern
## 3.3 "Nirvana" · 2026–2126 — what 3.3 is and is not; six epochs in which the lantern passes from hand to hand; the rights of those who come after us

> *"No one today can promise the world of 2126. But we can leave the people of 2126 the tools, the evidence and the freedom to decide better than we did."*

---

## The Story

That night four sat by the fire beneath the tower: the carpenter, Sadhu, Only-that, and Lumi with the lantern in her lap.

*"Everywhere I have been they said *three three*,"* said Lumi. *"Nirvana. What is that?"*

Only-that first said "only that", as was his habit, and then: *"It is a name for work. Not for a finished thing. People like to confuse the name of a plan with the name of a ship. The ship we sail is called what is carved on its keel, and that is read from the keel, not from songs. *Three three* is a list of what we must do before we allow ourselves to say that we have sailed somewhere."*

*"And what must you do?"*

The carpenter took charcoal and wrote on stone, as though writing a list of repairs on a ship:

*"First the register — so that every word about the ship has a line where it is written whether it is so, or whether it is only going to be so. Then the house and the bridge — so that identity is safe and so that the bridge can be crossed in both directions even with one's own bundle. Then the voice in the cave — first fenced in, then with confirmation, never alone. Then the gate — open, safe, without traps for attention. Then the gardens — from the pile to a documented bucket. Then the sky — science before great words. Then the handing over — so that the ship sails even if we vanish tomorrow. And finally a review of everything we have written."*

*"That is eight."*

*"Nine gates are at the end,"* said Only-that. *"Truth, safety below, settlement on the bridge, the house, the voice, the gate, the gardens and the sky, the handing over, the speech to people. Each must have evidence. Until it does, no one may say *we have sailed there*. So far not one has been closed behind us."*

Lumi looked at the stone. *"That is a lot of work for one ship."*

*"It is not for one ship,"* said Sadhu. *"It is for a hundred years."*

---

And then Sadhu did something Lumi had not expected: he took the lantern from her lap and handed it to the carpenter. The carpenter handed it to Only-that. Only-that handed it back to Lumi. It went round, from hand to hand, and at every handing the light in it narrowed and showed a different Lumi.

**The child.** Six years old, barefoot, by the fire. The years when the ark is small and the most important thing is not to make a large unverifiable claim out of a small verifiable foundation. The lifted branch in the channel. *Anchoring and evidence.*

**The girl on the threshold of adulthood.** She stands on a foreign shore and teaches people she has never seen how to light their own fire, how to read a chain and how not to believe her. Ships are sailed even by those who were not on the ark; they translate her books into tongues the carpenter did not speak, and they criticise her. *Federation and local responsibility.*

**The woman.** The wind grows stronger — droughts, pressures, noise. The ship is not saved by becoming bigger, but by repairing itself quickly after every blow, with no one sweeping cracks under the carpet. One day someone will come with a proposal that would harm the ship, and the crew will say no — and the no will be enough. *Resilience in a time of pressures.*

**The woman of fifty.** She hands over the keys. Those who grew up with the ship do not take the carpenter as an authority. They may rewrite what he wrote, split the ship in two if they disagree, and no one may tell them that they thereby betrayed the story. *Intergenerational handing over.*

**The old woman.** She sits by a fire she did not light and listens to people speaking of verifiable accounting, of a helper without a key, of a bucket with three witnesses — and they do not say the word *ZION* while doing so, because they no longer need it. It does not trouble her. *Common infrastructure.*

**And then the lantern in hands Lumi does not know.** The light in it burns on. What today looks exceptional — public proof, the right to refuse, transparent care, knowledge handed on, a repairable machine — is ordinary. No one writes books about it. *The quiet completion.*

The lantern returned to Lumi. She was silent for a long time.

*"So I will not receive it? I only carry it part of the way?"*

*"You will receive it,"* said the carpenter. *"But not as an inheritance. As work. An inheritance can be lost. Work can only be done, or not done."*

---

Sadhu added one more thing, the thing said in this camp to every young person who came near the fire. Not as a condition. As a right:

*"You have the right to verify every word before you join in. You have the right to refuse — a coin, a vote, a story — when you do not believe them. You have the right to build where you live, without the permission of someone far away. You have the right to privacy, and to have someone tell you understandably what you are consenting to. And you have the right to criticise the ship without anyone telling you that you are its enemy. If anyone denies you any of that, it is not this ship — whatever it may be called."*

---

The lantern showed today.

The light narrowed and Lumi saw exactly where the ship is in September 2026. The thirty-day voyage without a stop is under way — it began on the twenty-third of August and ends on the twenty-second of September, and until then it must not be said to be complete. The independent inspection of the ship by foreign eyes has not yet begun. The drill of how to build the ship again from backups has been only half carried out. Nine gates at the end of the list: not one ticked off. On the keel is carved the name of the running ship, and *three three* is not on the keel — it is on the stone by the fire, as a list of work. And she saw that about the handing over of the keys to the next generation a paper has been written with years and role names — and that the paper is an intention, not a mechanism anyone has yet verified on the chain.

*"It is a list,"* said Lumi.

*"It is a list,"* said Only-that. *"And that is more than a dream. A dream has no lines that can be ticked off."*

## What it means

**3.3 "Nirvana" is a name for work, not for a finished thing — and the hundred-year compass is a scenario of handing over responsibility, not a prophecy.** The book takes over the Execution Charter and the Generational Compass of Mise Amenti and lets them pass through Lumi's hands so that one thing becomes visible: **the lantern is not inherited, the lantern is carried.**

1. **What 3.3 is.** A planning and integration designation. The actual version of the product, the binary or the chain is taken exclusively from the release, `StatusV3.md`, the `internal repository` and the code. No page may mark 3.3 as complete until evidence exists for each of the nine gates: R1 truth (release tag, changelog, tests, compatibility), R2 L1 safety, R3 L2 settlement, R4 ZIS, R5 L3 autonomy, R6 L4 culture, R7 L5/L6 responsibility, R8 decentralisation, R9 communication.
2. **Eight works.** M0 register of evidence and a stable foundation → M1 identity and settlement → M2 Maestro in a sandbox → M3 the intent interface → M4 the OASIS gate → M5 from fund to documented impact → M6 science before great words → M7 decentralisation and handing over → M8 review. The basic rule of dependencies: no agent may control finance or production until M0, M1, M2 and the relevant parts of M8 are complete.
3. **The pole star of 3.3.** The version is ready when a person can: enter safely; own and verify their identity, data and means; transfer value only with visible risk and verified settlement; use AI as a helper without hidden permissions; create and meet in OASIS without manipulation; see where the common share goes and verify the impact; **take the project over into the next generation, even if today's team and server vanish tomorrow.**
4. **Six epochs — a scenario, not a prophecy.** I. Anchoring and evidence (2026–2030). II. Federation and local responsibility (2031–2040). III. Resilience in a time of pressures (2041–2050). IV. Intergenerational handing over (2051–2075). V. Common infrastructure (2076–2100). VI. The quiet completion of Mise Amenti (2101–2126). The measure of success of the last epoch is deliberately uncomfortable: *if no one needs the ZION brand because the principles live everywhere, that is success, not defeat.*
5. **What should be measured — and what should not.** Resilience (recovery after an incident, independent nodes, verified backups), freedom (portability of keys and data, easy exit), safety (bugs found, time to repair, published limits), L5 impact (audited budgets, independent verification), L6 science (reproducibility, negative results), AI (correctly refused actions, human override), culture (accessibility, translations, open criticism). Not: marketing uptime, engagement length, the number of "autonomous" actions, pretty photographs, viral hypotheses.
6. **The rights of Generation Z are everyone's rights.** To verify before I join in; to refuse; to build locally; privacy and understandable consent; to criticise without the label of enemy. If that cannot be done in practice, Mise Amenti is not credible, however beautifully it sounds.
