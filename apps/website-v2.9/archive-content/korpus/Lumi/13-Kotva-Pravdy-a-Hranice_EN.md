# LUMI — Chapter 13: The anchor of truth and the boundary
## The register of the Book of the Lantern — canonical identifications, a summary of L1–L6, known discrepancies in three books, what this book is not, a proposal for change

> *"Do not demand faith where evidence can be offered. Do not sell a horizon as the present. When the vision collides with a fact, repair the vision — never the fact."* — The first vow of Mise Amenti

## The Story

The last tent of the camp was the largest and the quietest. No pictures hung on its wall. One table hung there, and above it an inscription: *"This is where we come when the story collides with a fact."*

Only-that kept the records there. When Lumi entered, he did not even raise his head.

*"Your book has thirteen chapters,"* he said. *"Every image in it has its own line. When someone comes and says *this is not true*, we will not argue. We will go to the line, read the anchor and repair what needs repairing. The story — not the fact."*

*"And when it is me that needs repairing?"*

*"Then we repair you,"* said Only-that, and looked at her for the first time. *"You are a story too, girl. The best one we have. But a story."*

Lumi nodded and laid the lantern on the table beside the table. The light neither narrowed nor widened. Here there was nothing to show — here things were written down.

### §4 What this book is not

1. **It is not a religion.** The carpenter, Sita, Hanuman, Sadhu, the voice Amitabha and the meaning of the name Elizabeth are not theology. The book claims no spiritual authority or lineage; it cites cultural traditions (Christian, Hebrew, Hindu, Buddhist, Polynesian, Czech) with respect, and their public adaptation requires a cultural review (class E).
2. **It is not a financial promise.** Nowhere does it state a price, a return, an APR or "why start early" as a promise of profit. "Why start early" is a description of the emission plan with an express reservation (ch. 12).
3. **It is not a promise of flight or of impact.** The ring above the Earth, the blossoming gardens and the domes are images of a possible future. Without a module there is no station; without an impact packet there is no impact.
4. **It is not a biography.** Neither Lumi nor the guides are real persons. The book does not tell the private stories of the authors or of their close ones.
5. **It is not a replacement for the registers.** Where it differs from `StatusV3.md`, `MiseAmenti/07`, `BodhiGaia/09` or `Issobella/10`, those prevail.
6. **It is not OASIS game content.** Avatar 40 and the world ELIZABET exist in the data; "Lumi as the childhood form of Elizabeth" is a proposal of the story. Adaptation into OASIS is HORIZON: without a duplicate identity, without token rewards for the story, without collection of personal data, with the option to skip sensitive parts (`MiseAmenti/04` M4, `05`).
