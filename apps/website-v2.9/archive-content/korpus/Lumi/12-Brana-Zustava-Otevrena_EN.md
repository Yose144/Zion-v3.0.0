# LUMI — Chapter 12: The gate remains open
## Onboarding — a return to the gate, four doors for a newcomer, what no one may ask of them, and an empty page

> *"Home is not the last world on the map. It is something we take care of together."*

---

## The Story

Lumi returned to the gate.

It was the same place as at the beginning — a pillar with an ornament, a small circle of worn grass, Radha with salt and honey. But Lumi saw it differently. Before, she had looked through the gate at the worlds beyond it. Now she looked at the people coming to it.

One of them stopped a little before the pillar and went no further. Young, with a backpack, hands in pockets. He looked at the gate as though it might bite him.

Lumi went over to him. She did not take him by the hand. She sat down on the ground beside the path so that he would have to look down at her and not up, and waited until he asked himself.

*"What is behind it?"*

*"It depends which door you go through,"* said Lumi. *"There are four. You need not take any."*

---

She showed them to him one by one, as the carpenter had once shown them to her — not what to do, but what is.

*"The first door is for the one who wants proof first. Behind it there is nothing but a window: you watch the running network, leaf through code in which nothing is hidden, and ask people anything. A good crew can say *I don't know* and show the source. It takes two minutes and no one wants anything from you."*

*"The second door is for the one who wants to experience it. Behind it is this gate from the inside — a flight through mist, a galaxy, a book of avatars, a market. But I will tell you straight what everyone who stands here honestly will tell you: it is a gate that is being built. Content may change, progress may vanish. You are not a customer of a finished game. You are a co-builder of a garden."*

*"The third door is for the one who wants to put their machine to work. Behind it is work: first you make your own bundle — twelve words you will never give anyone — then you download or build the hammer and start striking. Conservatively. You watch what the network accepts and how your machine warms up. Someone will tell you that the first decade carries the highest reward per block and that in a small network you will learn more than when the crowd arrives. That is true of the plan you can read in the code. It is not a promise of how much you will earn — that depends on your machine, on others, on costs and on a price no one knows."*

*"And the fourth door is for the one who wants to carry a piece of the bridge. Behind it nothing is finished — a missing test, a bug someone overlooked, a page to be translated, an image, music, a skill you already have. You bring it, or you don't."*

The young man looked at the four doors Lumi had drawn in the dust with her finger.

*"And what will they want from me?"*

*"That is the one thing I will tell you urgently,"* said Lumi, and stood up for the first time. *"No one — ever — may ask you for your twelve words. No support, no administrator, no prize. No one may ask you to believe a story, to pay for entry, to say more about yourself than you wish. No one from the ship will write to you first with an offer of how to get rich. If someone does, they are not from the ship — whatever they call themselves."*

*"And if I lose those twelve words?"*

*"Then you have lost what was in the bundle. No one will give it back to you. Not even I. That is not a punishment. It is the price of a door without a lock."*

---

The young man stood up, dusted off his knees and took a step towards the gate. He stopped on the threshold.

Lumi did not help him inside. She helped him **across the threshold** — only as much as was needed for him to stand on the other side and be able to decide for himself whether to go on or to return. Radha smiled and scattered the salt into the grass. The world went on with its ordinary life: someone carried water, someone repaired a fence, someone laughed at something no one else would understand.

---

The lantern showed today — for the last time.

The light narrowed and Lumi saw exactly what a newcomer of 2026 can actually do: open a window at `zionterranova.com` and watch the live network; leaf through the code at `github.com/Zion-TerraNova/v3-Mainnet`; enter the gate at `oasis.zionterranova.com` and walk the market at `market.zionterranova.com`; or make their own wallet and start the hammer:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Zion-TerraNova/v3-Mainnet.git
cd v3-Mainnet/V31
cargo build --release --bin zion-miner
./target/release/zion-miner \
  --pool pool.zionterranova.com:8444 \
  --wallet zion1...your_address \
  --worker my-first-machine
```

She saw that the official doors have one name — `zionterranova.com` and its subdomains — and that binaries are verified against the sums from the release, not according to who sends them. And she saw that the gate today really is only a preview under construction, with content that may change, and that this is written there in black and white so that no one has to find it out for themselves.

---

Then Lumi laid the lantern on the ground beside a small book she had carried in her pocket the whole way. The book had twelve written pages: the ark, the house, the bridge, the cave, the gate, the garden, five gardens, the ridge, the ring, the tower, the fire with four people and — this one. The last page was empty.

Elizabeth stood beside her. She did not ask. Lumi said it herself, for everyone who will ever come to the gate:

*"I cannot write this page for you."*

And Elizabeth, priestess of the future, asked as she asks everyone who passes — not as an examination, but as an invitation:

*"What will remain of you when you go on?"*

No one answered. It was not needed. The gate remained open.

## What it means

**The book ends exactly where the canonical onboarding begins — and that is deliberate.** Twelve chapters have passed through the ark, the house, the bridge, the cave, the gate, the gardens and the sky, so that the reader sees the whole ship. But the whole ship cannot be taken in hand. What can be taken in hand is one of the four paths from `ZION_ONBOARDING_PUBLIC_CZ.md`. Lumi shows the doors; she does not open them for anyone.

1. **Four paths, none obligatory.** Observer (2 minutes: web, code, questions), player (5 minutes: OASIS preview, market), miner (15 minutes: own wallet, `zion-miner`, a conservative start), builder (a test, an issue, a translation, a skill). The exact procedure is in the onboarding; the book does not rewrite it, it only points to it.
2. **What no one may ask of you.** The mnemonic and private keys never to anyone — no support, admin or "prize" needs them. A lost key = lost ZION, no reset exists. The official domains are `zionterranova.com` and its subdomains; the official code `github.com/Zion-TerraNova/v3-Mainnet`; binaries are verified against SHA256SUMS from the release. No one from the project contacts you first with an investment offer.
3. **What ZION does not promise.** No getting rich overnight. No price guarantee. No finished AAA game. No passive income without work. No religious claim — the figures are archetypes, not theology.
4. **"Why start early" is not a promise.** The emission schedule gives the first decade the highest reward per block, and a small network is a good place to learn a wallet, a rig and security. The actual return depends on hashrate, difficulty, costs and market price — and that appears nowhere in this book.
5. **The gate is not an examination.** Entry into the network has no judgement, citizenship or faith. Entry into the physical communities of L5 is according to their framework also non-KYC (four gates, children under 18 free) — and even that framework is a horizon today. No one may be marked as lesser for passing differently, or not passing at all.
6. **The empty page is the only true answer to Elizabeth's question.** The book will not fill it in. That is the whole difference between a story and a recruitment.
