# LUMI — Chapter 10: The quantum fire and the watcher
## Draha · Only-that · Strizh · L6 — three stones and their burial, a trickle that flows by itself, a vessel locked into time, and a guardian who cannot open the gate

> *"Whoever does not write down the burial of their hypothesis is selling hope, not science."*

---

## The Story

Down below the ridge, where the last harbour lights ended and the path upwards began, stood the tents of the printers of truth.

No pictures of ships hung in them. Tables hung there: *what we know, what we do not know, what we would know if it turned out to be true, and what it would cost if it turned out to be false.* The circle was led by a woman named Draha. When Lumi entered, she was just laying three stones on the table.

*"The first stone: thrust without exhaust. The old physics says a ship moves by throwing something backwards. New ideas say — perhaps not always. We do not know. But it can be measured, and if it turns out not to be so, the ash will tell us."*

*"The second stone: energy from the void. The void is not empty; something stirs in it. Whether anything can be wrung out of it, we do not know. But we may ask — as long as we admit the answer may be no."*

*"The third stone: metrics. A physicist once wrote an equation according to which space contracts before the ship and expands behind it — the ship flies without flying. Mathematically beautiful. In practice it needs things that may not exist. We write it as an open question, not as a promise."*

Lumi looked at the stones. *"And if all three fall?"*

*"Then we have learned to count well,"* said Draha. *"That is not a lost fire. That is a fire that warmed those who held it."*

Beside each stone lay a slip of paper. Lumi read it: on each was written **what would refute that stone**. *"We call that a burial,"* said Draha. *"Every hypothesis is written down together with its burial. Whoever does not do that is not selling science. They are selling hope dressed up as certainty."*

*"And weapons?"* asked Lumi, because she had heard that fire can also be thrown.

*"None. Safety gates are not a brake — they are the helm. An experiment that is afraid of the question *and what if something goes wrong?* is not science. It is a bet."*

---

A tent further on sat a teacher they called Only-that, because he always said "only that" first and the answer only afterwards. Before him he had two little boxes — one light and open, the other heavy and locked.

*"This is the trickle,"* he said, pointing at the light one. *"Out of every block of bread, five parts in a hundred go upwards — into the well of the sky. No one decides that. The protocol did it itself, at the birth. As long as there is mining, it flows. Like rain."*

*"And this?"*

*"Only that this is different. When the network was born, it set aside a great vessel for the sky — two and a half billion. And it locked it. Not with a person's key. With the key of time. It opens only when the chain reaches the height written in the code — one hundred and forty-four thousand notches. Until then no one may touch it, not with a good song, not with a better intention."*

*"And when it is unlocked?"*

*"Then a path follows: proposal, vote, waiting, the guardians' signatures — and a record that anyone can check. Money that flows upwards does not flow into a pocket. It flows into a question: **what deserves to fly?**"*

---

At the very edge, where the path upwards began, stood a tower. Not large. Only tall enough that from it the whole path could be seen along which the stream headed for the sky.

In it lived a guardian named Strizh. He hardly moved and nothing escaped him. His work was strange: **he must not open anything, close anything, or send anything onward.** He might only watch and count.

*"If I could open the treasury,"* he said to Lumi when she had climbed up, *"one weak morning would be enough and the whole well would be gone. This way you can catch me by the hand — and you will find on me only a list of how much water flowed through. I cannot lie, because I have nothing to lie with."*

*"And if something goes wrong?"*

*"Then I write that it went wrong. That is my whole power. And it is greater than it looks: a guardian who cannot steal cannot be bribed. A guardian who only reads cannot pass off a wish as a fact."*

Lumi looked from the tower up to where the path led. *"Would you not like to fly one day?"*

*"Me? No. I keep watch over the honesty of the path from here, from the ground. When you fly, I will be counting your fuel."*

---

The lantern showed today.

The light narrowed and Lumi saw exactly as much as there actually is in 2026. The light box: out of every block, every minute, five parts in a hundred rise to one particular place — and they accumulate there, because no one has yet paid out a single unit from them. The heavy box: locked, two and a half billion, with the number *one hundred and forty-four thousand* engraved on the lock, and the chain had on that day somewhat over forty-three thousand notches — most of the journey to the key of time still remained. The tower: it stood and the guardian in it really was counting, only for those who ask from inside; the window for passers-by had no glass yet. The stones: three, on the table, with their slips — and none of them had yet been tried in a laboratory; no engine, no demonstrator, only a roadmap on the web headed *vision*. And nowhere anything that could be thrown.

*"So the well is full and no one drinks from it,"* said Lumi.

*"For now,"* said Strizh. *"And every day I write down that it is so. When that changes, I will write that down too. It is the only thing I can do. And it is enough."*

## What it means

**L6 boasts of what it cannot do — and that is the strongest thing the book says about the sky.** The Book of the Sky divided the fire (ch. 2), the fund (ch. 6) and the watcher (ch. 7) into three chapters; this book assembles them into a single walk from the tents to the tower, because they belong together: **a hypothesis with a burial, a stream without an administrator, a guardian without a key.**

1. **The three stones are hypotheses, not a programme.** Reactionless thrust, vacuum energy, warp metrics (Alcubierre and variants such as "Ekam") — three research axes for which it is written in advance how we will know they lead into a wall. Workstream M6: every proposal contains a hypothesis, literature, methodology, data, budget, conflict of interest and a **falsification criterion**; negative results have the same visibility as successes. It must never be claimed commercially that ZION has solved FTL, antigravity or energy from nothing.
2. **Safety gates are the helm.** No part of L6 research is intended for armament. Whoever is afraid of the question "and what if something goes wrong" is not doing science.
3. **Two streams, two natures.** *The trickle*: 5 % of every block to the canonical L6 address, automatically, without a vote. *The vessel*: premine slot 6 — 2.5 billion ZION, locked until block 144 000, after unlocking an admin multisig and a DAO vote. The two addresses are different and the book does not braid them together.
4. **The well is full and no one drinks from it — and that is right.** Decision G10 holds L6 in *read-only* mode: the fund accumulates, every expenditure must pass along the path proposal → vote → waiting → the guardians' signatures, and that path today awaits a missing interface. Whoever says that Issobella "pays scientists" is lying. Whoever says that means are accumulating for the question *what deserves to fly* is telling the truth.
5. **Strizh is the protocol's second eyes.** The first eyes are the chain — immutable, dull to deception, incorruptible. The second eyes are the service that reads the chain and translates it to people. It has no key, and therefore it can faithfully keep watch.
