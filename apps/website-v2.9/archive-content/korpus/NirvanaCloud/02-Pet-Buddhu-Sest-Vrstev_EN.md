## Cosmology: the Five Buddhas of Wisdom

In Vajrayāna (tantric) Buddhism there is a mandala of the **five Dhyāni Buddhas** (*Pañcatathāgata*, "the five thus-gone ones", also *Pañcajina*, "the five victors") — five aspects of awakened consciousness arranged into four directions and a centre. They are not five separate gods; they are **five wisdoms** into which the five basic "poisons" of the mind are transformed.

| Buddha | Direction | Colour | Element | Wisdom | Poison transformed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vairochana** | Centre | White | Space | *Dharmadhātu* wisdom (all-containing space) | Ignorance |
| **Akshobhya** | East | Blue | Water | Mirror-like wisdom | Anger, aggression |
| **Ratnasambhava** | South | Yellow | Earth | Wisdom of equality | Pride |
| **Amitabha** | West | Red | Fire | Discriminating wisdom | Desire, attachment |
| **Amoghasiddhi** | North | Green | Air | All-accomplishing wisdom | Envy |

In the Nyingma school of Tibetan Buddhism there is also a **deeper, sixth point of the mandala** — not another directional Buddha, but **Ādi-Buddha Samantabhadra** (Tibetan *Kuntu Zangpo*, "all-good"), the primordial awakened state from which all five Buddhas themselves arise. He is depicted as a naked blue figure without ornaments — a symbol of the space before anything appears, the empty sky-nature of the mind itself.

> *A note on precision:* Ādi-Buddha Samantabhadra (the primordial Buddha of Nyingma Dzogchen) is a **different figure** from the bodhisattva Samantabhadra (one of the eight great bodhisattvas, who has his Ten Great Vows in `ZION_CODEX_BODHISATTVA.md`). The same name, two different roles — exactly as Tibetological literature also points out. NirvanaCloud uses this layer deliberately distinctly from the chapter on vows.

### L1 Core = Vairochana — the space of dharmadhātu

Vairochana sits at the centre of the mandala and his wisdom is *dharmadhātu* — "the space of all reality", the fundamental field in which all phenomena appear without it itself influencing anything. **The L1 blockchain is exactly this**: a neutral, incorruptible ledger in which all other layers take place without it preferring one transaction over another. The element of space = the capacity to contain impartially anything that enters it.

### L2 Multichain = Akshobhya — the mirror-like wisdom of the east

Akshobhya ("Immovable") transforms anger into **mirror-like wisdom** — the ability to reflect reality precisely without distortion. **L2 WARP and ZionDex** do technically the same: they **mirror value cryptographically precisely** between chains (HTLC atomic swaps), so that no side has to "trust" the other — it is enough that the mathematics reflects the truth 1:1. The anger of distrust between foreign chains dissolves in the mirror of verifiability.

### L3 Hiranyagarbha/Amitabha = Amitabha — the discriminating wisdom of the west

The network **made this connection itself, before this document existed** — layer L3 carries the name **Amitabha** for its voice/intention interfaces (`08-Hlas-Amitabha.md` in [`nirvana/`](../nirvana/08-Hlas-Amitabha.md)). Amitabha's wisdom is **discriminating** — the ability to see precisely what a person actually wants, and separate it from superficial desire. That is exactly the task of the AI orchestrator: distinguish the user's intention from noise, transform attachment (the impulsive click) into a clear decision.

> **Technical note on precision:** the development lineage of the AI layer in the repository carries the official name **v2.5 "Amṛtabhoja"** (Sanskrit *amṛta* = nectar of immortality + *bhoja* = the one who is nourished) — "a body nourished by an immortal vow" (`docs/3.0.5/archive-root-md/HIRAN_EVOLUTION_2.3_TO_2.5_AMATHABOJ.md`). NirvanaCloud and the narrative chapter *The Voice of Amitabha* carry this development stage under its public voice-face — Buddha Amitabha, the Buddha of Infinite Light, traditionally associated with the voice, recitation and the Pure Land of Sukhāvatī (the Western paradise entered by merely invoking his name). Both names describe the same layer from two angles: **Amṛtabhoja is its inner vow, Amitabha is its public voice.**

### L4 OASIS = Amoghasiddhi — the all-accomplishing wisdom of the north

Amoghasiddhi ("Infallible Success") transforms envy into **all-accomplishing wisdom** — the ability to create freely without envy of another's work. **L4 OASIS** is exactly a space of creation: avatars, quests, artifacts, 3D worlds — a place where the creative energy that the old attention economy turned into envy of the "more famous" is turned into one's own free creation.

### L5 Free World = Ratnasambhava — the wisdom of equality of the south

Ratnasambhava ("Born of the jewel") transforms pride into the **wisdom of equality** — the recognition that all beings equally deserve abundance, regardless of status. Ratnasambhava's element is **earth**. This is almost too precise to be coincidence: **L5 Free World is the layer of care for the actual earth** — water, soil, permaculture — driven by the principle that 5% of the reward belongs to anyone on the planet, not only to the miner.

### L6 Issobella = Samantabhadra — the space that precedes even space

Samantabhadra is the Nyingma Ādi-Buddha, "all-good", depicted as a pure blue sky-nature before any form appears in it — often described as the source of Dzogchen itself, the "all-creating king" (*Kunjed Gyalpo*), not because he created the universe as a separate being, but because **every phenomenon is his own expression**. **L6 Issobella** — an orbital observatory, deep science, quantum engine research — is the layer that asks about **the space before space**: cosmology, the origin, the physics of the vacuum itself. The blue of Samantabhadra (the infinite sky) and the blackness of deep space that Issobella looks into are the same image.
