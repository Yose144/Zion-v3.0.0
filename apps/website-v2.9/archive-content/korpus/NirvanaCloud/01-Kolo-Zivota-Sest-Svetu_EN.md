## Cosmology: the Bhava-chakra

**The Bhava-chakra** ("wheel of becoming", Tibetan *srid pa'i 'khor lo*) is one of the oldest and most widespread images of Buddhism — painted on the outer walls of Tibetan monasteries precisely so that it could be seen even by those who will never step inside. According to the *Mūlasarvāstivāda-vinaya*, the Buddha wanted one at every entrance, with a resident monk to explain it to pilgrims.

The wheel is held in the jaws of **Yama, the Lord of Death** — a reminder that everything on the wheel is subject to impermanence. At the centre are three animals biting each other's tails — **a pig (ignorance), a snake (aversion/anger) and a rooster (attachment/desire)** — the three poisons that drive the whole wheel. Around them are six rays — six worlds into which a being is repeatedly reborn according to karma:

| World | Sanskrit | Characteristic | Dominant poison |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Devas** | *deva* | Wealth, power, long life, pleasure — but blindness to the suffering of others | Pride, complacency |
| **Asuras** | *asura* | Strength, ambition, rivalry, envy of the devas | Envy, hatred of others' success |
| **Humans** | *manuṣya* | The rare balance of suffering and joy, **the only world where awakening is easily attainable** | All poisons in a mild, workable measure |
| **Animals** | *tiryañc* | Instinct, survival, no reflection | Ignorance |
| **Hungry ghosts** | *preta* | Huge empty bellies, tiny throats — endless craving, the impossibility of being satisfied | Attachment, addiction |
| **Hells** | *naraka* | Extreme suffering, violence, war | Anger, aggression |

The classical reading stresses: **these worlds are not only post-mortem places.** They are **states of mind** that a being passes through even within a single day — and they are also **collective states of society** into which whole civilizations fall when one poison begins to rule them.

## What this means for ZION

`NirvanaCloud` reads the Bhava-chakra as a **diagnostic map of what is broken in Babylon — and of what the ZION protocol builds against it.** This reading is deliberately two-layered: first inner (psychological), then civilizational (systemic).

### The world of the Devas — the pride of centralized power

**Inwardly:** the illusion that success and wealth are permanent and deserved without obligation to others.
**Civilizationally:** central banks that print currency without the consent of those who use it; platforms that claim users' data as their own property; corporations "too big to fail".
**The antidote in the protocol:** **transparent, algorithm-based emission** (LWMA-60, hard cap of 144 billion ZION) — no one, not even the founders, can print a single coin outside consensus. The power of the devas dissolves when the rules cannot be bent in favour of one.

### The world of the Asuras — envy and endless competition

**Inwardly:** the feeling that another's happiness is my lack; the need to win over someone, not with someone.
**Civilizationally:** resource wars, patent wars, walled gardens of technology giants where innovation serves to block competitors rather than benefit people.
**The antidote in the protocol:** the **open-source MIT licence** and the **interoperable L2 multichain** (`WARP`, `ZionDex`) — value can flow between chains without anyone having to "lose" for another to "win". Competition shifts from a struggle for control to a competition of contribution.

### The world of hungry ghosts — the endless economy of scarcity

**Inwardly:** the *scarcity mindset* — the feeling that there will never be enough, no matter how much is accumulated.
**Civilizationally:** the **attention economy** — social networks designed to provoke a dopamine hunger that can never be satisfied, because satisfaction would mean the end of advertising profit. This is perhaps **the most accurate description of a hungry ghost humanity has ever built**: the enormous "belly" of the attention industry, the tiny "throat" of real meaning that can never get through it.
**The antidote in the protocol:** the **automatic 5+5% humanitarian and scientific flow** (L5 Free World, L6 Issobella) — the value the network creates is *structurally forced* to flow outward, not to pile up in one empty belly. A miner cannot "eat" 100% of the reward even if they wanted to.

### The world of animals — algorithmic instinct without reflection

**Inwardly:** acting from pure reflex, with no room for the question "why am I doing this".
**Civilizationally:** recommendation algorithms that know more about the user than they know about themselves, feeding them content exactly at the edge of their impulsive reaction — with no attempt at their growth.
**The antidote in the protocol:** the **Dharma Validator** at the core of L3 Hiranyagarbha/Amitabha (`hiranyagarbha.rs`) — a compile-time enforced ethical check (Ahimsa, Satya, Asteya, Brahmacharya, Aparigraha) *before* every AI output, not only after the harm.

### The hells — exploitation, war, debt slavery

**Inwardly:** a state where a person no longer sees any way out, only survival in pain.
**Civilizationally:** debt slavery (microloans at usurious interest), conflict minerals, forced labour in the supply chains of old technologies.
**The antidote in the protocol:** **no debt at the core of the protocol** — ZION has no interest rate, no central lenders, no margin calls. A coinbase transaction is an act of creation, not an act of credit.

### The human world — a rare opportunity

The Buddhist tradition stresses that **human birth is the rarest and most precious of all six** — not because humans are superior, but because only in the human world is there **enough suffering to motivate awakening, and enough relief for strength to remain for it**. The devas are too content to try; the inhabitants of the hells are too flooded with pain.

> **This is exactly why ZION targets ordinary people, not the devas of Wall Street nor the victims of the hells of extreme poverty without internet access.** It is a network for the one who has a phone, a little time and a question — precisely that rare human position on the wheel.
