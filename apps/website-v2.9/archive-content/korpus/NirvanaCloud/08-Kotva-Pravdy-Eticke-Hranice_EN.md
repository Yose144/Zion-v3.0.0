# NirvanaCloud — Chapter 8: The Anchor of Truth and Ethical Boundaries
## What NirvanaCloud is, what it is not, whom it does not belong to, and where its limits are

> *"A story may open a door. It must not close it behind a person."*
> — `08-ZION-Nova-Civilizace.md`

---

This chapter gathers in one place the ethical guardrails that otherwise appear scattered across the whole `NirvanaCloud`, `nirvana/` and `SulZeme/` series. It is a **binding framework for reading the entire series**, not merely an appendix.

---

## 1. What NirvanaCloud is NOT

- **It is not a religious claim.** Tibetan Buddhism, the hermetic mythology of the Halls of Amenti, Christian, Hindu and other archetypes used in this and previous series (`The Salt of this Earth`, `nirvana/`) are **literary and philosophical inspirations**, not a theological assertion. ZION has no spiritual authority and pretends to no lineage, initiation or any form of spiritual succession from any tradition.
- **It is not academic history.** The Emerald Tablets of Thoth ([Chapter 5](./05-Sine-Amenti-Dokonceni-Mise.md)) are a modern esoteric text, not a recognized Egyptological source — this is stated explicitly in the text and must remain so in every further expansion.
- **It is not a financial promise.** No episode, chapter or manifesto in this series says "invest and get rich", nor predicts the price, yield or market capitalization of ZION over any time horizon — including the hundred-year plan in [Chapter 7](./07-Stoleti-Plan-2026-2126.md).
- **It is not a substitute for professional help.** [Chapter 6](./06-Generace-Z-Manifest.md) states explicitly that for mental-health difficulties one should seek a qualified professional — not a protocol, not a community, not a mythology.
- **It is not a claim to cultural authority.** The use of Tibetan Buddhist concepts (Bhava-chakra, Bardo Thödol, Kalachakra/Shambhala, the mandala of the five Buddhas) **pretends to no initiation, lama lineage or authorized transmission** — exactly as `docs/TerraNova/BASE_FINAL/geography/asia/tibet/02-VAJRAYANA-DIAMANTOVA-CESTA.md` warned earlier: *"Real tantric practice requires a lama with authentic transmission, initiation, years of preparatory practice and bodhicitta as the foundation... if someone says 'I'll teach you tantra in a weekend' — run."* NirvanaCloud **teaches nothing to anyone** in the spiritual sense — it merely **borrows a map of states** from the tradition as inspiration for writing about technology.
- **It is not a political position** on Tibetan sovereignty, religious freedom in any country, or any current geopolitical conflict.

---

## 2. What NirvanaCloud IS

- **A literary and architectural inspiration.** In the same way `The Salt of this Earth` uses Jesus, the Buddha and Krishna as archetypes of trust, `NirvanaCloud` uses the Bhava-chakra, the Bardo Thödol, the Kalachakra and the Halls of Amenti as **maps of states and structures** that help show why the network is built precisely this way.
- **A tool for long-term thinking.** The hundred-year plan ([Chapter 7](./07-Stoleti-Plan-2026-2126.md)) is an exercise in thinking beyond the horizon of one human career or one election cycle, without pretending to certainty.
- **An invitation to verification, not to belief.** Every chapter contains an "Anchor of Truth" — a box separating the mythological image from a verifiable fact on the running network.

---

## 3. Financial boundaries

- ZION, like any other cryptocurrency, **does not guarantee yield, protection against loss of value, or a future price.**
- Self-custody of private keys means both freedom and responsibility — a lost key may not be recoverable.
- If public communication about ZION ever becomes part of an offer of a crypto-asset in the European Union, it must be clearly identified as marketing, fair, clear and not misleading, in accordance with Article 7 of the **MiCA** (Markets in Crypto-Assets) regulation — the same requirement that `canon/books/marketing/ROAD_TO_ZION_CZ.md` already imposed on the entire Road to Zion series. `NirvanaCloud` is written as **an invitation to verification**, not as a solicitation to buy.

---

## 4. Cultural sensitivity and boundaries on the use of archetypes

- Mantras, mudras and mandalas mentioned in this series are used **exclusively in a pedagogical-metaphorical context**, never as game cosmetics, sound effects or a marketing symbol for sale.
- Kailash and other sacred mountains are not depicted in any part of the ZION ecosystem (including OASIS, see `WORLD_TIBET.md`) as "conquerable" challenges.
- The figure of the 14th Dalai Lama is mentioned only as a **public historical and ethical inspiration** (see `03-DALAJLAMA-A-SAMBHALA.md`), never as a physical person "portrayed" in game content without his consent.
- Whenever this series is expanded in the future with further elements of Himalayan, Egyptian or other traditions, the same rule as in `WORLD_TIBET.md` §11.3 applies: **recommended consultation with experts on that tradition** before any expansion into an interactive (game) format.

---

## 5. Generational and psychological sensitivity

- [Chapter 6](./06-Generace-Z-Manifest.md) is written as **an address, not a diagnosis.** Generation Z is not described in this series as a "problem" for ZION to solve, but as an active party with its own capacity to decide.
- The text avoids creating false hope as well as false apocalypse — climate, demographic and AI risks are described with reference to independent sources (WEF, Global Challenges Foundation, The Millennium Project), neither dramatized nor trivialized.
- No part of this series forces the reader into any action. The paths of observer, player and builder from `08-ZION-Nova-Civilizace.md` §VI remain equal.

---

## 6. Relation to the living code and the whitepaper

Whenever a mythological description in this series diverges from the current state of the code, the whitepaper, `StatusV3.md` or [`MiseAmenti/07-Registr-Dukazu.md`](../MiseAmenti/07-Registr-Dukazu.md), **the living code and the evidence registry always prevail.** Mythology is a layer of explanation, not a layer of truth about technical facts. If a reader finds a discrepancy, the correct procedure is to report it as an ordinary documentation bug (a GitHub issue), not as a theological dispute.

---

## 7. Feedback and correction

This series, like `The Salt of this Earth` and `nirvana/`, is a **living document**. If anyone — especially from the Tibetan, Himalayan, Egyptological or Buddhist studies community — points out an inaccuracy, a misleading simplification or a lack of respect for the tradition, the repository has an **explicit commitment to correct the text**, just as it corrects bugs in code (see `08-ZION-Nova-Civilizace.md` §V.4: *"Admit a mistake faster than someone else can find it."*).
