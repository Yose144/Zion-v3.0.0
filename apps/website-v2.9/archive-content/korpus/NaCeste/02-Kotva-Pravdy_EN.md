## Proposal according to MiseAmenti/08 §3

### Intent
To add to the corpus a standalone literary story that shows responsible uncertainty, listening and the right to change course — in one explicitly possible world that follows on from onboarding, MariaCaminho and Terra Nova.

### State of the claim
MYTH / HORIZON as a whole. No claim about the state of vessels, communities, grants, blessings, miracles or about specific persons. The future world is not a colony nor a single authority; communities may refuse a visit (in the story: Raiatea and Australia as a refused visit; the handover on the isthmus as a refused transport of seed without a permit, not a refusal of people). The design of hull III is not approved; the story does not specify it.

### Risks
- A reader might infer that the hulls, communities or meetings with hosts exist or are promised.
- The depiction of Raiatea, the Australian coast and Ekam without consultation with their hosts and guardians; the story therefore attributes speech or consent to none of them (no consent was used; FPIC is a boundary of the story, not decoration).
- The image of Lumi and the Marian names: archetypes, not claims about persons or blessings.
- The maritime emergency scene is not a guide; the decision belongs to a qualified captain.
- Confusing a continuation with a fifth book, or the projection of 2040 with reality.

### Boundaries
It is not a fifth book, an official canon, a financial or legal document, a promise of payouts, a schedule, nor a description of a real expedition. It does not use numbers from the historical Genesis/Quantum books. The pilgrim of MariaCaminho remains an archetype.

### Review (pending)
Pending: a factual and legal editor, a cultural consultant (pilgrim and Marian traditions, the Pacific, Australian custodians, Ekam), a review of the public copy. None of these has taken place yet.

### Rollback / corrective procedure
Corrections of discrepancies are recorded according to `MiseAmenti/08` §4 without rewriting and without deleting history.
