# Chapter 4 — The Keeper of the Circle
## Seat IV · the hearth — Golden Republic Bohemia; a woman who holds salt on the table and the law that the throne stays empty

> *"A circle is not founded by drawing it. It is founded by no one entering it as first."*

---

## The Story

In the middle of a land that has no sea there is a hearth that never prepared to wait for a sea.

Bohemia — the Golden Republic — is a world that needs no shore, because its centre holds a different kind of water: **salt on the table**. A table at which the village sits; salt that is not rubbed into a wound but into bread; and a circle held in such a way that no one sits at its head.

The woman who came from there laid two things on the circle's table: **a small glass of salt and a charter**. Salt — because in her land a feast does not begin with food but with the greeting "salt above gold" — and a charter — because in her land a circle is not held by good intention but by **a law that forbids a throne**.

*"I do not carry an administration,"* she said when she had laid both down. *"I carry the rule that administration must not become ownership. That is my whole office — to see that the circle never collapses into a chair."*

And those who knew her knew she was not speaking of herself — she was speaking of the **Golden Bull**, which in her land hangs above every table: the republic is not to be divided, given away or sold — held by a circle for as long as no one wants to own it; and when someone does, the law sounds from there again: **a circle without a throne.**

---

The Golden Republic in the corpus holds its place where you would expect a palace — and instead of a palace there stands **a table**. The seven laws of the republic are not read as a constitution — they are read as seven chairs at a table: each holds one side, and none reaches across another.

And she — the Keeper of the Circle — is the one who passes that table. Her work is not to decide; her work is to **hold the deciding in a circle**: who speaks, who listens, who holds the time, who holds the key — and above all: who must not decide alone.

*"In my land they say,"* she told, *"that salt makes gold taste. But I know that salt makes gold **suffer** — gold that someone carries on their head all the time goes mad. The salt each person takes with a finger teaches that ownership is a spice, not a dish."*

And therefore the circle she holds has a single rule written in capital letters: **whoever would sit on a throne leaves the circle.** Not because a throne would be ugly — because a throne is in a circle an error, not a function. A circle stands on the leg of every seat; a throne would stand on the leg of one — and then the circle would not be a circle but a chain.

---

The land she holds is in the revealed future the background of the circle — **a heart that does not look to the sea, because it holds the key heart of the territory**: Říp — the mountain to which the first lawgivers came to sit down so as to learn what should become of the land; and Prague — the city where for a hundred years circles of lawgivers have met who never sat on thrones but always at tables.

*"I do not hold a landscape,"* she said. *"I hold the rule that a landscape held by a circle must not be divided — it must be inherited. And an inheritance a circle does not hold shatters between fingers before another hand can hold it."*

---

The light narrowed.

The lantern showed Bohemia today: a constitution and seven laws **in draft**, not in force — documents with a date that await DAO ratification; the project sheet of Golden Republic Bohemia as HORIZON, not BUILDING. The salt on the table is LIVE as a metaphor — and as a metaphor it suffices.

*"It is small,"* said Lumi.

*"It is small,"* nodded the Keeper of the Circle. *"But salt is always small — and without it you cannot hold bread in your hand. A circle that wants to hold must not leave the salt in the wound."*

---

## What it means

**Golden Republic Bohemia is the place where 5 % of every block becomes the law of holding — a circle that has no throne, and salt that teaches gold to suffer.**

1. **A circle without a throne.** The corpus (`l2/dao/plany/ústava`, `Maitreya/14`) founds the Golden Republic on a single rule: not to divide, not to sell, not to own by one. The Keeper of the Circle holds this rule — she does not decide, she **sees that the decision remains a circle**.
2. **Salt as a function.** "Salt above gold" is a canonical greeting (the 7 beings, Onboarding) — the Keeper of the Circle carries it as a law: salt makes gold suffer (ownership as a spice, not a dish).
3. **A lawgiver, not a queen.** Her role is not representation — it is **an instrument of holding**: who may speak, who holds the time, who holds the key. In private law this corresponds to an administrative (not an ownership) position.
4. **Říp and Prague.** The canonical geography (the mountain one came to sit at; the city where lawgivers without thrones have met for a hundred years) is LIVE as a tradition; its use for the project is HORIZON.
