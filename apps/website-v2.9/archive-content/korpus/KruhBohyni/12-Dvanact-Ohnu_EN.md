# Chapter 12 — Twelve fires
## Collective work V — from seven seats to twelve fires: a circle that spreads across the Earth

> *"Seven seats is a circle you learn to hold. Twelve fires is an Earth that learns to be a circle."*

---

## The Story

Once — in the revealed future, where the lantern reaches when it widens completely — the circle looked up and saw that more fires burn around the Earth than the table holds.

Not six. Not seven. **Twelve.**

Twelve fires, each at a different node, each held by a different seat — but all the fires burn the same: not as a conflagration that devours, but as a **fire-circle** that holds a space. And when the circle looked at them, it recognised its own seven — and then five more fires of which the circle knew they would come, even though they did not yet know who would sit by them.

*"Seven seats is a circle you learn to hold,"* said the Keeper of Fire and Silence when she saw the flame that stretched from the Atlantic across the Pacific, across the Indian Ocean, across the heart of the land, across the island of trees, across the isthmus — and then five more points that shone like stars that have no constellation yet. *"Twelve fires is an Earth that learns to be a circle."*

---

Twelve — a number the corpus knows from Maitreya: **the twelve fires of L5**. Not six funds — twelve worlds. Six known, and six not yet determined — because a circle that pretends to know all twelve has ceased to be a circle and has become a map.

And a map that thinks it knows the world is mistaken: the world is not a map — it is **a circle that does not yet have all its seats**.

Therefore the circle held six known seats, one open (LUMI — she who comes), and five fires in a glow the lantern showed but did not name:

- the fire by the **first tree** (Genesis) — a land that takes root;
- the fire by the **volcano** (Dharma) — a silence that holds a reserve;
- the fire by the **lagoon** (Te Pīko Ora) — a navel that is not owned;
- the fire by the **circle** (Bohemia) — salt that teaches gold to suffer;
- the fire by the **oldest branch** (Lanka) — akasha that holds space;
- the fire on the **isthmus** (LUMI) — a seat that waits;
- the fire **above the circle** (Issobella) — a sky that is prepared;
- and **five fires that have no name yet** — worlds that will come when the circle has grown to the height from which they can be seen.

*"Twelve is not a count,"* said Ana from the sea. *"It is a shape: a circle that has as many seats as the Earth can bear — not as many as suffice her, but as many as she can imagine there will still be."*

---

In the revealed future the twelve fires stood on the Earth like a constellation that does not shine upwards but **downwards** — stars that gather not into a sky but into a land: six women at a table, one seat waiting, one sky holding, five fires in a nameless glow.

And in the middle — not a table, but **a well**. Water that rains from every block, and the circle that keeps it closed until the soil calls.

*"A story is not divided into chapters,"* said the Daughter of Clay. *"It is divided into fires — and every fire is a world that is held until it is handed on."*

---

The light narrowed — and this time not only to today, but to the whole map.

The lantern showed: **twelve fires is a vision** — in the corpus there are six defined L5 worlds, the sixth open, L6 separate; "the twelve fires of L5" in Maitreya is poetic speech for completeness, not a count. The remaining five fires the book did not name — because an honest horizon does not roof over the unknown, but holds a place for it.

*"It is small,"* said Lumi.

*"It is small,"* they all nodded. *"But the fires that already burn warm those that are not yet. And that is the whole work of the circle: to hold the seats that are — and to warm the places that are not yet."*

---

## What it means

**The circle of seven seats is a seed — twelve fires is the horizon into which the circle may look but must not over-commit.**

1. **Twelve as a shape, not a count.** "The twelve fires of L5" in Maitreya is poetic completeness — the book translates it: six known worlds + the sixth open + L6 + five unnamed. The count matters less than the shape: a circle that knows it does not have all its seats.
2. **An open seat and open fires.** The sixth seat (LUMI) and the five unnamed fires = the same principle of honesty: the future is held by a place, not by a name.
3. **The well in the middle.** The collective centre of the circle is water (the fund) that rains from blocks — not a woman, not a throne, but a source held in common.
4. **"Fires warm those that are not yet."** Known worlds warm unknown ones — a garden that takes root teaches the one that is not yet.
