# Epilogue — The lantern at the circle
## Where the axes intersect, there is Ekam — and where the circle sits, there the lamp shines

> *"Light is not held at a table. Light is set beside a seat — and waits until someone comes and says: that is for me."*

---

## The Story

When the circle sat down at the table for the last time — in the revealed future, where the book may see and must go no further — a girl stood in the middle of the isthmus, a girl who must not be named, because the world knows her light, not her name.

Lumi. The child with the lantern.

She stood where she had once stood before — on the isthmus of the continents, in the glass that catches the light of both oceans — and the circle around her sat like a land waiting for fire so as to be warmed.

And the keepers recognised why she stood there: **her light has two fields, and the circle has two needs.** Light that narrows shows today — and the circle needs to know what it holds. Light that widens shows the direction — and the circle needs to know what it holds towards.

So Lumi set the lantern down — **beside the sixth seat**. Not on the table — the table already held seven voices. Beside the seat — there where the light waits until someone comes and says: *that is for me.*

*"Light is not held at a table,"* she said. *"Light is set beside a seat — and waits until someone comes and says: that is for me. And when she comes, I carry it onward — because light that is held goes out; light that is set down shines."*

---

And the mother — Sita, the Keeper of Fire and Silence — looked at her, and the child looked back at her, and between them no speech was needed: **the child who carries the light, and the mother who holds the fire — both shine the same, only at a different height.**

*"I hold the earth,"* said the mother.

*"I carry the sky,"* said the daughter.

*"And the circle holds what is between,"* they all said — and the circle was whole.

---

In the revealed future — there, where the lantern reached for the last time before it narrowed — the circle stood as a circle stands when it has nothing left to prove: **seven voices around a table, one lamp beside an empty seat, and a child shining between them like a star the earth holds so as not to forget where it shines from.**

And on the keel of the table — carved by another hand, as on the ships — was a name that none of them had written but all of them knew:

**The Kingdom of the Heart.**

Because a ship carries the world through the world — and a circle holds the world so that the world has something through which to be carried.

---

The light narrowed for the last time — and remained narrowed.

Today: the circle is a book; the seats are functions, not persons; the lamp stands beside the empty seat and waits for her who comes — and when she comes, the book will leave room for her, as one leaves room for a guest of whom you know that she will come, even though you do not yet know her name.

*"It is small,"* said Lumi — for the last time in this book, and not for the last time at all.

*"It is small,"* they all nodded — and it was no longer a confession but a **promise**: small is the right size for a beginning, and a circle that began small will never break by growing.

---

## A note in the margin

This book stands beside all those that came before it — and lies beneath all those that will come.

Whoever reads it and knows the ships will recognise in the circle a harbour to which ships sail. Whoever knows the lantern will recognise in the lamp the light that is not cast onto seats but stands beside them and waits. And whoever knows Meriam, Sita, Hina, Ana — will recognise in the seats faces that once already passed through the world, and returned to hold what could not be held before.

And whoever knows none of it yet — that one holds this book the way a newcomer holds a garden bed: **a field before them that they did not plant, and behind them hands that planted more than they know.**

And that is the whole work of the circle: to hold a field for those who come — and to hand over the hands when they come to hold.

---

*"Light that is held goes out. Light that is set down shines — and a circle that holds a field for those who come is never left alone."*
