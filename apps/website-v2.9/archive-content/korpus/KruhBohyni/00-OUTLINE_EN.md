## The dramatic principle of the whole book

**The Fifth Book ended with the handing over of the seats.** This book begins with the assembly of the circle — and its only conflict is not "the evil world outside", but **the peaceful resistance of time**: the project is built for years, the well fills with blocks, and the woman who holds a seat must learn to wait better than Babylon can hurry.

Every chapter holds three planes:

1. **Revealed** — a world already in bloom: the circle sits, the gardens bear fruit. (MYTH / HORIZON)
2. **The lantern's tilt** — the light narrows: what of this place and this fund actually is today. (LIVE / BUILDING / INTENT)
3. **The anchor of truth** — a table at the end of the chapter.

**Forbidden moves:** no woman is "the leader of the circle"; no name; no date; no promise of performance; the circle never decides about keys — the chain and the DAO do.

## PROLOGUE — The table with no head

- **Situation:** the revealed future. A place that is all six places at once — a table at the crossroads of the world; seven chairs, one empty.
- **Arrivals (one page per woman):** each brings a sign of her world — a handful of clay, a coal in a shell, a navigator's knot, a circle with salt, a leaf of the oldest tree, a lamp for the empty seat, a mirror catching a star.
- **Climax:** a child with a lantern appears at the edge of the light and asks: *"Who rules here?"* The circle's answer is the closing sentence of the prologue and the theme of the whole book.
- **Anchor:** the whole prologue = MYTH; the only "facts" are the existence of the seven worlds in the canon (BodhiGaia 03–13, Issobella 11) — marked BUILDING/HORIZON.

## PART I — THE TABLE (ch. 1–2)

### 1. The Daughter of Clay (Genesis · earth)

- **Revealed:** the Atlantic slope, where she arrived first; the planting of the first tree as a rite — not an opening, but a *taking root*; a pilgrim who comes unannounced and receives both a bed and work.
- **The lantern's tilt:** today the garden is a documented community under negotiation — not a harvest; "the most revolutionary thing is to grow food for yourself and your neighbours" (Vandana Shiva, the motto of TerraNova).
- **The typical Pérá turn:** the Daughter of Clay is the second keeper in order — the one who goes wherever someone is missing (the substitute seat as a virtue: to hold until the other arrives — the seed of ch. 11).

### 2. The Keeper of Fire and Silence (Dharma · fire)

- **Revealed:** a woman carries a volcano's coal in a shell across the Atlantic; on La Palma she lays it in the place where seven domes and the Tree of Life will one day grow; little Buddhas learn silence by the fire.
- **A special duty:** by her own fire she also keeps **an extra well** — a reserve that is not hers; the circle entrusted her with water for everyone, because her calm can refrain from touching what does not belong to her.
- **The lantern's tilt:** the temple today = an architectural design + a web 3D concept (BUILDING); the L5 reserve (300M) = INTENT of allocation in a private instrument — in the book only "the extra well".
- **Theme:** silence as the qualification of a keeper — whoever can refrain from touching can hold.

## PART II — THE GARDENS (ch. 3–9)

### 3. The Daughter of the Lagoon (Te Pīko Ora · water)

- **Revealed:** a woman born by water who surfed before she walked — in the myth she reads the stars and the tide; wayfinding as consensus: the ship finds the shore when the crew serves the direction, not the helmsman.
- **The lesson of Rapa Nui:** the island that ate its own trees — why the circle plants a palm, not a statue.
- **The lantern's tilt:** the community is documented in the L5 docs (BUILDING); the physical node = HORIZON.
- **An echo of the surfer's truth:** a navigator does not know when the wave will come — she can only be ready when it does. Projects are not built by a deadline, but by waiting in shape.

### 4. The Keeper of the Circle (Bohemia · the hearth)

- **Revealed:** the heart of the land, where she arrives last — because a circle without a throne grows accustomed most slowly; salt on the table, the Golden Bull as a proto-DAO; Říp as the mountain where the council meets.
- **The lantern's tilt:** the project sheet (HORIZON); DAO governance LIVE (1 ZION = 1 vote, 5-of-7 guardian).
- **Theme:** the woman holds not land, but **the way of deciding** — the lawgiver of the circle: rules that no one sits on the head.

### 5. The Keeper of the Tree (Lanka · akasha)

- **Revealed:** a woman sits beneath a tree older than empires (Sri Maha Bodhi, 288 BCE — a LIVING tradition); akasha = the space that holds the other elements; bhakti as governance — love as a way of administration.
- **The lantern's tilt:** the project sheet of Bodhi Lanka (HORIZON); the Rama Setu bridge = a myth between islands.
- **Theme:** the oldest living tree in the world as proof that **a tree outlives an empire** — that is why the circle plants trees, not institutions.

### 6. The sixth seat (LUMI · the bridge)

- **The only chapter without a heroine:** the seat remains empty throughout the chapter. The place on the isthmus of the continents: a native elder speaks of FPIC — ask first, then build; the circle holds the seat open as Elizabeth holds the chair for Hanuman (Maitreya).
- **Revealed:** at the end of the chapter a figure appears in the distance by the water — approaching? The book does not answer. The seat glows with a lamp, not a crown.
- **The lantern's tilt:** LUMI = the intent of an allocation of 500M from the L5 reserve (INTENT); land/FPIC UNSUBSTANTIATED; the administrator undetermined — that is the living truth of the empty chair.
- **Theme:** some seats are held better empty than hastily occupied.

### 7. She Who Holds the Sky (L6 · sky)

- **Revealed:** the second daughter of the ocean — from the same water as the Lagoon, but her waves went upwards; she sits by the circle, but her world hangs higher: the ring above the Earth, a ground station on land shared with LUMI.
- **The lantern's tilt:** the L6 fund = LIVE (2.5 bn, timelock 144 000); the ground node in Costa Rica = HORIZON; the station = HORIZON.
- **Theme:** the seventh point of the circle is at the same time outside it — the sky is not sat upon, it is held. The relationship of the sisters: the Lagoon taught the Sky to read water; the Sky teaches the Lagoon to read stars.

### 8. The well that rains (the fund)

- **Parable:** the tithe is not a gift someone bestows — it is rain that falls by itself from every block: five in a hundred to the springs (L5), five to the stars (L6). The wells fill; no one from the circle opens them — the circle of elders (multisig) and the voice of the village (the DAO) open them.
- **The lantern's tilt:** the 89/5/5/1 consensus split = LIVE; read-only trackers = LIVE; payouts = gate G10, so far none.
- **Theme:** the circle gathered *before* the well opens — preparing hands is faster than preparing money.

### 9. Three keys and one voice (administration)

- **Parable:** the woman holds the project; the chain holds the keys; the signature is made by the circle of elders; the voice is given by the village. Maitreya's three keys (copper/work, jasper/patience, crystal/truth) return as the three duties of a keeper.
- **The lantern's tilt:** multisig + DAO voting LIVE; transfer of the admin = 3-of-3 + a 1-year timelock + DAO 51 %.
- **Theme:** distributed power as the only safe power — the inheritance of the Gen Z rules in a female line.

## PART III — RULES AND HANDING OVER (ch. 10–13)

### 10. How the circle holds (sociocracy)

- Seven questions for every project (from the BodhiGaia/06 protocols of the Earth); the vow of care *"I vow to care for this land as I would care for my own body"* before every key; impact only with three witnesses (the impact packet, M5).
- **Theme:** speed has a locomotive, the circle has a walk.

### 11. When a woman has walked her path (succession)

- The parable of the substitute keeper: a seat is not sold, inherited or won — it is taken over when a hand releases it; the Daughter of Clay from ch. 1 turns out to be the one who can go wherever someone is missing.
- **The lantern's tilt:** the dead man's switch = LIVE (5 years of inactivity → transfer); the substitute seat in a private instrument — in the book only a function.
- **Theme:** the circle outlives every one of its members — that is its only test.

### 12. Twelve fires (L5 as a whole)

- The circle of keepers as the hearth of the twelve fires of the New World (the canonical list from the Maitreya glossary); why six women suffice for six places — and no more is touched.
- **Theme:** the circle is not omnipotence; it is sufficiency.

### 13. The anchor of truth and the boundary (the register)

- The full table: every seat → world → state today → missing evidence; what the book is not (persons, law, religion, a promise); the relationship to `` `internal documentation` `` (structure yes, names no); a proposal of inclusion and review.

## EPILOGUE — The lantern at the circle

- The child with the lantern returns from the prologue: this time she sits on the edge of the table and asks again — and the circle answers not with a sentence but with a gesture: it sets before her the seventh piece of bread and lets the lamp on the empty seat burn.
- The closing image: **sign it yourself** — the circle remains open for every woman who withstands the test of care.
- The dedication prayer of the corpus: *Gate, Gate, Paragate, Parasamgate, Bodhi Svaha.*
