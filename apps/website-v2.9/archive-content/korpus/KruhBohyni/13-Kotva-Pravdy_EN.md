# Chapter 13 — The anchor of truth and the boundary of the book
## The summary anchor of the whole book — and the sentences the book will never say

> *"The story may be beautiful. The evidence must be exact. And a book that confuses those two things stands on a boundary it has drawn for itself."*

---

## What this book is

**The Circle of Goddesses — the Book of the Keepers** is a parable: seven seats of a circle of care that holds lands the way a fleet holds paths — not owning, but holding.

It is a book about functions, not about persons. About seats, not about the women who sit on them. About a structure of care that the corpus already contains (L5 worlds, funds, administrative roles) but which did not yet have its own speech — because the lines had a story and the circle did not.

And it is a book about a future that is revealed by the lantern — **the revealed future**: an image that appears when the light widens, and narrows back to today when the light tilts down.

## What this book is not

The book **is not** and never will be:

- **A list of persons.** No character is a portrait of a particular woman; the link to real persons, where it exists, remains exclusively in `` `internal documentation` `` and is not carried into the corpus. The names the book uses (Meriam, Sita, Hina, Ana) are **archetypal layers**, not identity.
- **A legal document.** The structure of the funds, administration and succession is taken from `` `internal documentation` `` as a parable — not as a legal text. For legal truth the private instruments apply; for public truth the register of evidence applies.
- **A cult of goddesses.** "Goddess" is an archetype of care — a function that every woman (and every person) can carry. The book founds no cult, requires no faith, proclaims no salvation.
- **A financial promise.** The funds are presented as a structure of care — not as an opportunity. None of the stories may be read as "invest and get rich". ZION has held this boundary from the first page.
- **A claim to truth about the worlds.** Every L5 world is either BUILDING, or HORIZON, or INTENT — none is presented as finished. The lantern always narrows to today.
- **Proof that "this is how it will be".** The revealed future is a **direction**, not a forecast. The book shows how far the circle may see — not where it will arrive.

## The boundary the book has drawn for itself

And because the book holds a circle that holds a holding — it also holds a boundary:

**The truth of the story and the truth of the world must not be confused.** The story may be beautiful, the evidence must be exact — and a book that confuses those two things stands on the boundary it has drawn for itself. It holds that boundary the way the circle holds the seats: not by not wanting to cross it — but by knowing that crossing it would make of it something other than what it promised to be.
