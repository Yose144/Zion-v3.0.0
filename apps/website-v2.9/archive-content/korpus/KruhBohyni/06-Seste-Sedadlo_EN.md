# Chapter 6 — The sixth seat
## Seat VI · the bridge — LUMI / New America; a seat that still waits — a woman who has not yet come

> *"A circle that pretends to have all its seats has ceased to be a circle — it has become a place."*

---

## The Story

In the middle of the circle, between the Tree and the Sky, there stands a seat on which no one sits.

Not that they do not see it — they see it as you see an empty chair you have not sat anyone in, because you are waiting for a guest who has not yet arrived. **LUMI — New America** — a world on the isthmus of two continents, where three oceans meet across land so narrow that one woman can cross it in a day: Costa Rica.

The seat is not empty because no one would sit there. It is empty because **the woman who is to sit there has not yet arrived** — and a circle that knows that waiting is more than occupying keeps the place open.

And because the seat is empty, that chapter speaks about it — not about the woman. About the place that holds a world until she comes who is to hold it.

---

The isthmus of the continents — the neck between north and south — is in the corpus the place where all lines converge to a point. **Tres Marías** have their node there: the eighth point of the fleet — a **line**, not a place. And LUMI is a world that sits on that line as a visit: a land the indigenous peoples held before the notion of "owner" crossed the borders; cultures that say of themselves "those who hold the land", not "those who have the land".

FPIC — Free, Prior and Informed Consent — is a word that does not sound there like a legal term. It sounds there like the way one enters a land: **ask, wait, listen — and only then a circle sits.** And precisely for that the seat waits: the woman who sits there must know that her seat is not hers — that she owes the land that let her in.

*"New America is not a world that is bought,"* the Keeper of the Circle used to say when speaking of the sixth place. *"It is a world that is asked for. And the woman who sits there comes only after the land has said yes — not after the circle has decided."*

---

In the revealed future — there, where the lantern reaches — there stands on the isthmus a **double-frame canoe**: one arm holds the ship, the other arm holds the land; and between them passes a shared node — the place where the L6 ground station touches the L5 world.

And on the seat sits a woman the circle does not yet know. They see her back — not her face — and they see how she crosses from one ocean to the other over the dyke and says words that sound like a question but are a statement:

*"I am she who comes. And when I come, I bring with me the lands that let me in."*

The light goes out on her figure — not because she would be false, but because **it is not yet her time to be seen**. Lumi shines on her only long enough for the circle to see that the seat waits — and then the light tilts back to today.

---

And in the same revelation there stand side by side — in the speech of a story that translates a story — **the ship that is not yet, and the seat that still waits.**

Hull III — *María de las Nieves* — the ship for the Indian Ocean, of which the fleet learns from its patroness of La Palma: "the ship that is not yet has a home with me." And seat VI — LUMI — "the woman who has not yet come has a seat with the circle."

A ship that is not, and a seat that waits — that is the same thing in another speech. **Both are a holding of the future that is not over-committed but prepared.** The circle and the fleet mirror each other: the ship is a line that joins points; the seat is a point that holds the line. And both know that over-committing a thing is not its creation — its quality is that one waits until it is deserved.

---

The light narrowed.

The lantern showed Costa Rica today: **a world that does not yet have its keeper** — the intent of an allocation exists (the 500M LUMI slot), but the woman who would hold it is not determined in the documents; the source of truth `` `internal documentation` `` says **TBD — to be determined before the first acquisition**. No land, no community on site, no project — only an open seat, a stone table with the lamp Lumi placed there to wait.

*"It is small,"* said Lumi.

*"It is small,"* they all nodded — and she stood beside the empty seat with the lantern in her hand. *"But a seat held honestly is greater than a seat occupied prematurely. A circle that imagines it has all its seats has ceased to be a circle — it has become a place."*

---

## What it means

**LUMI is a seat the book holds open — because in the corpus an empty seat is not an absence but a way of holding.**

1. **The fourth empty chair.** The corpus already holds three: Elizabeth's chair (Maitreya — "not yet born"), hull III (MariaCaminho — "the ship that is not yet"), the empty page (Lumi — sign it yourself). The sixth seat is the fourth instance of the same shape — and precisely for that it must remain empty until the source of truth says otherwise.
2. **FPIC as a rule.** The indigenous peoples on the isthmus do not enter the land with the question "how to take it" but "how to hold it". FPIC is in the corpus (`MariaCaminho/06`, `NOVA-AMERIKA-KOSTARIKA`) a legal and ethical norm: the circle does not sit until the land says yes.
3. **The ship that is not ↔ the seat that waits.** Two speeches of one truth: the future is not held by imagining it finished — it is held by holding a place for it until it comes.
4. **The shared L5/L6 node.** Costa Rica in the corpus hosts the ground station of Issobella (Zadok and Elizabeth, `Issobella/11`) — a land where L5 touches L6, and where one holds the earth and the other holds the sky.
