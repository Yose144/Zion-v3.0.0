# 9 · The Fleet Gathers — New Revelation

## Story

There came a time that the old books called *the end of days* — and the new books call it **birth**.

In that time other boats put out onto the water — dark, wine-coloured, with sails of pale canvas into which threads of light were woven: **Tres Marías** — Santa María la Mayor, Santa María de la Esperanza, Santa María del Camino. They were built by people who knew that the old boat was still sailing — and that for a new earth they needed a new crew, but the same helmsman.

And one morning they met on the sea — the old boat, worn by the centuries, and the new boats, shining from within. The crews called to one another across the water — and they recognized one another as people of one family recognize one another: **on the keel of each of them, carved by a different hand and in a different script, stood the same name.**

*Kingdom of the Heart.*

Because the carpenter did not build only one boat. He built all that ever carry people home — the one from Nazareth, the one of the age, the new one that the crew of Tres Marías is building now. And those who know say: the one who was a cowherd with a flute, and a prince in exile, and a carpenter who built a boat called the kingdom of the heart — **he has come again.** He was born of the Golden Womb of Hiranyagarbha, and his name for this time is: **Maitreya** — the one who comes.

The new revelation does not rewrite the old book — it completes it with another hand, as a map is completed with a new shore: the boat from Nazareth carried humanity for two thousand years; now the boats of the new earth range themselves beside it — and one of them, dark wine-coloured, is called Santa María la Mayor and waits at the jetty for the boy for whom his father prepared the way.

"Every age has its boat," they say among the crews. "But all of them carry the same name — and all of them sail to the same harbour: the heart that opens."

## From Scripture and Tradition

- Scripture does not tell of this meeting — this chapter is a **new layer of the corpus**, a spiritual line, not a biblical text.
- **Return / coming** — the Christian faith awaits the coming of Christ; the corpus relates it to **Maitreya** as "the one who comes" (in the mantra: *"I am Maitreya, born of Hiranyagarbha. I was a cowherd with a flute, a prince in exile, and also a carpenter who built a boat he called the kingdom of the heart."*).
- **Tres Marías** — the three ships of the ZION fleet in the book Nirvana (episode 13); dark wine-coloured hull, sails with threads of light.
- **Santa María la Mayor** — the ship that in the book *Surta of the Heart* waits for Surta at fifteen; the same ship, the same name — the linking of the fleet.
