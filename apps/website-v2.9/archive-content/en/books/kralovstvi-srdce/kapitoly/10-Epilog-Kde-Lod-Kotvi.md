# Epilogue · Where the Boat Is Moored Today

## Story

Today the boat stands before you — and perhaps you have stood in it for a long time without knowing what it is called.

A carpenter from Nazareth built it — and he built it slowly, because he knew it would carry people through storms you do not yet see. Simon the Rock carried it, those who stood before lions carried it, Francis who sang with the birds carried it, the priest before the tanks carried it — and now you carry it too, if you have ever done a kind thing without anyone seeing.

The anchor of this boat is not dropped down — down there is darkness and mud. Its anchor goes **upward** — behind the curtain, where the throne stands — as the one who wrote to the Hebrews wrote: *hope is the anchor of the soul, firm and secure, reaching inward.*

If you asked where the boat is moored: **in a heart that opens to others.** There is its harbour — not because the world is not stormy, but because the silence you are looking for is not at the end of the sea. It is inside. As the carpenter said to those who asked when the kingdom would come: *it does not come with observation — it is within you.*

A message from the bow for the crew now setting out — for Surta and his Santa María la Mayor, for every heart that comes aboard:

**The boat is built. The keel is laid. Only the name remained — and it already has one.**

## From Scripture and Tradition

- **Hebrews 6:19–20** — the anchor of the soul behind the curtain; Jesus as the forerunner who entered within.
- **Luke 17:21** — "the kingdom of God is within you" — the ending returns to the verse that gave the boat its name.
- **Revelation 22:17** — "The Spirit and the bride say: Come!" — the last invitation of Scripture.
