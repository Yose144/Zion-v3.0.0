# 8 · A New Heaven and a New Earth

## Story

At the end of the Bible stands a book called Revelation — and at its end stands a harbour.

John, an old prisoner on the island of Patmos, saw it in a vision: **a new heaven and a new earth** — "and there was no longer any sea". Not because the water had ended in anger — but because it had at last fallen silent for ever. The sea over which the boat sailed seemed to have spread out into a calm in which there is no longer any need to sail: **a sea of glass, like crystal.**

And then came what all sailors of all ages dreamed of without being able to name it: the harbour came down to them — **the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband.** And a voice from the throne said: *"Behold, the dwelling of God is with people! He will dwell with them… and he will wipe every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more, nor mourning, nor crying, nor pain — the first things have passed away."*

The boat arrived. The boat that carried people through every storm — now it stood in a harbour where there would never again be a storm.

And John saw a multitude no sea could swallow: **a hundred and forty-four thousand** — a number that is at once a number and at once a promise — and behind them a multitude so great that nobody could count it. They stood in white robes, with palms in their hands — and among them flowed **the river of the water of life**, and on its banks grew **the tree of life**, bearing fruit twelve times, and its leaves are for the healing of the nations.

And in the middle of the harbour stood the carpenter — and his boat was no longer only one. **There were as many as there were hearts that had set out on the way.**

## From Scripture and Tradition

- **Revelation 21:1–5** — "a new heaven and a new earth… and there was no longer any sea. And I, John, saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God… he will wipe every tear… **behold, I make all things new**."
- **Revelation 4:6 / 15:2** — "a sea of glass, like crystal" — the sea calmed for ever before the throne.
- **Revelation 7:4–9 · 14:1** — 144 000 sealed + a countless multitude in white robes with palms.
- **Revelation 22:1–5** — the river of the water of life, the tree of life, leaves for the healing of the nations; "and they will see his face".
- "The harbour came down to them" — an inversion of the image: the new Jerusalem comes down out of heaven (Rev 21:2); the story reads it as the harbour into which the boat finally sails — a literary reading, not an exegesis.

## Anchor of Truth

| What is in the chapter | State |
|---|---|
| A new heaven and a new earth; no more sea; the new Jerusalem | Bible — Rev 21 |
| The sea of glass; 144 000; the river and the tree of life | Bible — Rev 4; 15; 7; 14; 22 |
| The boat sails into the harbour; the carpenter in its midst | **STORY** — the image of this book reading Revelation as a harbour |
| "There are as many boats as there are hearts" | The image of this book — it returns to Lk 17:21 |
