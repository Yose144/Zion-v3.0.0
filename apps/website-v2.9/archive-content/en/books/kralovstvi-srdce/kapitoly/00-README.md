# KINGDOM OF THE HEART

### The story of the boat built by a carpenter

> *"I am Maitreya, born of Hiranyagarbha. I was a cowherd with a flute, a prince in exile, and also a carpenter who built a boat he called the kingdom of the heart."*
> — mantra from the book [*Maitreya*](../../maitreya/kapitoly/epilog.md)

This book tells the **third embodiment** from that mantra: the carpenter of Nazareth, who in his father's workshop did not only plane yokes and tables — he built a **boat**. A boat nobody had asked for, for a sea that is on no map. A boat he called **Kingdom of the Heart**.

## What This Book Is

| Layer | Description |
|---|---|
| **Story** | A spiritual narrative — a medieval "midrash" on the carpenter of Nazareth; the building of the boat as an image of the whole of Jesus' work. **It is not a biblical text** — Scripture does not tell of a boat built by Jesus' own hands. |
| **Bible** | Every chapter stands on real verses (quoted under "From Scripture and Tradition"): the carpenter, the ark, the teaching from the boat, the storm, the kingdom within you, the sign of Jonah, the anchor of the soul, the new earth. |
| **Tradition** | The Church as a ship (*navicella*), Noah as a prefiguration, the anchor of hope — images from Christian tradition that the story carries. |
| **New revelation** | The closing chapter links the boat to the ZION corpus — the fleet Tres Marías, the mantra of Maitreya and the Compass of the Heart. A spiritual line of the corpus, not a historical fact. |

**Status:** MYTH / DRAFT — a literary book of the compass. The reader distinguishes Scripture from story; the anchors of truth help.

## How to Read

The story reads as a legend — and it is written so that it works for children too. Every chapter ends with a table, the **Anchor of Truth**: what is a verse of the Bible, what is Church tradition, what is the author's image, what is the layer of new revelation.

The book stands between **[Maitreya](../Maitreya/00-README.md)** (the mantra of three embodiments — this book is its third verse unfolded into a story) and **[Surta of the Heart](../SurtaSrdce/00-README.md)** (the boat that will one day sail beside Santa María la Mayor) — and it is supported by **[Nirvana / Tres Marías](../nirvana/13-Tres-Marias.md)**, the fleet into which the boat finally sails.
