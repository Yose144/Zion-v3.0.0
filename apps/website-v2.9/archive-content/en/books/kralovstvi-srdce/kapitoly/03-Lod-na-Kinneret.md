# 3 · The Boat on the Kinneret

## Story

One morning — before the fishermen had returned from the night's haul — they carried the boat on wheels down from Nazareth to the lake. They walked all day; the carpenter walked beside it with his hand on the hull as on a horse's withers.

At the Kinneret, by the town of Capernaum, the sea of Galilee was waiting — water that was at once a lake and at once a sea for all who lived by it. The fishermen were just untangling their empty nets. They had caught nothing — again.

The carpenter brought the boat down to the water. It lay on the surface quietly, as though it had been there since the beginning of time — as when a rightly made thing comes home. The fishermen watched, and afterwards said among themselves that no boat like it had ever stood there: low, fast, the keel so clean that the water beneath it murmured as against a rock.

The carpenter sat down in the bow, facing the shore — and as people gathered, he began to speak. Not from the synagogue, not from a step — **from the boat**. His voice was carried by the water further than walls; people stood along the shore and heard, even the furthest ones, who would not have stood at the doors. The fisherman Simon, who had pulled nothing all night, listened to him — and then, at a word, let down his nets where he himself no longer believed. He drew them up full, until they tore.

"Carpenter," said Simon, as they stood in the dripping silver glory of fish, "why did you build that boat? You will not sell it."

"I built it so that one could teach from it," he answered. "So that the people might hear from the water what they do not want to hear in the temples. Fishermen, come with me — I will teach you to **catch people**."

And they left the nets and the little boats and went. The boat stayed on the water — and from that moment it was never again only wood.

## From Scripture and Tradition

- **Luke 5:3** — "He got into one of the boats, which belonged to Simon, and asked him to put out a little from the shore; then he sat down and **taught the crowds from the boat**." — Teaching from the boat is a real image in the Gospels; sound carried over water to the shore is the experienced truth of the lake's acoustics.
- **Luke 5:4–10** — the miraculous catch after a night of empty nets; Simon Peter and the sons of Zebedee.
- **Mark 1:17 / Matthew 4:19** — "Come after me, and I will make you **fishers of people**."
- **Mark 1:16–20** — the calling by the boats on the Sea of Galilee (Kinneret — "harp" — the Hebrew name of the lake).
- **John 21** — after the resurrection again: the boat, the night without a catch, the word from the shore, the full net.

## Anchor of Truth

| What is in the chapter | State |
|---|---|
| Teaching from the boat, the calling of the fishermen, the miraculous catch | Bible — Lk 5:1–11; Mk 1:16–20 |
| That the carpenter built the boat himself in Nazareth | **STORY** — the image of this book (Scripture says only "the boat, which belonged to Simon") |
| "A boat, so that one could teach from it" | The image of this book — it embodies what the Gospels tell |
| Kinneret = lake/sea of Galilee | Geography + the Hebrew name |
