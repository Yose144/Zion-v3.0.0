Milaräpa


When I look at samsara, at the enemy,

at first it seems like a joyful experience.

Later this experience becomes delusional.

I finally understand, it's a dungeon, no escape.

The vastness of samsara no longer has power over me.


When you look at your love

you will see in her the form of a laughing goddess.

Later, however, she becomes a mischievous woman.

She ends up being a demon with a black face.

Desire for a lover is unhappiness.


When you look at your child

it seems to you that he resembles a young god

speaking with a milky mouth.

However, when he grows up, he will be a distant relative.

Finally, he is your enemy creditor.

It is a great mistake to feed an ungrateful enemy.


When you survey your wealth

at first you think of him as a rare gem.

After that, it seems to you that without him you are nothing, without success.

After all, you are like a bee gathering honey.

All that you have painstakingly acquired

others will take it away from you.

Think about it and practice the Dharma.


At one point I had a kettle,

and I don't have it anymore!

This insignificant event makes it clear

the whole law of impermanence

and shows us

what kind of situation you are in.


The moment she shattered

with the kettle that used to be my wealth

she became a lama

and she spoke magnificently

about the inevitable impermanence of things.




THE SONG OF VICTORY


In the footsteps of the Master,

Teacher Marpa,

on the path of salvation in solitude

I walk humbly

Give me strength

to remain with You in Unity.


He dispelled the delusion forever,

leads of the world

faded away tired of the silence,

the flower of consciousness and being blooms,

in my heart,

created by love.


The mud and muck of my mind is gone,

strife is silenced in Your wisdom

and I live in peace, independent,

by the measure of his own holiness.


The demons will not be disturbed by the king anymore,

when his troops rolled away time;

I live in the present

with peace in my soul.


O guru, I look at your brightness

and in your footsteps I walk lightly

alone on victory lane.







TEACHINGS OF JESUS OF NAZARETH WHICH YOU WILL NOT FIND IN THE BIBLE

(Found around 1950 in Palestine)


He spoke these words to his disciples while traveling in Egypt. Fragments of these lessons are recorded in the canonical gospels. They are all recorded in the so-called Qumran scrolls, which were found in Palestine near the Dead Sea in the middle of the twentieth century...

When he came down from the mountain where he welcomed the sunrise, those who gathered at the foot of the mountain said: You are a source of inspiration for us. Your words change hearts and your wisdom enlightens the mind. We want to listen to you.


  Tell us who we are?

He smiled and said: You are the light of the world. You are stars. You are the temple of truth. Within each of you is the Universe. Subdue the mind to the heart, ask your heart, listen through your Love. Blessed are those who know the language of being.


  What is the meaning of life?

Life is a journey, meaning and reward. Life is a dance of love. Your mission is to blossom. Being is a great gift. Your life is part of the history of the Universe. Life is more beautiful than all theories. Life is a reason to celebrate. Life is valuable in itself. Life happens in the present and the meaning of the present is to be present in it.


  Why do misfortunes haunt us?

You reap what you sow. Unhappiness is your choice. Poverty is a human creation. Bitterness is the fruit of ignorance. When you blame, you lose power. When you desire something, you chase away luck. Wake up.

A beggar is one who is not aware of himself. Those who do not find the inner kingdom are homeless. He who wastes time unnecessarily becomes a wretch. Don't make your life a living. Don't let the crowd destroy your soul. Don't let wealth be your curse.


  How to overcome adversity?

Don't judge yourself. Do not deny yourselves, for you are divine. Do not compare or divide. Thank you for everything. Rejoice, for joy works wonders. Love yourselves, for those who love themselves love others. Bless danger, for the brave find bliss. Pray when you feel joy and misfortune will not touch you. Pray, but don't do business with God.


  How do we achieve happiness?

Happy are those who are grateful. Happy are those who are calm. Happy are those who have found paradise within themselves. Happy are those who receive gifts with joy. Happy are those who seek. Happy are the awakened. Happy are those who listen to the voice of God. Happy are those who fulfill their missions. Happy are those who have known unity. Happy are those who draw near to God through meditation. Happy are those who live in harmony. Happy are those who see the beauty of the world. Happy are those who have opened themselves to the Sun. Happy are those who flow like rivers. Happy are those who are ready to accept happiness. Happy are the wise. Happy are those who are aware of themselves. Happy are those who love. Happy are those who extol life. Happy are those who create. Happy are those who are free. Happy are those who forgive.


  What is the secret of abundance?

Your life is the most precious jewel in God's treasury. God is the jewel of the human heart. The wealth within you is immense and the abundance around you inexhaustible. The more you give, the more you get. The world is rich enough for everyone to become rich. Don't blame yourself for wealth. Happiness stands at the doorstep of your house. Open yourself to abundance and turn everything in life into gold. Blessed are those who have found the treasure within themselves.


  How to live in the light?

Live every moment of your life to the fullest. A life not lived to the fullest breeds bitterness. Know that what is within is also without. The darkness of the world comes from the Darkness of the Heart. Man is the seed of the Sun. Meditation on God is dissolution in the Light. A clear mind is like the radiance of a thousand suns. Blessed are those who long for the Light.


  How to achieve harmony?

Just live. Do no harm to anyone. Don't be jealous. Let doubt purify you, but not weaken you. Dedicate your life to beauty. Create for the sake of creating, not for appreciation. Deal with others openly. Change the past. Bring something new to the World. Fill the body with Love. Become the energy of Love. Love enlivens everything. Where there is love, there is God.


  How to achieve a perfect life?

A happy person can change many people. The unfortunate remain slaves. Happiness loves freedom. Joy is where there is freedom. Understand the essence of happiness. Open up to the world and the world will open up to you. If you stop being in opposition, you become the ruler.


  What is the essence of reality?

  "I" is the name of Eternity. Self-awareness is the door to a higher reality. Self-awareness is the key to transformation. When you find the answer to the question, "Who am I?", you answer all questions. You become the cause of your own happiness.

And he looked at them all with love and added:

"Silence reveals a lot.

Be yourself.”
