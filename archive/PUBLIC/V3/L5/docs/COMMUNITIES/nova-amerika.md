## 1. Identity and Purpose

Nová Amerika is the **sixth and final node** of the Terra Nova L5 network — the **Bridge & Memory** node. Where the five pentagram nodes each hold an element (earth, fire, water, air, ether), Nová Amerika holds the **connection** itself: the land bridge where North and South America touch, and the cultural bridge between the indigenous nations of the Americas and the network we are building.

Its center is not a farm, not a temple, not a lagoon. Its center is a **circle of elders** — the FPIC-governed council through which every decision about this node passes. The community does not exist to host indigenous culture as an exhibit. It exists to be *governed alongside* it.

Three anchors define this node:

- **Memory** — the languages, seeds, medicinal plants and stories of the Americas' first nations (Bribri, Cabécar, Boruca, Ngäbe-Buglé, Maleku, Huetar, Chorotega) documented only with their holders' consent and remaining their property.
- **Bridge** — the geographic midpoint of the continents; a community where Polynesian (Te Pīko Ora), European (Genesis, Bohemia) and Asian (Bodhi Lanka) nodes meet the Americas.
- **Ground beneath the sky** — the same plot is planned to host the **L6 Issobella ground segment** (research campus, TT&C antennas, observatory). L5 underfoot, L6 overhead — one land, two layers, separate funds and governance.

> *"The bridge did not connect shores — it connected people. And the people it must connect first are those who were here before the map."*

## 2. Legal Structure

| Aspect | Status | Details |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Legal entity** | 🔵 Researching | Costa Rican association (asociación) vs community foundation vs hybrid |
| **Land ownership** | 🔵 Scouting | Target: 5–15 ha rural land **outside** indigenous territories (private purchase or long-term lease) |
| **Foreign ownership** | 🟢 Feasible | Costa Rica permits foreign land ownership (unlike Sri Lanka); still requires local legal counsel |
| **Indigenous territories** | 🔴 Hard boundary | The 24 legally recognized territories are autonomous — the project never acquires land inside them; partnership only via FPIC |
| **Zoning** | 🔵 Researching | Must permit: agriculture, education, eco-structures, research equipment (antenna field for L6 segment) |
| **Tax residency** | 🔵 TBD | Costa Rican non-profit structures; ZION DAO treasury interface |

### Recommended legal path

1. **Phase 0:** FPIC dialogue before any purchase — approach territory councils (ADI Talamanca, ADI Boruca etc.) as partners, not neighbors.
2. **Phase 1:** Costa Rican association (asociación) for the community + separate entity or joint-venture agreement for the L6 campus parcel.
3. **Phase 2+:** Foundation structure for the seed library & cultural programs; cooperative for the farm output.

## 3. Why Costa Rica

| Factor | Value | Meaning for L5 |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| **Geography** | ~9–10° N, land bridge N↔S America | Geographic midpoint of the Americas; symbolic and practical meeting point |
| **Indigenous presence** | 8 recognized territories (~100k people) | Living cultures: Bribri, Cabécar, Boruca, Ngäbe-Buglé, Maleku, Huetar, Chorotega |

## 4. Community Design (Vision)

- **Council of Elders** — representatives of partner territories in governance from day one (FPIC).
- **Seed Library of the Americas** — cacao, maize, beans, squash, local varieties; exchange with all L5 nodes.
- **Permaculture & agroforestry** — syntropic systems inspired by traditional Costa Rican cultivation.
- **Medical Table** — traditional medicine ↔ L5 holistic protocols; knowledge remains community property.
- **Residency programme** — guests from territories, students, craftspeople; exchanges with Te Pīko Ora and other nodes.
- **LoRa mesh + Guardian Node** — off-grid comms, block validation, 90/10 split.
- **Shared plot with L6** — the Issobella ground campus shares land, water, energy and mesh; separate budget and governance.

## 7. Risks

- **FPIC is non-negotiable** — if partner territories decline, the project re-scopes or relocates. No consent, no build.
- **Legal complexity** — foreign entity + indigenous partnership + shared L5/L6 parcel needs careful structuring.
- **Climate** — tropical humidity, lightning, hurricane season (Caribbean side); affects both farm and antenna field.
- **Steward vacancy** — no project steward appointed yet; must be named before first acquisition.
- **Cultural respect** — documentation of languages/knowledge only with consent; no extractive "knowledge harvesting".
