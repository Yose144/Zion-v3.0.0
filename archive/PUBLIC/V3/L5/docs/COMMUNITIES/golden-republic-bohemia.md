## 1. Identity and Purpose

Golden Republic Bohemia is the **fourth node** of the Terra Nova L5 network — the **Heart**. Where Genesis Garden is the **root** (earth, beginning, the seed), Dharma Temple is the **trunk** (fire, practice, the path), and Te Pīko Ora is the **flower** (water, completion, the living tree), Golden Republic Bohemia is the **heart** — the organ that circulates governance, that pumps decisions through the body of the network.

Its center is not a farm, not a temple, not a lagoon. Its center is a **circle** — a stone amphitheater under the open sky, where the community gathers to decide. No throne, no pulpit, no podium. Just a circle, salt on an oak table, and fire in the middle.

> *"In the Golden Republic there is no captain. There is a circle. Everyone paddles, no one commands."*

This is a **governance laboratory** — the first physical node where the voluntary protocol of coexistence from the Golden Republic concept is prototyped in practice. Where Czech wisdom meets ZION protocol:

- **Salt** (*berit melach*) — the covenant that holds without words. What we have tasted together, holds even without paper. ZION as salt: it does not govern the meal, it guarantees the rules hold.
- **Bridge** — Charles IV built the Charles Bridge at an astrologically precise moment (1-3-5-7-9-7-5-3-1). A bridge connects, it does not divide. ZION is a bridge between communities, not a wall.
- **University** — Charles University (1348), the first in Europe open to all nations. Education as a right, not a commodity.
- **Golden Bull** — the constitution of the Holy Roman Empire (1356), which prevented civil wars over the election of emperors. Written rules, distributed power, stability through structure. A proto-DAO.
- **The Ploughman and the Prophetess** — Přemysl the Ploughman, called by Libuše from the field to the throne. "Salt of the earth" — those who are forgotten until they are needed. A king who remembers he held a hoe.
- **The Linden Tree** (*lipa*) — the tree of Slavic sovereignty. Under the linden, decisions were made long before parliaments existed. Linden tea, linden honey, linden wood — a tree that has been with Bohemia for a thousand years.

> *"Golden Republic Bohemia builds governance in human time — not in quarterly reports, not in epoch blocks. Every decision made in the circle, every linden planted, every salt covenant shared — these remain long after this team has departed."*

## 2. Legal Structure

| Aspect | Status | Details |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Legal entity** | 🔵 Researching | Czech `z.s.` (spolek / association) vs `z.ú.` (ústav / institute) vs community foundation (*nadace*) |
| **Land ownership** | 🔵 Scouting | Target: 2–5 hectares in Říp region / central Bohemia / Vysočina |
| **Zoning** | 🔵 Researching | Must permit: agriculture, educational activities, temporary structures, eco-tourism |
| **Building permits** | 🔵 Not yet required | Phase 1 uses temporary/eco-cabin structures; permanent pavilions in Phase 2 |
| **Tax residency** | 🔵 TBD | Czech tax optimization for non-profit agricultural/educational entities |

### Recommended legal path

1. **Phase 0–1:** `z.s.` (spolek / civic association) — lowest barrier, fast registration since 2014 legal reform (no Ministry approval needed), no minimum capital, eligible for EU grants
2. **Phase 2+:** Transition to `z.ú.` (ústav / public benefit institute) — better for long-term land ownership, international donations, institutional partnerships (universities, foundations)
3. **Alternative:** `Community foundation` (*komunitní nadace*) — for endowment-style funding, land trust model, multi-generational stability
4. **Hybrid:** `z.s.` (operator) + `nadace` (land trust) — separates operations from land stewardship

### Land strategy

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Buy** | Full control, collateral for loans, permanent stewardship | High upfront cost (EUR 15,000–50,000/ha for agricultural land in Bohemia), bureaucracy |
| **Long-term lease (30+ years)** | Lower initial investment, flexibility | Less security, lease restrictions on permanent structures |
| **Co-ownership (sdružení)** | Shared risk, community equity | Complex governance, exit difficulties |
| **Municipal concession** | Very low cost, local integration | Uncertain availability, political risk |

**Recommended:** Long-term lease with purchase option — secure the land, prove the model, then buy. Czech agricultural land is significantly cheaper than Western European equivalents.

## 3. Physical Infrastructure

### 3.1 Energy

| Parameter | Current | Phase 1 Target | Phase 2 Target |
|-----------|---------|----------------|----------------|
| **Primary source** | Grid (temporary) | 8 kWp PV + 16 kWh LiFePO4 battery | 20 kWp PV + 40 kWh battery + grid fallback |
| **Daily production** | Grid-dependent | ~30 kWh (summer) / ~12 kWh (winter) | ~70 kWh (summer) / ~25 kWh (winter) |
| **Backup** | Grid | Grid (minimal use, winter critical) | Battery + generator (emergency) + grid fallback |
| **Critical loads** | Basic lighting, phone charging | Fridge, water pump, kitchen, governance pavilion | Full operations, workshop tools, ZION node rack, educational center |
| **Efficiency measures** | Solar LED, hand tools | DC microgrid for lighting, solar water heater, passive solar design | Heat pump, rammed earth thermal mass, biomass backup (wood) |

**Continental climate reality:**
- Winter (Dec–Feb): Short days (8h daylight), frequent overcast, snow cover — **solar production drops 60–70%**
- Summer (Jun–Aug): Long days (16h daylight), high production — **surplus for battery charging**
- **Critical design factor:** Battery sizing must account for 3–5 consecutive overcast days in winter
- **Recommendation:** Grid connection as fallback for winter (unlike off-grid Mediterranean/tropical nodes) — minimal use, but essential for reliability

**Technology stack:**
- Panels: Longi / Jinko / local Czech/EU supplier
- Batteries: Pylontech / BYD LiFePO4 (5–15 year warranty)
- Inverter: Victron MultiPlus-II (hybrid, scalable, proven off-grid)
- Monitoring: Victron VRM + custom ZION telemetry (future)
- **Winter heating:** Wood stove (local biomass), passive solar (south-facing glass), rammed earth thermal mass

## 6. Governance

### 6.1 Off-chain: Sociocratic Circles

```
General Circle (all Guardians + long-stay members)
    ├── Operations Circle (daily farm, guests, maintenance)
    │       ├── Agriculture Sub-circle
    │       ├── Hospitality Sub-circle
    │       └── Infrastructure Sub-circle
    ├── Finance Circle (budget, accounting, ZION node, grants)
    ├── Community Circle (conflict, wellness, culture, admissions)
    ├── Governance Circle (protocol design, circle coordination, Guardian gates)
    └── Knowledge Circle (documentation, education, seed library, Akashic archive)
```

**Decision-making:** Consent-based (not consensus — "no reasoned objection" threshold). Double-linking: each sub-circle elects a representative to parent circle.

**The Central Decision Circle as primary governance space:**
- All major community decisions are made in the **stone amphitheater** — the physical circle
- No electronic devices in General Circle meetings held in the circle (paper notes only)
- Salt on the oak table — a reminder that what is decided here is a covenant
- Fire in the center — a reminder that governance is alive, not bureaucratic
- **Winter exception:** When weather prevents outdoor circle meetings, Pavilion Most (Bridge) serves as indoor governance hall

### 6.2 On-chain: ZION DAO

| Function | Mechanism | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Treasury spending (< 500 EUR) | Operations wallet, 2-of-3 | Immediate |
| Treasury spending (500–5,000 EUR) | Finance Circle proposal, 3-of-5 | 48h review |
| Treasury spending (> 5,000 EUR) | General Circle vote → on-chain proposal | 7 days, 60% majority |
| New Guardian admission | Community Circle → General Circle → DAO record | 14 days, consent |
| Protocol upgrades (node, mesh, governance) | Governance Circle + Finance Circle | 48h review |

Four gates must be passed before a person becomes a full Guardian of Golden Republic Bohemia:

| Gate | Requirement | Duration | Status |
|------|-------------|----------|--------|
| **Gate 1 — Arrival** | First visit, land walk, circle observation, salt shared | 1 week minimum | 🔵 Designing |

**Linden tree planting:** Every new Guardian plants a linden tree in the alley. The tree is named, catalogued, and cared for by the community forever. This is the **living ledger** of Golden Republic Bohemia — an immutable record written not in code but in wood and leaf and blossom.

## 15. Cultural Sensitivity Framework

Golden Republic Bohemia draws inspiration from Czech history and mythology. This requires careful framing:

| Element | Appropriate Use | Inappropriate Use |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Přemysl / Libuše** | Inspiration for "leader from the soil" — governance rooted in land work | Claim to Přemyslid dynastic authority, nationalist revivalism |
| **Charles IV / Golden Bull** | Inspiration for distributed power, written rules, proto-DAO thinking | Claim to Holy Roman Empire continuity, monarchist nostalgia |
| **Charles Bridge** | Metaphor for connection, ZION as bridge between communities | Replica of Charles Bridge, tourist kitsch |
| **Charles University** | Inspiration for open education, knowledge as commons | Claim to institutional authority, academic credentialism |
| **Salt covenant** | Universal principle — *berit melach* — covenant that holds without words | Appropriation of specific religious traditions without context |
| **Linden tree** | Czech cultural symbol, Slavic sovereignty, living ledger | Nationalist symbolism, exclusion of non-Czech participants |
| **Říp** | Geographic anchor, mythological center, landscape connection | Claim that this site is "the" center of Czech identity |

**Guiding principle (from Execution Charter):**

> *"No monetized 'sacred skins', no gamification of spiritual authority."*

Golden Republic Bohemia must not be sold as a "return to the Czech golden age." It is a **governance laboratory**, not a national myth for sale. The Czech cultural elements are **inspiration and context**, not authority or product.
