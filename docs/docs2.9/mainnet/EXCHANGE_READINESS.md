# 🏦 ZION Exchange Readiness Guide

**How to get ZION listed on exchanges — without a company**

---

## 📋 Overview

ZION is a **decentralized, permissionless protocol** maintained by an open-source community.

There is:
- ❌ No company
- ❌ No legal entity
- ❌ No central authority
- ✅ Community of independent contributors

This guide explains how to approach exchange listings in a legally compliant way.

---

## 🎯 Listing Strategy (Realistic Path)

### Phase 1: DEX (Easiest, Legally Clean)

**Requirements:**
- ✅ MainNet running
- ✅ Native token exists
- ✅ Community exists

**Options:**
- Atomic swaps
- Wrapped ZION (bridge)
- Community-run liquidity pools

**Legal Advantage:**
- No company needed
- No listing contract
- No issuer
- Peer-to-peer exchange only

> 💡 **This is the cleanest start.**

---

### Phase 3: Larger CEX (After History)

**Path:**
1. Smaller CEX first
2. Build liquidity + uptime
3. Establish history
4. Then approach larger players

**What larger exchanges check:**
- MainNet stability (min. weeks/months)
- Reorg policy
- Deposits/withdrawals test
- Node documentation
- Core dev contact availability

> They ask: *"If something breaks at 3 AM, who fixes it?"*

---

## 🚫 What NOT To Do (Without Company)

| ❌ Never Do | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Sell tokens for fiat | Requires regulation |
| Promise returns | Securities law |
| Talk about "investment" | Legal risk |
| Hold custody for others | Money transmission |

> ⚠️ Doing any of these triggers regulatory requirements.

---

## ⚖️ Legal Framework (EU/CZ)

### Your Role

You are:
- ✅ Open-source developer
- ✅ Network participant

You are NOT:
- ❌ Issuer
- ❌ Broker
- ❌ Investment provider

### ZION Classification

Properly structured ZION is:
- ✅ Utility / protocol token
- ✅ Mined, not sold
- ✅ Permissionless
- ✅ No promise of profit

➡️ **NOT a security**

## 🛡️ How to Protect Yourself Legally

### Communication Guidelines

✅ **Say:**
- "Core contributors"
- "Guardians"
- "Community"
- "Protocol"

❌ **Never say:**
- "Company"
- "CEO"
- "Roadmap guarantees"
- "Investment opportunity"

### Public Statements

Always include:
> "ZION is an experimental protocol. Participation is voluntary and at your own risk."

Never say:
- "Price will go up"
- "Buy now"
- "Guaranteed returns"
