## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Śiva Mahādeva** — the **Trimūrti** axis of **transformation**: the ending of illusion, care for yogis and devotees (Paśupati), the cosmic dancer (**Tāṇḍava**). In OASIS he complements the already developed Vaiṣṇava axis (Kr̥ṣṇa, Rāma, Kalkī).

---
