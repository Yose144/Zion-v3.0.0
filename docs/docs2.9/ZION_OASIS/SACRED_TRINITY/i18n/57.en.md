# 57. DOÑA ROSALÍA IXMUKANÉ — Keeper of the Popol Vuh 📖

*Chichicastenango | Chilam balam | Intergenerational transmission*

---

> *"The trick is not in the weapons. The trick is in the cooperation of two — Hunahpú and Xbalanqué — who touch hands and jump together."*

---

## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Doña Rosalía** is a **composite** of modern chilam balam women — a **keeper of the oral memory** of the Popol Vuh and a bridge between the **church of Santo Tomás** and the market where the story **meets life**.

---

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Location:** Chichicastenango (market + church as a lore anchor).
- **Ability:** **Summon Popol Spirit** — temporary allies from the myth in battle (narrative summon).
- **NPC pair:** her granddaughter **Anička** — intergenerational transmission.

---

## 📜 QUEST LINE

**"Nine Houses of Lies"** — modern counterparts of Xibalba (media, isolation, attacks on decency…).

---

## ⚡ ABILITY

**Story Summon** — calling characters from the Popol Vuh as **temporary allies** with a cooldown and ethical framing.
