## 🌟 Who Is the Dalai Lama?

**Name:** Tenzin Gyatso (བསྟན་འཛིན་རྒྱ་མཚོ) - "Holder of the Teaching, Ocean [of Wisdom]"  
**Tibetan title:** Kundun (ཀུན་དགའ) - "Presence" (spiritual presence in human form)  
**Worldly title:** His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama  
**Incarnation:** the 14th Avalokitéšvara (Bodhisattva of Compassion)  
**Date of birth:** 6 July 1935, Taktser, Tibet  
**Symbol:** The ocean of wisdom, laughter in the midst of suffering, a bridge between East and West

---

## 🏔️ The Story of a Life

### Childhood in Tibet (1935-1950)

**Birth:**
- Family: simple farmers in a small Tibetan village
- Name: Lhamo Thondup (ལྷ་མོ་དོན་འགྲུབ) - "The Goddess Who Fulfils Wishes"
- Age 2: recognised as the reincarnation of the 13th Dalai Lama
- Age 4: officially enthroned as the 14th Dalai Lama

**The recognition test:**
```
The monks brought out objects:
├─ The 13th Dalai Lama's mala (rosary) → "This is mine!"
├─ A drum → "This is mine!"
├─ A walking stick → "This is mine!"
└─ False objects → He ignored them
```

**Monastic upbringing:**
- Potala Palace, Lhasa (the capital of Tibet)
- Education: Buddhism, philosophy, metaphysics, medicine
- Teachers: the most illustrious lamas of Tibet
- Study: 18 hours a day, memorising 100,000+ verses

---

### Invasion and Exile (1950-present)

**1950: The Chinese invasion**
- China occupied Tibet (allegedly a "liberation")
- Age 15: forced to assume political power (normally not until 18)
- Negotiator: he sought a peaceful settlement with China

**1959: Escape into exile**
- Tibetan national uprising against China
- Exile: across the Himalayas into India (two weeks, on foot, in disguise)
- 80,000+ Tibetans followed him into exile
- Founding of the Tibetan government in exile (Dharamsala, India)

**1989: Nobel Peace Prize**
- For nonviolent resistance to the Chinese occupation
- Philosophy: "My enemies are my greatest teachers"
- He refused violence even as Tibet faced genocide

**2011: A historic decision**
- He abdicated political power (handing it to a democratically elected government)
- The first Dalai Lama in history to give up power
- "Democracy is better than divine monarchy"

### Loving-kindness Meditation (Metta)

**The practice (5 minutes a day):**

1. **Towards yourself:**
   > *"May I be happy. May I be at peace. May I be free from suffering."*

2. **Towards those you love:**
   > *"May those I love be happy..."*

   > *"May all those I do not know be happy..."*

4. **Towards your enemies:**
