# 52. HEȞÁKA SÁPA — Black Elk 🪶

*Lakota Wičháša Wakȟáŋ | Guardian of the Sacred Circle | He Sapa*

---

> *"I saw that it was holy in a circle. And this circle — everything that is holy moves within it."*

---

## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Heȟáka Sápa** (Black Elk, Oglala Lakota, 1863–1950) is the archetype of the **visionary** who carried the **Great Vision** of the six grandfathers. In ZION OASIS he is not a copy of a particular person — he is a **stylized guide** through the circle, the directions and the ancestors.

---

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Location:** He Sapa (the Black Hills) — the sacred centre of the Lakota world.
- **Teacher:** for players at **CL2–CL4** who seek **connection with the ancestors** and the meaning of the **sacred circle**.
- **Dialogues:** Lakota phrases with translation (e.g. *Mitakuye Oyasin*).

---

## 📜 QUEST LINE

**"The Sacred Circle"** — four journeys (East → South → West → North); after the fourth, a **vision** tailored to the player's path.

---

## ⚡ ABILITY

**Vision Quest** — a personalized revelation after completing the four directions.
