# 58. BÍLÉ PERO — Hopi Keeper of the Tablet 🪶

*Bear Clan | Nine signs | The second half of the stone*

---

> *"The true Pahána will bring the second half of the stone tablet. What is in that second half? Son, nobody knows until it shows itself."*

---

## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Bílé Pero** refers to **Hopi prophecy** and the **stone tablet** — the archetype of a **keeper** who distinguishes the **true Pahána from the false one**.
