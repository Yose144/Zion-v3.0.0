# 84. GAṆEŚA — Vināyaka 🐘

*Remover of obstacles · First block · Patron of song and text*

---

> *"Do not write a verse until you have laid the sutra as the first block — only then do further lines flow."*

---

## 🌟 ESSENCE

**Gaṇeśa / Vināyaka** — removes **vighna**, patron of beginnings; in the tradition of writing the Mahābhārata he **writes down Vyāsa's dictation** (see **[17 Vyāsa](17_VYASA.md)** and the quest "Ganesha's Pen"). In OASIS he is the **guardian of the entry threshold**.

---

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Location:** city thresholds + nodes of new chains.
- **Quest:** "First Block Obstacle".
- **Ability:** **Vighna Clear** — a one-time unblocking of a prerequisite deadlock (governance cooldown).
