# 179. GALACTIC CONVENOR — The Parliament of the Galactic Federation ✨

*A round table and **tiers of mandate** only within the frame of the **[Galactic Federation](../../../TerraNova/cosmic/GALAKTICKA_FEDERACE.md)** — a constitution above "race", an audit above pride.*

---

> *"A federation is not a scale of who has better blood — only a treaty among systems where minorities have the right to slow a war down."*

---

## 🌟 ESSENCE

Galactic Convenor — an **interstellar parliament**: it facilitates the degrees of federation (**charter → council → plenum → envoi → civil ring**) as a parallel to DAO **delegation + veto**.

Galactic "lines" or culturally distinct civilizations may appear only as **faction aesthetics** while respecting the **hard filter** of the federation (**no parallels to human ethnicity and no phenotype supremacy**).

---

## 🎮 ROLE IN ZION OASIS

- **Quest:** "Protocol Parliament Session" — PvE about **weighted mandates**, **term limits**, **transparent rotation**.
- **Quest (optional chain):** "Federation Tier Audit" — the player **maps institutional tiers** from [`GALAKTICKA_FEDERACE.md`](../../../TerraNova/cosmic/GALAKTICKA_FEDERACE.md) onto the lore UI (without a real political stance).
- **Ability:** **Orbit Silence** — a brief **pause of toxic broadcasts** from scripted raid leads (*not a silencing of dissent in the DAO — only scripted grief*).
