## 📋 1. TOKEN CLASSIFICATION

### 1.1 Utility Token vs Security Token

**ZION Token je klasifikován jako UTILITY TOKEN:**

#### Howey Test Analysis (US benchmark, applicable EU)

```
SECURITY token = Investment + Common Enterprise + Profit Expectations + Efforts of Others

ZION Analysis:
├─ Investment of Money:           ✅ YES (users pay EUR for tokens)
├─ Common Enterprise:             ❌ NO (decentralized blockchain, not company equity)
├─ Reasonable Profit Expectations: ⚠️ MIXED (utility focus, but price appreciation possible)
└─ Efforts of Others:             ❌ NO (open-source, community-driven development)

RESULT: UTILITY TOKEN (2/4 criteria met, leaning non-security)
```

#### European Approach (MiCA Framework)

**Asset-Referenced Token (ART)?** ❌ NO
- ZION není vázán na fiat měnu nebo komoditu
- Hodnota není stabilizovaná na referenční aktiva

**E-Money Token (EMT)?** ❌ NO
- ZION není vázán 1:1 na EUR nebo jinou měnu
- Není vydáván bankou nebo payment institution

**Crypto-Asset (General Category)?** ✅ YES
- ZION spadá pod obecnou kategorii "crypto-asset"
- Podléhá MiCA Title III-IV (transparency, marketing rules)

#### Utility Charakteristiky ZION

| Funkce | Popis | Utility Score |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **Mining rewards** | Kompenzace za PoW mining | ✅ ✅ ✅ |
| **Transaction fees** | Platba za blockchain transakce | ✅ ✅ ✅ |
| **DAO governance** | Voting rights v decentralizované organizaci | ✅ ✅ |
| **Staking** | Lock tokens pro síťovou bezpečnost | ✅ ✅ |
| **Humanitarian tithe** | 1.618% redistribuce na charity | ✅ |
| **DeFi collateral** | Zajištění půjček, liquidity pools | ✅ |

**Závěr:** ZION má silnou utility basis → **není security**

### 1.2 Argumentace pro Utility Status

**1. Decentralizace:**
- Open-source blockchain (GitHub public repo)
- Community governance (DAO)
- No single entity controls issuance
- Mining is permissionless

**2. Funkcionalita před spekulací:**
- Primární use case: mining, governance, payments
- Whitepaper zdůrazňuje utility, ne investment returns
- Marketing focused on technology, not price appreciation
- No promises of profit or ROI in official materials

**3. Network Effect:**
- Token value derives from network adoption, not company performance
- Similar to ETH (utility for gas fees) or BTC (store of value)

**4. No Profit Sharing:**
- Presale revenue goes to development, not distributed as dividends
- Token holders don't receive company profits
- No equity-like rights (voting is governance, not business decisions)

### 3.1 Legal Basis

**EU Directives:**

### 3.2 KYC Requirements

#### Tier 1: Basic KYC (€0 - €1,000)

**Required:**
- Full name
- Email address (verified)
- Country of residence

**Optional:**
- Phone number

**Risk:** Low  
**Verification:** Email confirmation only

#### Tier 2: Standard KYC (€1,001 - €15,000)

**Required:**
- Full name (as per ID)
- Date of birth
- Country of residence
- Government-issued ID (passport, ID card, driver's license)
- Selfie with ID (liveness check)

**Verification Method:**
- Automated (Sumsub, Onfido, Jumio)
- Manual review for edge cases

**Risk:** Medium  
**Processing Time:** 24-72 hours

#### Tier 3: Enhanced Due Diligence (€15,001+)

**Required (all from Tier 2, plus):**
- Source of funds declaration
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement <3 months)
- Politically Exposed Person (PEP) screening
- Sanctions screening (OFAC, UN, EU lists)
- Optional: Video verification call

**High-Risk Indicators:**
- Large cash purchases (>€50k)
- Structuring (multiple transactions to avoid thresholds)
- High-risk jurisdictions (FATF grey/black list)
- PEP status
- Unusual transaction patterns

**Verification Method:**
- Manual review (compliance officer)
- Third-party databases (World-Check, Dow Jones)

**Processing Time:** 5-10 business days

### 3.3 Prohibited Jurisdictions

**Sanctioned Countries (OFAC, EU, UN):**
- ❌ Russia (due to Ukraine war sanctions - verify current status)
- ❌ North Korea
- ❌ Iran
- ❌ Syria
- ❌ Cuba (US sanctions, less relevant for EU entity)
- ❌ Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk regions

**High-Risk Jurisdictions (FATF):**
- ⚠️ Afghanistan, Myanmar, etc. (enhanced DD required)

**IP Blocking:**
- Geo-blocking for sanctioned countries
- VPN detection (flag for manual review)

### 3.4 AML Procedures

**Transaction Monitoring:**
```python
RED FLAGS:
├─ Rapid purchase-refund cycles (money laundering test)
├─ Multiple accounts with same identity documents
├─ Payments from third-party accounts (not buyer's name)
├─ Cryptocurrency mixing services as payment source
└─ Inconsistent KYC data (address doesn't match IP location)

ACTIONS:
├─ Flag for manual review
├─ Request additional documentation
├─ Freeze transaction (pending investigation)
└─ Report to Financial Analytical Unit (FAU) if >€15k suspicious
```

**Record Keeping:**
- Retain KYC data for 5 years after customer relationship ends
- Transaction records: 5 years minimum
- Suspicious activity reports (SAR): 5 years

**Reporting:**
- Suspicious transactions >€15,000 → FAU (Finanční analytický úřad)
- Large transactions >€15,000 → automatic reporting (within 5 days)
- Monthly aggregated reports to internal compliance

## 💰 5. DAŇOVÉ ASPEKTY (Tax Implications)

### 5.1 DPH (VAT)

**Presale tokenů = OSVOBOZENO OD DPH**

**Právní základ:**
- Směrnice EU 2006/112/ES (VAT Directive), čl. 135(1)(e)
- ESD rozsudek C-264/14 (Hedqvist): Bitcoin a kryptoměny jsou osvobozeny od DPH
- Zákon č. 235/2004 Sb. (zákon o DPH), § 54

**Zdůvodnění:**
- Kryptoměny = platební prostředek (means of payment)
- Směna EUR → ZION = směna měn
- Směna měn je osvobozena od DPH

**Fakturace:**
```
Faktura č. 2026001
Omnity.One s.r.o., IČO: 19828748

Položka: ZION Token Presale - Builder Pack
Množství: 81,250 ZION
Jednotková cena: €0.00615 (€500 / 81,250)
Celkem: €500.00

DPH: OSVOBOZENO OD DPH dle § 54 zákona o DPH
(kryptoměny jako platební prostředek)

Způsob úhrady: Bankovní převod / Kreditní karta
```

**Poznámka:** Pokud by byla poskytnuta SLUŽBA (např. consulting, development), 
pak by DPH 21% bylo aplikováno. Ale samotný prodej tokenů = osvobozeno.

### 5.2 Daň z příjmu právnických osob (Corporate Income Tax)

**Sazba:** 21% (Česká republika, 2025)

**Zdanitelný příjem:**
```
Tržby z presale:                       €5,000,000
(přepočet na CZK kurzem ČNB k datu přijetí platby)

Mínus uznatelné náklady:
├─ Mzdy a sociální pojištění           €800,000
├─ Vývoj a R&D                          €1,500,000
├─ Marketing a propagace                €500,000
├─ Právní a compliance služby           €200,000
├─ IT infrastruktura (servery, hosting) €300,000
├─ Nájemné, kancelář                    €50,000
├─ Účetní a daňové služby               €30,000
├─ Pojištění                            €20,000
└─ Ostatní provozní náklady             €100,000
                                        ──────────
Celkem náklady:                         €3,500,000

Základ daně (zisk před zdaněním):      €1,500,000
Daň z příjmu (21%):                     €315,000
Čistý zisk po zdanění:                  €1,185,000
```

**Optimalizace:**

**1. R&D odpočet (Výzkum a vývoj):**
- Česká republika: Odpočet 100% výdajů na R&D + dodatečný odpočet 100% (= celkem 200%)
- Podmínky: Dokumentovaný R&D projekt, přiznáno MPO (Ministerstvo průmyslu a obchodu)
- Benefit: Snížení základu daně o €1,500,000 (R&D výdaje)

```
S R&D odpočtem:
Základ daně: €1,500,000 - €1,500,000 (R&D) = €0
Daň: €0 ✅
```

**2. Časové rozlišení (Matching Principle):**
- Tržby z presale 2026 vs. Náklady v letech 2026-2027
- Pokud mainnet launch až 2027, část nákladů lze časově rozlišit

**3. Reinvestice do DHM (Dlouhodobý hmotný majetek):**
- Servery, mining zařízení → kapitálové výdaje
- Odpisy rozložené do budoucích let

### 5.3 Srážková daň (Withholding Tax)

**Dividendy zahraničním investorům:**
- Pokud by Omnity.One vyplácela dividendy, srážková daň 15% (nebo dle DTA)

**Řešení pro airdrops:**

## 🚨 7. CRISIS MANAGEMENT & INCIDENT RESPONSE

### 7.1 Regulatory Intervention

**Scenario:** CNB or other authority challenges token sale

**Response Plan:**
1. Immediate pause of presale (within 24 hours)
2. Legal counsel consultation (emergency meeting)
3. Prepare response to authority (written, with evidence)
4. Offer refunds if non-compliant (with penalties covered by Omnity.One)
5. Amend whitepaper/T&C if minor issues
6. Resume presale only after clearance

### 7.2 Security Breach (Hack, Data Leak)

**Scenario:** QR database hacked, customer data leaked

**Response Plan:**
1. **Immediate containment:**
   - Shut down affected systems (within 1 hour)
   - Isolate compromised data
   - Change all passwords, API keys

2. **Assessment:**
   - Determine scope of breach (which data, how many users)
   - Forensic analysis (external security firm)

3. **Notification:**
   - ÚOOÚ (Czech DPA): within 72 hours (GDPR Art. 33)
   - Affected users: without undue delay (GDPR Art. 34)
   - Public statement (transparency)

4. **Remediation:**
   - Issue new QR codes (if compromised)
   - Offer identity theft protection services (free)
   - Implement additional security measures

5. **Legal liability:**
   - GDPR fines: up to 4% of annual turnover or €20M
   - User lawsuits (potential)
   - Insurance claim (if cyber insurance in place)

### 7.3 Smart Contract Bug

**Scenario:** Critical vulnerability in presale escrow contract

**Response Plan:**
1. **Freeze contract** (if pause function available)
2. **Audit emergency:** Immediate re-audit by CertiK/OpenZeppelin
3. **Bug bounty payout** (if discovered by white-hat)
4. **Fix & redeploy:**
   - Patch vulnerability
   - Migrate funds to new contract (multi-sig approval)
   - Test extensively on testnet
5. **User communication:**
   - Full transparency (blog post, email)
   - Timeline for fix
   - Compensation for any losses (if applicable)

### 7.4 Market Collapse (Crypto Bear Market)

**Scenario:** BTC drops 80%, presale demand collapses

**Response Plan:**
1. **Lower expectations:**
   - Accept that €5M target may not be reached
   - Adjust development plan to €2-3M budget

2. **Extended timeline:**
   - Prolong Phase 3 (from 6 weeks to 12 weeks)
   - Allow more time for market recovery

3. **Alternative funding:**
   - Venture capital (if necessary)
   - Grants (blockchain development grants)
   - Community crowdfunding (Gitcoin, Giveth)

4. **Pivot strategy:**
   - Focus on utility, not speculation
   - Build core product despite low funding
   - Prove value before asking for more investment
