## What is OASIS?

OASIS is not a game. It is not a metaverse. It is not a website.

OASIS is a **living multiverse** — a persistent, shared, online reality where
thousands of players explore a galaxy of 55 worlds, each one a different
genre, a different culture, a different dimension of experience.

It sits on top of the ZION blockchain as **Layer 4** — the layer where
proof-of-work meets consciousness, where mining becomes a spiritual act, and
where the line between game and reality begins to dissolve.

You can fly through it right now, in your browser, at
[oasis.zionterranova.com](https://oasis.zionterranova.com).

## The Galaxy — 55 Worlds

The OASIS galaxy is a spiral of 55 worlds, each placed at a deterministic
position in 3D space. The galaxy is not decoration — it is **navigation**.
Every point of light is a place you can travel to.

The worlds fall into five categories:

| Category | Count | What they are |
|:---------|:-----:|:--------------|
| **Star systems** | 10 | The cosmic backbone — Sirius, Alpha Centauri, Orion, Pleiades, Arcturus, Antares, Betelgeuse, Vega, Tau Ceti, Proxima Centauri. Each is a civilisation with its own culture, economy, and quests. |
| **Worlds** | 17 | Cultural and mythological realities — ancient Egypt, India, Tibet, China, Japan, Maya, Inca, Aztec, Babylon, Greece & Rome, Norse & Celtic, Africa, Aboriginal Australia, Aotearoa, Indonesia, Atlantis, Lemuria. |
| **Planets** | 12 | Genre worlds — each planet is a different game: FPS arenas, open-world sandboxes, MOBA battlefields, cyberpunk cities, retro 8-bit dimensions, and more. |
| **Sectors** | 9 | Cinematic universes reimagined as playable space — space opera, dystopian futures, magical realms, deep-space exploration. |
| **Dimensions** | 7 | Alternate layers of reality — AI futures, steampunk, solarpunk, horror, underground, western, 8-bit. Dimensions exist outside normal space-time. |

Every world has:
- a **vibe** — the emotional texture of being there,
- a **summary** — what makes it unique in the multiverse,
- **tags** — its layer and type,
- a **Golden Egg clue** — one piece of the great puzzle,
- **quests** — generated per world, across exploration, combat, harvest,
  puzzle, and social categories.

## The Golden Egg

At the heart of OASIS is a single question: **can you find the Golden Egg?**

The Golden Egg is hidden somewhere in the multiverse — it could be on any
world, in any dimension, in any era. **108 clues** are scattered across the
galaxy, grouped into seven categories: sacred profiles, knowledge levels, the
ZION whitepaper, the source code itself, blockchain data, community events,
and a temple pilgrimage.

Three master keys unlock the final path:
- **Ramayana Key** — 30 clues
- **Mahabharata Key** — 35 clues
- **Unity Key** — 43 clues (requires both previous keys)

The first three players to reach the highest consciousness level, collect all
108 clues, and assemble all three keys win from a **prize pool of over 8
billion ZION** — plus NFTs, titles, and permanent legacy in the OASIS
universe.

The Golden Egg is not just a treasure hunt. It is an **initiation**. Each
world, each quest, each decision moves you closer to — or further from — the
truth. In the Vedic tradition, the Golden Egg is *Hiranyagarbha*: the cosmic
seed, the unity from which all worlds are born.

## What is live right now

The OASIS web client at [oasis.zionterranova.com](https://oasis.zionterranova.com)
is the **first public window** into the multiverse. It runs in any modern
browser with WebGL2 — no download, no install.

### What you can do today

- **Warp into the galaxy** — a cinematic intro sequence carries you from the
  edge of the universe to the galactic core.
- **See all 55 worlds** — each world is rendered as a 3D node with category
  colours, labels for star systems, and an oriented warp-gate torus.
- **Fly through space** — free-flight mode with WASD/QE/Space/Shift controls,
  pointer lock, a speed HUD, and a boost bar. You are a pilgrim ship moving
  through the galaxy.
- **Explore warp-gate networks** — hyperlanes connect neighbouring worlds
  with pulsing, additive energy lines.
- **Select any world** — a detail panel slides in with the world's name,
  category, location, vibe, summary, tags, Golden Egg clue number, archetype,
  and generated quests.
- **Enter a world** — clicking through flies the camera into a procedural 3D
  environment unique to each world category:
  - *Star systems* — glowing star with corona and orbiting satellites.
  - *Planets / worlds* — procedural canvas-textured planet with atmosphere,
    rings, and moons.
  - *Sectors* — dodecahedron with wireframe overlay.
  - *Dimensions* — torus-knot wireframe with additive duplicates.
- **Filter by category** — isolate star systems, worlds, planets, sectors, or
  dimensions.
- **Hear the universe** — Web Audio ambient drone, brown-noise wind layer,
  warp sweep effects, and a mute toggle. Audio activates on your gesture.

### Visual highlights

- **Galaxy core** — a glowing, Contact-inspired centre that the camera spirals
  toward during intro.
- **Tree of Life** — a procedural fractal tree with golden emissive branches,
  9 glowing fruits, and purple roots — the Fountain made cosmic.
- **Nebula** — translucent sprite clouds that give depth to the void.
- **Selection beacon** — a pulsing beam and ring around the focused world.
- **Warp flash** — a full-screen flash on entering a world.
- **Pilgrim ship** — a small stylised vessel visible in flight mode, with
  engine trail particles scaled by speed.
- **Warp-gate vortex** — swirling energy planes inside each gate torus, with
  hyperlane streamers flowing between connected worlds.

## The Technology

The current OASIS web client is built with:

- **Next.js** + **React** + **React Three Fiber** + **Three.js**
- **@react-three/drei** for camera controls, HTML overlays, and helpers
- **@react-three/postprocessing** for Bloom and cinematic effects
- **Web Audio API** for the ambient soundscape
- A **deterministic PRNG** (mulberry32) — the galaxy looks identical on every
  device, every reload, because randomness is seeded, not accidental

The galaxy data — all 55 worlds, their positions, vibes, summaries, quests —
lives in a single engine-agnostic config. This is deliberate: the same data
that drives the web client today will drive the **Unreal Engine 5** client
tomorrow. The domain model is separated from the rendering layer so that
OASIS can move from browser to native without rewriting the universe.

The backend is a **Rust / Axum** server with REST API, WebSocket real-time
events, and SQLite persistence — part of the ZION L4 crate (`zion-oasis`).

The galaxy lives whether you are logged in or not. Economies move,
territories shift, the Golden Egg waits. The goal is a single, living world

The old pattern of gaming is: play → grind → get bored → leave. OASIS
inverts it: **the longer you stay, the more the universe reveals.** Every
world is a door. Every quest is a step. Every clue is a thread in a tapestry

## How to enter

1. Go to [oasis.zionterranova.com](https://oasis.zionterranova.com).
2. Click **Enter the OASIS** — the warp intro begins.
3. You arrive at the galactic core. Look around.
4. Click any world to inspect it. Use the filter bar to isolate categories.
5. Press **Flight Mode** to take manual control. Fly with WASD, look with the
   mouse, boost with Space.
6. Approach a world and enter it — the camera dives in, the warp flash hits,
   and you are standing in a procedural 3D environment.

No account needed. No wallet needed. Just a browser and a sense of wonder.
