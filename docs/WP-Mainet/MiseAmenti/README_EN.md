> **Canonized:** 2026-08-31.

---

## Why Mise Amenti exists

ZION already has technical plans, whitepapers, onboarding guides, narrative books, operational runbooks, and the NirvanaCloud expansion. Without a shared center, however, two risks emerge:

1. **The story outruns reality** — a horizon gets mistaken for a shipped product.
2. **The technology loses its meaning** — people see ports, contracts, and tests, but not the human problem the network is intended to serve.

`MiseAmenti/` is that center. It is not a replacement whitepaper, not a replacement for code, and not a new religion. It is the **canonical integration layer** that holds together:

- **evidence** — what can be verified today;
- **construction** — what is being actively built and has measurable outputs;
- **horizon** — a long-term direction, not a promised product;
- **story** — human language that helps explain purpose but never rewrites facts.

## Protected vocabulary

- **Mise Amenti** — a literary and organizational name for the long-term commitment to preserve verifiable knowledge, human dignity, freedom of choice, and the capacity for recovery across crises. It is not a historical claim about literal “Halls of Amenti.”
- **Nirvana** — an image of technology ceasing to feed fear, debt, and attention addiction. It is not a religious claim or a promise of paradise.
- **Global Assimilation** — exclusively **voluntary interoperability, open adoption, and cooperation**. Never cultural, political, economic, or personal coercion.
- **Global Attention** — human attention and computation directed toward education, care, creation, and verifiable contribution; never dark patterns, addictive UX, or manipulation.
- **Consciousness** — in narrative texts, a metaphor for attention, ethics, and coherence. No claim is made that software, models, or agents have demonstrable subjective consciousness or spiritual authority.
- **LIVE / UNDER CONSTRUCTION / HORIZON / HYPOTHESIS / MYTH** — mandatory claim-state labels defined in [`01-Kanon-a-Ustava.md`](./01-Kanon-a-Ustava.md).

## The first vow of the Amenti Mission

> **Do not ask for faith where evidence can be offered.**  
> **Do not sell a horizon as the present.**  
> **Let everyone enter freely, question freely, and leave freely.**  
> **When a vision conflicts with a fact, correct the vision — never the fact.**

This is the canonical form of the “rescue mission”: not rescuing people against their will, but preserving tools, truth, memory, and mutual aid so people can rescue one another together.
