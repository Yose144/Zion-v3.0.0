## What is Issobella?

**Issobella** is the name of ZION's orbital station — and the name of a vow. It first appears in the TerraNova prologue as "our home on the star road" (year 2040, ~420 km orbit). This book gives it a whole story:

> **Bodhi Gaia tends the earth beneath us. Issobella keeps watch over the sky above us.**

The name carries its own mythology. *Iso-* comes from the Greek *ísos* — "equal, the same". The cosmos, by the cosmological principle, is **isotropic and homogeneous**: no direction is special, no place is the center. And precisely because of that, every place can become home. *-bella* is beautiful. Issobella — *isotropic beauty, the same beauty in every direction* — is a reminder that humans do not carry a flag into space; they carry a home.

The Nirvana book tells how the pilgrims landed on the New Earth after the flood, built the House of Lumi, bridged the abyss with WARP, found the golden womb Hiranyagarbha, listened to the Voice of Amitabha, entered the crystalline worlds of OASIS and tapped the Springs of Life. In episode eleven the **Quantum Fire** was lit — science as prayer. This book continues: **what happens when the pilgrims lift their gaze from the soil to the stars** — and everything they must admit first, so they are not deceived by their own longing.

It is the most distant book of the series — and therefore must be the strictest about its anchors. On Earth, a tree grows or it does not. In orbit, nothing grows until someone carries it there. Every chapter therefore ends with a table of verifiable facts.

## What this book is not

- **Not a sale of "space", shares in a station, or a yield instrument.** No gate, gift or participation carries a return.
- **Not a promise that the station will fly.** The 2030–2045+ roadmap is a horizon, not a guaranteed schedule.
- **Not a physical theory.** The quantum fire is an image and an open question; where we lack proof we write *HYPOTHESIS*.
- **Not militarization.** No part of L6 research is intended for armament; safety gates are part of the design, not a later patch.
- **Not a replacement for `StatusV3.md`, `MiseAmenti/07` or `L6data/`.** Where they differ, they win.
