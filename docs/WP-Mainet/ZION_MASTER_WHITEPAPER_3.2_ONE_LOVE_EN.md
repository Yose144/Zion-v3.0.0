You stand at the porthole of an orbital station and look down. A blue
sphere. Clouds like white veils over the oceans. And along the night edge

as the skin of an apple, the only thing between everything alive and the
void.

Astronauts have a name for this moment: the **Overview Effect**. Up

On your display blinks a message from the network that keeps this station
aloft: thousands of nodes across the world, a humanitarian fund
distributing aid with no politician, no corporation, no application form.
Automatically. Transparently. Immutably. Because it was programmed that
way — not as a rule, but as a value.

That is the **HORIZON**. The year 2040 has not arrived and nobody
guarantees it.

But the will that points toward it began for real: in 2025, in one
commit, in one network, in one intention. And that intention has been
written into every block since:

> **What if a network served life — and not the other way around?**

One planet. One people. One love.

# BOOK TWO — Quantum Revolution: what was broken

The old world runs on extraction. Mine the earth, mine the attention,
mine the body, mine the future. Money moves in circles of circles, and at
the center there is nothing. The speculator is rewarded more than the
builder. The middleman grows fatter than the farmer. The one who holds
secrets holds power, and the one who has nothing is asked to trust.

Blockchain was born to break this. But too often it became the same song
tuned louder. Proof-of-work became an arms race of specialized hardware
that burns the earth for profit. Proof-of-stake became a bank in drag.
Tokens became casino chips. DAOs became chat rooms where the rich vote
their wealth.

ZION does not pretend to be a perfect answer. It is an honest answer.

- **ASIC resistance** — Ekam Deeksha is memory-hard and NPU-friendly, not
  just a hash loop for the highest bidder.
- **Humanitarian fee split** — every block routes 5% to humanitarian
  relief, 5% to the Issobella science/community fund, and 1% is burned as
  the pool-fee slot. No vote can turn off the value.
- **Open source, auditable, runnable** — not a whitepaper fantasy.
  Production binaries are running on the Edge server right now.

This is the quantum turn: from extraction to contribution, from trust to
verify, from "me first" to "we first."

# BOOK THREE — Ekam Deeksha: who am I on the path

Ekam Deeksha is not an algorithm. It is a discipline.

In Sanskrit, **Ekam** means "one." **Deeksha** means "initiation." The
miner's machine does not only crunch numbers. It enters a memory-hard
ritual of transformation: scratchpad mixing, AES rounds, thermal noise,
Keccak final hash. Each hash is a small test of the machine's will to
find order in noise.

In the v3.2 Mainnet Alpha the parameters are canonical:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Scratchpad | 512 KiB |
| AES passes | 2 |
| Random reads | 128 |
| Final hash | Keccak-256 |
| Active from | V31 genesis |

The CPU reference implementation, the OpenCL/CUDA/Metal GPU kernels, and
the `zion-miner` backend mappings are synchronized. This is the **Ekam
Deeksha v3.2** canonical form. No ASIC on the market today can optimize
512 KiB of memory-hard mixing into a straight SHA loop. The playing field
stays wide.

But Ekam Deeksha is also a mirror. The one who mines ZION is not only
earning a reward. They are sustaining a network that carries a
humanitarian fund, a cross-chain bridge, a game world, a DAO, and a
promise. The work is the initiation. The block is the answer.

Terra Nova. It is far away. But every block is a step toward it.

# TRINITY — The three streams of one river

The official ZION miner runs three parallel streams:

| Stream | Coin | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Stream 1 | ZION | Mainnet ZION PoW — Ekam Deeksha v3.2 |
| Stream 2 | GPU AuxPoW | ZANO (etash/progpow-like), external pool via `eth_submitLogin` / `eth_getWork` |
| Stream 3 | CPU AuxPoW | VRSC (VerusHash), external pool via `mining.authorize` / `mining.extranonce.subscribe` |

This is the **Trinity engine**. One machine contributes to three
networks. Revenue is tracked proportionally and can be switched
autonomously with 15% hysteresis. PRL (Pearl) is explicitly excluded from
autonomous selection.

The pool also runs full V3 parity: PPLNS, payout confirmation sweep with
UTXO fallback, stratum v1, vardiff, and share forwarding to upstream
AuxPoW pools. Recent live results (2026-08-09 Trinity tuning session):
ZION 100% accept, ZANO 100% accept post-fix, VRSC ~83% accept (multi-hop
latency remaining).
