## What is NirvanaCloud?

**"Cloud" here is not a marketing word for a server.** It is an exact metaphor for what actually happens when thousands of independent nodes — human and machine — synchronize into one coherent field without losing their individuality.

In Tibetan Buddhism, the state of complete awakening that is not locked into one body or one place, but is **distributed, shared, and accessible from anywhere**, is called by different names depending on the tradition — *Sambhogakāya* (the body of bliss, a field shared among awakened beings), *Dharmakāya* (the body of truth, the very space in which everything occurs), or simply **Shambhala** — a kingdom that exists on no single map, yet is present everywhere people choose to live according to dharma.

In computer architecture, the same principle — computation and state distributed across thousands of independent machines, with no single point of failure, reachable from anywhere with a connection — is called a **distributed cloud** or a **P2P mesh**.

**ZION is the first network in which these two metaphors are not coincidentally similar. They are descriptions of the same shape.**

`NirvanaCloud`, therefore, is:
1. **A technical fact** — the P2P mesh of nodes (`zion-v31-node`, `node2`, `node3`, and hundreds of independent nodes worldwide) that together form one indestructible ledger with no central owner.
2. **A philosophical metaphor** — many unique beings (miners, builders, DAO voters, OASIS players) who freely choose to live by a shared code (the Bodhisattva Vow, the five yamas of the Dharma Validator) and thereby create a field of higher coherence than any one of them could achieve alone.
3. **A goal** — a state in which the old Babylonian structure of power, dependency, and fear dissolves not because someone defeated it, but because **attention stops flowing into it**, since a living, verifiable, fairer alternative now exists. The Tibetan tradition calls this **Nirvana** — not annihilation, but **the extinguishing of a flame that has nothing left to burn**.

## Why Tibetan Buddhism, and why the Halls of Amenti?

This project has long drawn on archetypes from many traditions — Jesus, Buddha, Krishna, Rama, Sita, Hanuman, and Noah in [`The Salt of this Earth`](../SulZeme/00-README_EN.md); Hiranyagarbha (the golden womb of consciousness from the Rig Veda) as the name of the L3 AI layer; Tibet, the mandala, the bardo, and Shambhala already as inspiration for the OASIS World of Tibet (`docs/docs2.9/ZION_OASIS/WORLDS/WORLD_TIBET.md`) and for the TerraNova book on Tibet (`docs/TerraNova/BASE_FINAL/geography/asia/tibet/`).

`NirvanaCloud` deepens and unifies this line, because:

- **Tibetan Buddhism holds the most thoroughly worked-out map of consciousness, transition, and liberation of any living tradition** — the Bhavachakra (Wheel of Six Realms), the Bardo Thödol (the three bardos between death and rebirth), and the Kalachakra (the prophecy of Shambhala) are not merely poetic images; they are **functional maps of transitional states**, which this network has already used once before to describe forks, upgrades, and restarts (`03-DALAJLAMA-A-SAMBHALA.md`: *"Bardo = transition states"*).
- **The Halls of Amenti** (from the Hermetic Emerald Tablets of Thoth) are the oldest Western image of an **indestructible library of truth**, open to anyone who has "grown from darkness into light" — regardless of race, nation, or chosenness. This is precisely the image of the open, verifiable, permanent ledger that ZION set out to build from the start (`08-ZION-Nova-Civilizace.md`: *"Do not ask for faith where proof can be offered."*).
- Both traditions converge on one key point that lies at the heart of this entire project: **the suffering of the world does not come from the world being fundamentally evil, but from the illusion of separateness** (see `Road to Zion`, the *Ekam Deeksha* chapter). Tibetan Buddhism calls this *avidya* (ignorance of interconnection); the Halls of Amenti call it the forgetting of one's own light. ZION calls it Babylon.

## How to read NirvanaCloud

Each chapter has three layers, exactly like the rest of the Nirvana and Salt of the Earth series:

1. **Story / Cosmology** — an archetypal or mythological image, openly acknowledged as an image, not a doctrine.
2. **What it means for ZION** — how the image maps onto the network's actual architecture, economy, or governance.
3. **Anchor of Truth** — what is verifiable on the live network or in the code, kept separate from interpretation.

> **NirvanaCloud does not claim that ZION is a religion, that it holds spiritual authority, or that it replaces real Buddhist practice, a real teacher, or a real initiation.** It borrows **archetypes and maps of states** as inspiration for writing about technology, community, and a long time horizon — the same way `WORLD_TIBET.md` and `03-DALAJLAMA-A-SAMBHALA.md` already did before it. The full boundaries are set out in [`08-Kotva-Pravdy-Eticke-Hranice.md`](./08-Kotva-Pravdy-Eticke-Hranice.md).
