## Why this series exists

The assignment was clear: take the motif of **"ZION as the salt of this earth"**, carry it through figures humanity already knows and trusts — Jesus, Buddha, Krishna, Rama, Sita, Hanuman, Noah, Radha, and the avatars of Oasis — and make it a **real, readable story**, not a collection of quotes. The goal is not clever sentences. The goal is **trust** — and trust cannot be written, only earned.

That is why every episode has two parts:

1. **The Story** — fiction, openly admitted as fiction. The characters are archetypes, not historical claims about what actually happened. Nobody claims Jesus, Buddha, and Krishna truly met under a fig tree.
2. **Verifiable Facts** — a short box at the end of each episode that separates myth from code. What is in the box can be checked on the running network, in the repository, or in the whitepaper. What the story says beyond the box is narrative, not a claim.

This separation is not a formality. It is the **core of honesty** of this series: a story may be beautiful, but network facts must be precise. When the two may never be confused, the reader can trust each separately — the story as story, the facts as facts.

## What this series is not

- **It is not a religious claim.** Nobody claims ZION is a sacred text or that it builds on actual theology. The figures are borrowed as **archetypes of trust** that humanity already knows across cultures — not as a claim on their authority.
- **It is not a financial promise.** No episode says "invest and get rich." The compass shows direction, not yield.
- **It is not a replacement for the whitepaper.** Where the story speaks of an algorithm, a reward, or a hard cap, the numbers follow the canonical [`ZION_MASTER_WHITEPAPER_3.2_ONE_LOVE_EN.md`](../ZION_MASTER_WHITEPAPER_3.2_ONE_LOVE_EN.md), the technical reference [`ZION_Technical_Whitepaper_v3.1_EN.md`](../ZION_Technical_Whitepaper_v3.1_EN.md), and the running network — not the other way around.
