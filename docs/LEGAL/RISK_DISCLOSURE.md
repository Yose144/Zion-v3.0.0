# Risk Disclosure

Participation in decentralized networks involves **significant risk**.

## Technical Risks

Risks include, but are not limited to:

### Software Risks
- software bugs or vulnerabilities
- smart contract failures
- protocol design flaws
- dependency vulnerabilities

### Network Risks
- network attacks (51% attack, eclipse attack)
- consensus failures
- chain reorganizations
- network partitions

### Security Risks
- loss of private keys
- loss of access credentials
- wallet compromise
- phishing attacks

## Economic Risks

### Market Risks
- extreme price volatility
- total loss of value
- liquidity constraints
- market manipulation

### Regulatory Risks
- regulatory uncertainty in various jurisdictions
- potential future restrictions
- tax implications
- compliance requirements

## Operational Risks

### Infrastructure Risks
- node failures
- mining difficulty changes
- network upgrades
- service interruptions

### Human Risks
- user error
- social engineering
- insider threats
- key person risk

## Experimental Technology Warning

ZION is experimental technology and:
- may change at any time
- may break without warning
- may cease to function entirely
- carries no warranty

## Participation Guidelines

You should only participate if:
- you fully understand these risks
- you can afford to lose all associated value
- you have conducted your own research
- you accept personal responsibility

## No Guarantees

The ZION community:
- cannot prevent losses
- cannot reverse transactions
- cannot recover lost keys
- cannot guarantee uptime
