# No Investment Representation

ZION is **not an investment**.

## What We Do NOT Do

The ZION community does not:
- promote ZION as an investment opportunity,
- guarantee price appreciation,
- promise returns, profits, or dividends,
- provide financial advice.

## Forward-Looking Statements

Any reference to future development, roadmap, or ecosystem growth is provided **for informational purposes only** and does not constitute a commitment or guarantee.

Development plans:
- may change without notice,
- may not be completed,
- may differ from expectations,
- carry no legal obligation.

## Appropriate Motivation

Participation in the ZION network should be motivated by:
- interest in decentralized systems,
- contribution to open-source technology,
- personal or educational experimentation,
- belief in the protocol's mission.

## Explicit Warning

**If you are seeking financial returns, ZION is not suitable for you.**

Do not participate if:
- you expect guaranteed profits,
- you cannot afford to lose your contribution,
- you are relying on ZION for income,
- you view ZION as a "get rich quick" scheme.

## No Fiduciary Duty

The ZION community and contributors:
- owe no fiduciary duty to token holders,
- make no promises about future actions,
- are not responsible for market conditions,
- do not guarantee any outcomes.
