## Why a Compass, Not a Map

A map says: *Go this way. Turn here. Arrive there.*

A compass says: *North is that way. The rest is up to you.*

Terra Nova is not a map. The world we are building does not yet exist — no map can precisely describe terrain that is shaped by the act of passing through it. Every community that emerges will be different. Every Guardian will bring their own path.

**But a compass always points. Even in a storm. Even in the night.**

| Cardinal direction | Symbolism |
|-------------------|----------|
| **North** | Consciousness — awakened, sharing, loving |
| **South** | Roots — Rigveda, Tesla, Mollison, Satoshi, Bhagavan |
| **West** | Technology — code, blockchain, AI (a tool, not a goal) |
| **East** | Horizon — Issobella, the stars, a galactic network of consciousness |
| **Centre** | **You** |
