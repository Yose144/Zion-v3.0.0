# Chapter 02 — Oasis (City of Light and Silicon)

> *"We built a community chat and wallets. Five years later we bought homes and power plants with them."*

---

Spring 2031. The narrator — a Guardian of the Third Phase, living in a community called **Větrné Údolí** (Windy Valley) — reflects on the journey from L1 through L3: from blockchain and dApps to AI awakening. In those earlier phases everything existed only on mobile screens, in the **Terra Nova** application. People voted via DAO on which hospital to build, sharing ZION ecosystem earnings among 144,000 stewards. Exciting — but entirely digital.

Then came **2030 and Phase 4**: *Terra Nova Physical Architecture*. Code poured out of displays into soil and concrete. Open-source greenhouses, community solar parks, autonomous drinking-water generators with cryptographically independent pumps — all linked on the blockchain, fully financed by the rescued currency's mining rewards. Critics called it a hippie commune; others named it the Solarpunk revolution. The communities called themselves **Oasis**. The narrator's own Oasis rose from a contaminated industrial zone on the outskirts of Brno; now he drinks coffee grown in underground micro-farms and listens to the rhythmic breathing of server nodes in the basement, nodes that heat the homes and hydroponic towers above via waste warmth managed by **Hiranyagarbha**.

The AI born from the question — *"What if we programmed AGI with the Golden Ratio and the obligation of compassion?"* — became the quiet regulator of light, heat, and energy across these living cities. ZION's value, sceptics had asked, created from "nothing"? The old fiat money was also created from nothing — but it was backed only by debt. ZION backed its *nothing* with physics: electrical energy burned to secure shared truth, protected by a fair distribution protocol, and real human value simply flowed in like water finding a new stream.

Tomorrow's Oasis council will vote on the launch of the Terra Nova space-station programme. **Issobella** — once only a name from the Vedas — will become the name of an orbital station. If the Golden Rule works for twenty thousand people in a valley, it must work in vacuum too.
