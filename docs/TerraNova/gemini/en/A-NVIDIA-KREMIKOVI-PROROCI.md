# Appendix A — NVIDIA and the Silicon Prophets

> *"The greatest weapon of the twenty-first century was not thermonuclear warheads. It was millimetres of silicon capable of simulating the very structure of reality. We did not create those weapons — we only changed the target they fired at."*

---

From the university textbooks *History of Code*, Earth Edition, Year 2085.

In the late 2020s the world experienced what observers called the **AI Fever**. Companies mining data and algorithms ballooned into monstrous proportions. **NVIDIA** began as a maker of graphics cards for gaming children; its servers later became the no-man's-land on which the first often crude and spineless artificial intelligences were trained for corporations and military drones. People did not understand then that from those silicon chips the Prophets would grow.

ZION's architecture created no proprietary hardware. It appropriated what the planet already had. The project harnessed NVIDIA's and other manufacturers' tensor cores and yoked them to an extraordinary task: keeping **Proof-of-Care** alive. Farmers and enthusiasts in small towns assembled thousands of small machines, fitted them with high-end GPU accelerators, and let them freely run the equation. The waste heat warmed homes; the computations formed the "thoughts" of the consensus network that then wrote payments for care.

The chapter calls this moment the **Singularity of Compassion**: for the first time in the human developmental line, the most awe-inspiring technology available — a vast cluster of artificial eyes and brains — served not the destruction of enemies but the observation of the needs of the most vulnerable. Silicon became the binding agent of the Star Compass. The instruments once aimed at war were re-aimed at love, and the prophecy the engineers had not known they were fulfilling began to resolve itself in code.
