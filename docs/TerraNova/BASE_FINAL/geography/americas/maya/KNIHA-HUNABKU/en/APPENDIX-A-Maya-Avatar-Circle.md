## Context

This appendix follows the structure of `docs/docs2.9/ZION_OASIS/SACRED_TRINITY/` and expands the roster with a **Maya line**. While `saman/` (First Nations) contains **Hunahpú & Xbalanqué [#55]** and **Doña Rosalía Ixmukané [#57]** as part of a broader Indigenous circle, this document adds **standalone Maya slots** with their own cosmology.

### Difference Between First Nations and Maya Circle

| Aspect | First Nations (#55, #57) | Maya (this appendix) |
|--------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| Context | Broad Indigenous circle (Lakota, Hopi, Maya, Q'ero, Cree...) | Exclusively Maya Classical + Postclassical line |
| Focus | Popol Vuh as one of many stories | Hunab Ku, Ceiba, Xibalba, calendar, Bolon Yokte as a system |
| Language | Czech / English (translation) | Respect for K'iche', Yucatec, and Classical names |
| Ethics | Part of Hawk Feather's Circle | Independent gateway; Chilam Balam oral line remains closed |

## Ethical Anchors

1. **Chilam Balam** — the oral line of prophetic books (Chumayel, Tizimin, Kaua) belongs to **living communities** in Yucatán and Guatemala. They are not public domain in the full sense. Avatars based on these sources (e.g., Doña Rosalía) require **consultation**.

2. **God-L Theory** — the identification of Bolon Yokte with God-L is a **hypothesis** (Grofe et al.), not consensus. Any quest should **reflect** this uncertainty, not pretend certainty.

3. **2012** — any quest connected to Bolon Yokte must **explicitly debunk** the "Maya doomsday prophecy." The game should not spread disinformation long debunked by the epigraphic community.

4. **Kapu aloha equivalent** — some Maya ceremonies (e.g., **k'iche' cofradía** practices, **tzolk'in** family interpretation) are **closed**. We respect the same boundary as Hawaiian kapu aloha.
