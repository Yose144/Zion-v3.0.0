## Where the Money Goes — Priorities and Practice

The DAO votes on allocation each month. Community priorities:

**Water — first priority.**

🟢 **REALITY 2026:** 771 million people lack access to clean drinking water (WHO, 2023). The technology exists — solar pumping stations, filtration systems, rainwater harvesting. What is missing is funding.

The ZION Humanitarian Fund finances specific projects — not grants to organizations, but direct installations in communities. Every coin recorded on the blockchain.

**Food and seeds — biological freedom.**

Seed Libraries are physical archives of biological diversity — thousands of varieties that industrial agriculture has erased over the past century. We preserve them. We propagate them. We share them.

**Education — knowledge as a right.**

Open, offline-first educational platforms. Libraries with access to the ZION knowledge commons. School programs integrating conscious development into curricula.

**Health — Medical Tables for all.**

Manufacturing and installation of Medical Tables in communities without access to conventional medicine. Training of local healers.

**Energy — solar micro-grids.**

Villages without electricity: solar panels, batteries, basic grid. Energy is the foundation of all other assistance — without electricity, nothing else functions.

## The Golden Republic — Politics Without Politicians

🌟 **HORIZON 2030–2035 (speculative horizon, deliberately):**

At a certain point, the Terra Nova network of communities reaches critical mass. Thousands of communities. Hundreds of thousands of people. A global infrastructure of care, education, health, and energy operating outside the old system.

A question will arise: *How is governance organized — without a state, without a political party, without elections every four years?*

**The Golden Republic** is the answer. It is not a state. It is not a revolution. It requires the overthrow of nothing.

It is a **voluntary protocol for coexistence** — like Bitcoin for money, but for social order.

### Eight Principles of the Golden Republic

| # | Principle |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | Membership is voluntary and revocable at any time |
| 2 | Rules of coexistence are formed locally, inspire globally |
| 3 | No monopolies — economic or informational |
| 4 | Education is a right — not a commodity |
| 5 | Health is a right — not a commodity |
| 6 | Energy is a right — not a commodity |
| 7 | Blockchain as transparent law (not government decree) |
| 8 | DAO as a living constitution (not a fixed document) |

The Golden Republic did not abolish old states. It did not offer them war. It offered a better alternative — and people gradually chose it.

> **Golden Republic Bohemia** (Czech Republic) is the first physical node prototyping the Golden Republic protocol in practice — a governance lab and circle of decision rooted in Czech wisdom: salt, bridge, Golden Bull, Přemysl the Ploughman, Libuše, Charles IV. In parallel, **Bodhi Lanka** (Sri Lanka) prototypes the **Bhakti protocol** — love (Rama-Sita), Sri Maha Bodhi (the oldest living tree, 288 BCE), Rama Setu bridge, and Ayurveda as a living backbone of care.

*Voluntarily. Gradually. One Guardian, one community, one network at a time.*

That is how civilization changes — not by revolution, but by replacement. Not by destroying the old, but by building the new.
