📊 Software Revenue & Treasury

ZION is Fair Launch – no token sale / no presale. Treasury is funded primarily through software sales (licenses, miner, DAO products) and community support. The section below is an allocation model (ratios), not a fundraising target.

🔧 Development (40%)

40%
  • Core Team: 5 full-time developers (backend, frontend, blockchain, DevOps)
  • Smart Contracts: Multi-sig escrow, vesting contracts, governance
  • Multi-Chain Bridges: Solana, Stellar, Cardano integration
  • Mobile Wallets: iOS and Android applications
  • Security Audits: CertiK or Quantstamp audit before mainnet

🖥️ Infrastructure (25%)

25%
  • Dedicated Servers: 10+ mining nodes, 5 RPC nodes globally
  • Pool Infrastructure: High-performance mining pools with low latency
  • Database Clusters: PostgreSQL, Redis, Elasticsearch for monitoring
  • CDN & Hosting: Cloudflare Enterprise, AWS/DO deployment
  • Monitoring: Prometheus, Grafana, 24/7 alerting systems

📢 Marketing (20%)

20%
  • Community Growth: onboarding, education, content, translations
  • PR & Media: development reports, partner articles, transparency
  • Events: meetups, conferences, community gatherings
  • Liquidity & Listings: preparation for listings (after milestones)

⚖️ Legal & Compliance (10%)

10%
  • MiCA Compliance: EU crypto regulation advisory
  • Token Classification: Legal opinion (utility vs security)
  • Payment/Fraud Compliance: protection against payment abuse (as needed)
  • IP Protection: trademark registration, brand protection
  • Tax Advisory: multi-jurisdiction tax structure

💰 Emergency Reserve (5%)

5%
  • Bug Bounties: Security research rewards
  • Unexpected Costs: Emergency patches, hotfixes
  • Opportunity Fund: Strategic partnerships
