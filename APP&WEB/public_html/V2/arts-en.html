What is Freedom Arts

I craft objects that behave like portals. Every canvas, board, or sound is blessed with mantra and linked to the ZION grid. The intention is to bring beauty, function, and spiritual protection into homes, oases, and community hubs.

Collections are produced at a slow-fashion pace: no mass printing, only handwork, limited runs, and transparent production. 10% of every sale flows into the Terra Nova ® humanitarian fund.

"Art is a prayer in motion. Breathe, create, share." — ZION Collective

Woodcarving WoodWork

Each board is hand-etched and layered with pigments and natural oils. I work with reclaimed wood from permaculture projects, carving mandalas, runes, and Tibetan mudras.

  • 🌲 Organic wood, sourced locally
  • 🔥 Hand-burning plus laser for micro detail
  • 🛡️ Finished with beeswax & resin
  • 🌀 Mantras infused in every artifact
