1. Introduction

ZION Terra Nova (hereinafter "ZION" or "the project") is an experimental open-source initiative that combines decentralized technologies with philosophical and spiritual principles. The project serves as a space for research, creation, and sharing of conscious technologies.

💡 ZION is not a traditional cryptocurrency or investment product. It is a technological experiment focused on supporting the conscious development of individuals and communities.

2. Nature of the Project

The ZION project is:

  • Experimental – In the phase of active development, testing, and iterations
  • Open-source – All code is publicly available for inspection and modification
  • Community-driven – Developed and managed by a decentralized community
  • Non-profit in spirit – The primary goal is not profit, but positive impact

⚠️ Important Notice: All participation in the project is at your own risk. The project provides no guarantees regarding functionality, stability, or future development.

3. Not an Investment

ZION Terra Nova explicitly rejects any perception as an investment opportunity:

  • ZION digital units are not securities or investment instruments
  • The project promises no financial returns or appreciation
  • Participation in the project should not be motivated by financial gain
  • The value of digital units may be zero at any time

If you are looking for an investment opportunity, ZION is not for you. The project is intended for those who want to actively contribute to the development of conscious technologies and community.

4. Blockchain and Digital Units

ZION uses its own blockchain built on the Proof-of-Work principle with the following characteristics:

  • Cosmic Harmony – A proprietary mining algorithm supporting decentralization
  • Consciousness Mining – A gamified system rewarding positive participation
  • Humanitarian Fund – 10% of each block goes to charitable purposes

ZION digital units primarily serve as:

  • A means for participation in DAO governance
  • A reward for contributing to the community
  • An experimental medium for tokenomics research

5. Open-Source License

All ZION project code is available under an open-source license on GitHub. This means that:

  • Anyone can view, study, and audit the code
  • Anyone can fork and modify the code for their own purposes
  • Anyone can contribute to the official repository
  • The code is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind

🔗 GitHub: github.com/Zion-TerraNova

6. Conscious Usage

Participants in the ZION project commit to a conscious and responsible approach:

  • Educate yourself – Understand the technology before using it
  • Don't risk more than you can afford – Experimental projects carry risks
  • Contribute positively – The community grows through constructive contributions
  • Respect others – Diversity of opinions enriches the project

7. Project Philosophy

"Technology without love is just engineering. Technology with love is magic."

ZION Terra Nova is built on the following pillars:

  • Consciousness over profit – Spiritual and personal development is more important than financial metrics
  • Transparency – Open communication and public code
  • Decentralization – No central authority, the community decides
  • Harmony – Technology should serve nature, not work against it

8. Privacy

The ZION project respects the privacy of all participants:

  • Mining and using the blockchain does not require identification
  • Websites use minimal tracking only for technical purposes
  • No personal data is sold to third parties
  • Community participation can be anonymous

9. Final Words

ZION Terra Nova is more than a project – it is an experiment in conscious technology creation. We guarantee nothing except a sincere effort to create something positive for the world.

Thank you for being part of this journey. Whether as a miner, developer, or simply someone who believes in a better future – your participation matters.
