This complaint procedure governs the mutual rights and obligations of the seller and buyer in the exercise of rights arising from defective performance (complaints).

2. Warranty Conditions

2.1 Warranty Period

  • 24 months for consumers (individuals purchasing outside business)
  • 12 months for entrepreneurs (legal entities and self-employed)
  • The warranty period starts on the day of receipt of goods by the buyer
⚠️ Attention: For digital content (ZION tokens, digital keys, software), warranty applies only to proven technical defects, not change of mind.

2.2 Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does not cover defects:

  • Caused by improper use or maintenance
  • Caused by mechanical damage by the buyer
  • Resulting from force majeure
  • Caused by unauthorized intervention in the goods
  • For used goods marked as "used" or "B-stock"

3. How to File a Complaint

3.1 Methods of Filing Complaints

  1. By email: shop@zionterranova.com
  2. By form: Fill out the complaint form on our website
  3. By mail: Send via registered mail with defect description and copy of purchase receipt

3.2 What to Include in Your Complaint

When filing a complaint, please provide:

  • ✅ Order number (e.g., ORD-1736122900-abc)
  • ✅ Purchase date
  • ✅ Defect description (what exactly doesn't work)
  • ✅ Photos of the defect (if visible)
  • ✅ Your proposed solution (repair, replacement, refund)
  • ✅ Contact details (email, phone)

4. Complaint Processing Deadlines

⏱️ Legal Deadlines:
  • 30 days - standard complaint processing period
  • 3 business days - we will inform you of receipt
  • Within 7 days - refund to account (after approval)

If we do not process the complaint within 30 days, you have the right to withdraw from the contract or demand a price reduction.

5. Complaint Resolution Options

5.1 According to Law, You Can Request

  • 🔧 Defect removal (repair)
  • 🔄 Replacement with defect-free goods
  • 💰 Reasonable price discount
  • 🔙 Withdrawal from contract (refund)

5.2 Refunds

If the complaint is justified and you choose withdrawal from contract:

  • We will refund within 7 business days from complaint approval
  • Refund via same payment method you used (unless stated otherwise)
  • For ZION tokens: refund in CZK at purchase date rate

6. Right of Withdrawal (14 Days)

🛒 Important for Online Purchases:
You have the right to withdraw from the contract within 14 days without giving a reason (pursuant to § 1829 of the Civil Code).

6.1 How to Withdraw

  1. Send us an email to shop@zionterranova.com
  2. State the order number and reason for withdrawal (optional)
  3. Send the undamaged goods back to our address
  4. We will refund within 14 days from goods receipt

6.2 Exceptions (When You Cannot Withdraw)

Right of withdrawal does not apply to:

  • ❌ Digital content (ZION tokens, keys) - after wallet delivery
  • ❌ Software licenses - after activation
  • ❌ Custom-made goods produced specifically for you
  • ❌ Sealed goods - after unsealing (health/hygiene reasons)
