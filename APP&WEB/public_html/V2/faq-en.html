What is ZION TerraNova?

ZION is a consciousness-based blockchain ecosystem that combines technical excellence with an ethical mission. We use our own Proof-of-Work algorithm called Cosmic Harmony and a unique Consciousness Mining system that rewards not only computing power but also the spiritual evolution of miners.

Fair Launch means ZION has no presale, no ICO, and no KYC. Inspired by Bitcoin and Satoshi Nakamoto — everyone has the opportunity to mine from the first block under the same conditions. 88.69% of the total supply will be mined by the community over 45 years.

Mining & Rewards

How do I start mining ZION?

Simple 3 steps:

  • 1. Download miner from zionterranova.com/download
  • 2. Connect to pool: 62.171.141.136:8444
  • 3. Enter your ZION wallet address and start mining

Full guide available on Start Mining page.

What hardware do I need for mining?

ZION uses ASIC-resistant algorithms, so you can mine on regular hardware:

  • CPU: Any modern processor (Deeksha optimized)
  • GPU: AMD or NVIDIA graphics card for higher hashrate
  • RAM: Minimum 4GB, recommended 8GB+

You can mine on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

What is Consciousness Mining?

Consciousness Mining is a unique reward system that recognizes 9 levels of consciousness. The higher the level, the bigger the bonus to mining rewards:

  • PHYSICAL — 1.0× multiplier (base)
  • MENTAL — 1.1× multiplier
  • COSMIC — 2.0× multiplier
  • ON_THE_STAR — up to 10× multiplier (master)

You earn XP through active mining, community participation, and spiritual activities.
