Why ®evoluZion

The project was born in 2008 as a rebellion against mass fashion. Every piece carries a prayer, a mantra, and a trace of handwork. EvoluZion v2 keeps the original DNA yet adds rasta futurism, digital identity, and merch for new light cities.

Each drop merges regenerative materials, sacred geometry, and modern technology. Collections are released in a slow-fashion rhythm.
