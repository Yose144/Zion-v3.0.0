 I am writing another article after a very long winter break in the Czech Republic, where I dealt with moving and other unpleasant issues. To summarize the year 2023, where my friend Kamil stayed on land in Portugal and I had to return to the Czech Republic to sort out all the problems. We were with Kamil for Christmas and we bought a wood stove, so he coped with the winter, but not completely safe because he was heating with wet wood and got soot inside the chimney, so he burnt out a little. Fortunately everything turned out well. This year I took a Junior Frond end developer course, and learned the basics of html,css,javascript. So I did a lot of work on the pc, and finally did some first draft of web pages. The vision is slowly emerging, but we lack the resources. Nevertheless, the development in Portugal will be a long run...

Anyway, I went to Portugal again on 6.5.2024, this time I flew to Porto and decided to go from Avier to Fugier de Foz by bike, atlancic coaste road 15. I have to say it was great, in Avier I bought a bike with landing gear and backpacks in the decathlon and off I went. I camped in the smaller mountains near the fugiera. The night under the grey sky was beautiful and the local virgin nature was fascinating, like somewhere in the jungle, only a pity that the waterfall I was looking forward to was not flowing. However, the next day I went on, but I was hit by a strong heat wave (again) and so I took the train the rest of the way, the sun would have burned me to dust. But I looked at the Pogtugal cyclo routes and there are plenty of them. So definitely another great activity.

I had this idea of converting a motorhome... ...and I've got other ideas in my head. I'm quite keen on cyclo camping. It's a great adventure. I recommend
