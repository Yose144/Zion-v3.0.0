Our Vision

Right now we are planting new trees and expanding the land to create a thriving, sustainable farm. Our goal is to grow a diverse range of organic crops while supporting the natural biodiversity of the place.

Looking ahead, we plan to open a surf school that connects love for nature, the ocean and movement. We want to offer a space where you can live actively and at the same time deeply feel the planet that carries us.

Join us at Terra Nova and become part of the journey towards a more sustainable and adventurous future.
