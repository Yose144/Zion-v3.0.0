🛒 Shopping Guide

Everything you need to know before ordering from the ZION eShop

🛍️

Step-by-step purchase flow

1

Browse the catalog

Explore handcrafted artefacts, seeds, engraving services, and digital drops.

2

Add to cart

Tap “Add to cart” on each product you want to reserve.

3

Review your basket

Open the cart, adjust quantities, and apply any vouchers.

4

Fill in delivery info

Enter shipping address, contact details, and (optionally) your ZION wallet.

5

Pick shipping

Choose Packeta pickup, courier to door, or free local pickup.

6

Pay securely

Complete checkout via Stripe card, bank transfer, or COD. Confirmation arrives by email.

Bonus tip:
Enter your ZION wallet to receive loyalty tokens for every fulfilled order. No wallet yet? We will generate one and send it via QR.
💳

Payment methods

Method Description Fee
Card (Stripe) Immediate processing with Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, or Google Pay. Free
Bank transfer Pay manually using the order number as the variable symbol. We dispatch once funds arrive. Free
Cash on delivery Pay the courier or pickup point when the parcel is handed over. +30 CZK

Pro tip

Card payments are handled instantly, which moves your order to the crafting queue right away.

ZION token loyalty bonus

Every product carries a loyalty multiplier. Once your order is confirmed, we log the matching amount of ZION tokens for you.

Once mainnet launches we transfer the tokens to your on-chain address.
