# Nová Amerika — Costa Rica

> **L5 Free World · sixth Terra Nova node** · Status: 🔵 Vision / Preparation
> *"A bridge between two continents and two oceans — and a bridge between the first nations and the future we write together."*

---

## Intent

**Nová Amerika** is the sixth and final planned node of the L5 Free World layer. Unlike the other nodes, it is not centred on a single community but on the **native cultures of both Americas** — the indigenous nations whose territories, languages and knowledge predate the states they live in today.

Costa Rica was chosen deliberately:

- **Geographic midpoint** — the land bridge between North and South America; the place where the two Americas touch, symbolically and practically.
- **Living indigenous territories** — Bribri and Cabécar (Talamanca), Boruca, Ngäbe-Buglé, Maleku, Huetar, Chorotega. Eight officially recognised indigenous territories.
- **Stability and nature** — no army since 1948, ~98% renewable energy, a quarter of the land protected.
- **Accessible land** — rural soil remains affordable compared to Europe or Polynesia.
- **Two ocean fronts** — the Pacific and the Caribbean on one territory.

## Principle: listen first, then build

Nová Amerika is not a project *for* indigenous communities — it is a project **with them**. Ground rules:

1. **FPIC** — Free, Prior and Informed Consent. No activity on or near an indigenous territory without the free, prior and informed consent of its council of elders.
2. **The community holds the keys** — infrastructure (solar, water, mesh) belongs to the community, not the fund. ZION is a tool, not a condition.
3. **No land tokenisation** — the project sells no soil, shares or "tokenised earth".
4. **A living library** — languages, seeds, medicinal plants and stories are documented only with their holders' consent and remain their property.

## What the node holds (vision)

- **Council of elders** — an advisory and decision-making frame; representatives of partner territories sit at the table from day one, not after construction.
- **Seed Library of the Americas** — cacao, maize, beans, squash and local varieties; exchange with other L5 nodes.
- **Permaculture farm** — syntropic and agroforestry systems inspired by traditional Costa Rican cultivation.
- **Medical Table** — bridging traditional medicine and L5 holistic protocols — provided that knowledge stays in community ownership.
- **LoRa mesh + Guardian Node** — off-grid communication and block validation, the same 90/10 split as all L5 nodes.
- **Residency programme** — guests from the territories, students, craftspeople; exchange stays with Te Pīko Ora (Polynesia) and other nodes.

## Shared land with L6

On the same plot (or an adjacent parcel) the **L6 Issobella ground segment** is planned — a research campus with antennas, a lab and an observatory. L5 and L6 share land, water, energy and mesh — but each has its own fund, governance and budget. Details: *Costa Rica Ground Station* in the L6 library.

## What this project is not

- Not a claim on indigenous territory — the project buys private land *outside* the territories and builds partnerships.
- Not a reservation or an ethnographic display — it is a living community, not an exhibit.
- Not an investment — no gate, gift or stay carries a return.
- Not a finished thing — today it is intent and preparation; no land has been purchased.
