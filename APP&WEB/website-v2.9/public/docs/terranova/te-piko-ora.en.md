# Te Pīko Ora — Crown · Raiatea, French Polynesia

The third physical Terra Nova node in the Pacific — the crown, the full manifestation, the place where the oldest ocean, the coconut palm and Polynesian distributed consensus meet.

Te Pīko Ora is designed as a **regenerative oceanic sanctuary**. Its heart is the **living piko** — the umbilical cord of every visitor planted in the soil, from which the tree of life grows. The va'a canoe, the marae and the wayfinding school form a moving circle without a captain.

## Concept

The site grows from the philosophy of Te Pīko Ora: **ocean + palm + canoe + community + ZION**.

- **Tree of life (nī — coconut palm)** — provides food, water, oil, fibre, fuel and shade.
- **Lagoon and reef** — a protected womb where marine life is born and coral is restored.
- **Va'a (canoe)** — everyone paddles, no single captain. DAO governance as wayfinding.
- **Marae Taputapuātea** — UNESCO World Heritage, cosmological centre of Polynesia.
- **Wayfinding school** — navigation by stars, waves, wind and birds; distributed consensus in practice.
- **Marine permaculture** — vāvā ponds, limu farms, pearl oysters, closed-loop coconut-fish-taro cycle.
- **Solar, rainwater and off-grid** — 100% renewable energy in a tropical paradise.

## Symbolism

- **Piko** — umbilical cord, centre, the point connecting human, ancestors and land.
- **Coconut palm (nī)** — tree of life, gives everything.
- **Lagoon (roto)** — womb, protected space of abundance.
- **Ocean (moana)** — cosmic connectedness, the great void that links islands.
- **Stars (fetu'u)** — map, consensus without a central GPS.
- **Tatau** — skin records, an immutable genealogy ledger.

## Construction and materials

- **Local timber and bamboo** — ohue, mi'o, coconut wood, corrugated iron on wooden frames.
- **Natural fabrics and thatch** — pandanus leaves, local textiles, passive shading.
- **Stone from rivers and reefs** — walls, terraces, paths.
- **Solar panels integrated into roofs** — tuned to the landscape.
- **Rainwater and greywater** — cisterns, root-zone treatment, closed-loop cycle.
- **Bamboo and canvas canoes** — traditional and modern va'a for school voyages.

## Energy and ecological system

- **Solar 10 kWp + 20 kWh LiFePO4** (Phase 1) → 40 kWp + 100 kWh (Phase 3) + floating solar.
- **Rainwater 50 m³ → 300 m³ cisterns**, supplemented by springs and, if needed, solar RO.
- **Marine permaculture** — vāvā ponds, limu, pearl oysters, fish traps, reef restoration.
- **Coconut oil as biodiesel** — 100 palms = ~400 L oil/year for backup generators.
- **Local varieties and seed library** — exchange with Genesis Garden, Dharma Temple, Bohemia and Bodhi Lanka.
