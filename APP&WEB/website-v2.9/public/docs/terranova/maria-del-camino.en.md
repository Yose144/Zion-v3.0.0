# María del Camino — the Ship of the Way (Sea Camino · Solar Sails)

> **L5 Free World · eighth point of Terra Nova** · Status: 🔵 Preparation — vessel in research phase
> *"Where the old world ended at the sea, the new one sails on."*

---

## Intent

**María del Camino** is the eighth point of the L5 Free World layer — and the only one that is not a place. It is a **way**: a sailing vessel for roughly fifty people that physically connects all seven fixed nodes of the network.

Every Terra Nova node carries one principle into the network — Genesis carries the garden, Dharma the temple, Te Pīko Ora the ocean, Bohemia governance, Bodhi Lanka akasha, LUMI the bridge between worlds, Uluru memory. **María del Camino carries connection itself** — the circulation between the points. Seed libraries, cultural archives, people and grants move between nodes across the sea, not by courier.

## The name

The ship is named for **María del Camino** — *Mary of the Way*, the Marian devotion whose sanctuary stands directly on the Camino Francés near León. For a thousand years pilgrims walked the Camino de Santiago; those who went all the way continued to **Finisterre** — *finis terrae*, the end of the earth — where the road stopped at the ocean and there was nowhere left to walk.

**María del Camino is that one step further**: the Camino that does not end at the sea but continues on it. Its prologue route even passes the three Marian sites woven into the network's own story — Pontevedra, Fátima and La Palma.

> *"Ultreia et suseia — onward and upward."* — the pilgrims' greeting of the Codex Calixtinus, the ship's motto.

## What the vessel holds (concept)

- **Solar sails** — the sails themselves are photovoltaic: flexible solar cells laminated into sailcloth (est. 60–200 kWp), plus hydro-regeneration under sail. Target: fossil-free passage.
- **~50 souls** — a permanent Guardian crew plus rotating residents: researchers, youth-bridge programmes, Medical Table practitioners, new Guardians in transit between nodes.
- **Self-sufficient** — desalination and rain catchment, hydroponic deck garden, provisions exchanged at the nodes — that is what the route is for.
- **Guardian node at sea** — a full ZION node validating over satellite, a LoRa/mesh gateway at the mast, and a treasury the ship can physically deliver: humanitarian cargo between nodes.
- **Pilgrim credential** — each leg is an *etapa*; crew and residents collect on-chain stamps like the pilgrim's *credencial*, with a *compostela* for completing the great circuit.

## Route — the Great Circle (vision)

The prologue is the **sea Camino** itself: Finisterre → Pontevedra → Fátima → La Palma (María de las Nieves, patroness of the island that hosts the Dharma Temple node). Then the main circuit: the Algarve (Genesis Garden) → Costa Rica (LUMI) → French Polynesia (Te Pīko Ora) → Aboriginal Australia (Uluru — visiting custodian communities on their terms, no claims) → Sri Lanka (Bodhi Lanka) → home.

## Why now

A network of seven fixed points is a constellation. A constellation needs a traveller to become a story — someone has to walk, or sail, between the stars. The ship makes the L5 promise physical: not a map of sites, but a living route. On the ship the whole pentagram is aboard in miniature — a garden, a temple, the ocean, a council, the archive — so whoever steps aboard has already arrived everywhere.
