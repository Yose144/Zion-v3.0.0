## Intent

**Ekam** is the tenth point of the L5 Free World layer — and the only one that physically exists. The Temple of Oneness near the village of Varadaiahpalem in Andhra Pradesh, India opened on **22 April 2008** as the Oneness Temple (later the Temple of the Supreme Light; since 2018 **Ekam** — Sanskrit for "One"). We do not build it and we do not own it — **we form a relationship** with the place that gave the ZION network its name: the mainnet proof-of-work is called `ekam_deeksha` and the network's genesis words read *"Om Namo Hiranyagarbha & Ekam Deeksha !"*.

For the network Ekam plays two roles: **the summit of the Great Route** (the initiation of completion) and **the template** — living proof that a temple designed as an environment for consciousness can actually be built.

## Architecture as template

The temple was not designed as the shrine of one tradition — it was built so that any inner experience could deepen within it. That is the principle every Terra Nova node carries:

- **Golden ratio and orientation** — proportions built on φ, Vaastu alignment to the cardinal directions, a geobiologically chosen site.
- **Column-free hall** — one uninterrupted space holding hundreds of people in a single visual and acoustic field; the pattern for every node's hall.
- **Three floors as an architecture of initiation** — preparation → aspiration → liberation; the same sequence the Great Route's initiations follow.
- **Water "island"** — a building in symbiosis with the water element; inspiration for the water design of other nodes.

*A note on precision: descriptions like "the temple as a generator of consciousness" are the Oneness lineage's teaching; L5 draws design principles from them (proportion, space, ritual framing), not empirical claims.*

## Role on the Great Route

**Initiation 7 — Completion (Satori).** The route: Bodhi Lanka → across the Palk Strait (~700 km, the Rama Setu line) → anchorage at Chennai → ~73 km overland to the Ekam campus. The pilgrim "completes" the journey here — yet the credential stamp is earned only by returning: *"After enlightenment — chop wood, carry water."* The Great Route continues across the Indian Ocean and Boa Esperança back home.

## Ethics of the relationship

Ekam works on the same model as Uluru and Boa Esperança — a **destination node**, not a capital project:

- The temple is operated by the Oneness/Ekam organisation; ZION does not build on it or own it.
- Pilgrim visits happen on the operator's terms; no claim on programmes, symbols or heritage.
- Registry: `vision`, zero allocation — the node's value lies in relationship and precedent, not investment.
