# Golden Republic Bohemia — Heart · Bohemia, Czech Republic

The fourth node of the L5 Free World Terra Nova — a governance laboratory for the Golden Republic in the land where the salt of the earth was born, where the king was a ploughman, and where an emperor built a bridge between heaven and earth.

Golden Republic Bohemia is designed as a **regenerative governance sanctuary** in the heart of Europe. Its heart is not a farm, nor a temple, nor a lagoon — it is the **circle**, where decisions are made. A place where Czech wisdom (salt, bridge, university, Golden Bull) meets the ZION protocol (DAO, Guardian Node, Proof-of-Care).

## Concept

The site grows from the Golden Republic philosophy: **soil + bridge + knowledge + governance + ZION**.

- **Říp as the axis** — the mountain where Libuše sent for the ploughman Přemysl. A symbiotic boundary between myth and soil.
- **Circle of decision-making at the center** — a stone amphitheater under the open sky, where the community circle gathers. No throne, no pulpit. Just a circle, salt on the table, and a fire in the middle.
- **Three pavilions around the circle** — Bridge (governance), University (knowledge), Golden Bull (protocol). Each with a different function, all with one intention.
- **Permacultural landscape around** — Czech heritage varieties, orchards, herb gardens, bees. Soil that feeds the circle.
- **Off-grid energy and water** — solar, rainwater, spring. Self-sufficiency as a condition of free decision-making.
- **Říp and the Elbe valley in the background** — a landscape where Czech mythology, rivers, and fields meet.

The pavilions are not Gothic replicas. They are **original contemporary structures** that visually evoke a bridge/triad and relate to the sacred geometry of Karlštejn's floor plan and the Golden Bull — without copying any existing symbol.

## Symbolism

- **Salt on the table** — a covenant that holds without words. "Salt of the covenant" (*berit melach*) — what we have tasted together, holds even without paper. ZION as salt: it does not rule the meal, it guarantees that the rules hold.
- **Bridge** — Charles IV laid the foundation stone of Charles Bridge at an astrologically precise moment (1-3-5-7-9-7-5-3-1). A bridge connects, it does not divide. ZION is a bridge between communities, not a wall.
- **University** — Charles University (1348), the first in Europe open to all nations. Education as a right, not a commodity.
- **Golden Bull** — the constitution of the Holy Roman Empire (1356), which prevented civil wars over imperial elections. Written rules, distributed power, stability through structure. A proto-DAO.
- **Ploughman and prophetess** — Přemysl the Ploughman, whom Libuše called from the field to the throne. "Salt of the earth" — those who are forgotten until they are needed. A king who remembers he held a hoe.
- **Circle without a throne** — in the Golden Republic there is no captain. There is a circle. Everyone paddles, no one commands.
- **Linden tree** — the tree of Slavic sovereignty. Under a linden, decisions were made long before parliaments. Linden tea, linden honey, linden wood — a tree that has been with Bohemia for a thousand years.

## Construction and Materials

- **Sandstone and wood** — Czech local materials, thermal mass, tradition of stone masonry.
- **Glass facades of pavilions** — transparency of governance, passive lighting, crystalline geometry.
- **Rammed earth and hemp insulation** — earthen building, local resources.
- **Wooden terraces and walkways** — connecting pavilions and the circle, evoking a bridge.
- **Solar panels integrated into roofs** and terrain.
- **Rainwater and greywater** — closed water loop.
- **Composting toilets and reed-bed filter** — closed nutrient cycle.

- **Guardian Node** — block validation, LoRa relay, Starlink + 4G failover; split 90% operator / 10% community treasury.
- **Multisig treasury** — 3-of-5, operations 2-of-3, reserve 3-of-5 cold, tithe and seed library fund.

- **Medical Table** — herbal pavilion, first aid, integrative medicine, Czech herbal tradition.
- **LoRa / Mesh network** — off-grid communication, telemetry, emergency broadcast.
- **Proof-of-Care** — rewards for genuine care of soil, community, and governance documentation.
- **Humanitarian Tithe** — 10% of node rewards goes to local and global humanitarian projects.
- **Golden Republic protocol** — governance laboratory: the first physical node where the voluntary protocol of coexistence from `TerraNova/06-L5-SVOBODA` §6.6 is prototyped.
