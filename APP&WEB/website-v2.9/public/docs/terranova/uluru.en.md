# Uluru — Australia (Tjukurpa · Songlines)

> **L5 Free World · seventh Terra Nova point** · Status: 🟣 Vision — listen first
> *"The land is not something we own. The land is something we are."*

---

## Intent

**Uluru** is the seventh point of the L5 Free World layer — and deliberately a **vision, not a building plan**. The red monolith at the heart of Australia is the sacred home of the Aṉangu, the traditional custodians, and of Tjukurpa — the Dreaming that carries law, stories and a map of the land.

Aboriginal Australia holds the **oldest living culture on the planet** — more than 60,000 years of unbroken continuity between people and landscape. A Terra Nova map without it was incomplete: the voice of the antipodes was missing, a heritage and a message no other continent can offer.

## Principle: a node as a relationship, not a site

Uluru is not land to be built on. Climbing it has been closed since 2019, and its future is decided by the Aṉangu within the Uluru-Kata Tjuṯa National Park (UNESCO — both natural and cultural heritage). The node therefore comes into being differently:

1. **FPIC** — Free, Prior and Informed Consent. No activity without the free, prior and informed consent of the traditional custodians.
2. **Knowledge stays with its carriers** — stories, languages and land knowledge are documented only with consent and remain owned by the communities that carry them.
3. **No tokenisation of heritage** — the project does not sell, tokenise or display sacred stories.
4. **A message, not a property** — L5 does not come to acquire the culture, but to listen to it and carry it onward as part of a shared map of humanity.

## What the node holds (vision)

- **Tjukurpa — the Dreaming** — the law of the ancestors as a continuing present: the relation between people, land and story.
- **Songlines** — singing tracks carrying map and message across the entire continent; the oldest message network on Earth.
- **Kanyini** — connectedness and the responsibility to care for land, family and story. The principle the L5 humanitarian tithe embodies in protocol.
- **Council of custodians** — the Aṉangu and other traditional owners sit at the table from day one.
- **Cultural grants** — L5 tools for Aboriginal-led initiatives: languages, land care, traditional fire.

## Why now

Every Terra Nova node carries one principle into the network: Genesis carries the garden, Dharma the temple, Te Pīko Ora the ocean, Bohemia governance, Bodhi Lanka akasha, LUMI the bridge between worlds. **Uluru carries memory** — sixty thousand years proving that humanity can live with the land for the long term. That is exactly the message the Free World needs from the antipodes.
