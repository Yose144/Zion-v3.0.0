## Intent

**Amenti** is the twelfth and final node of the L5 Free World layer — closing the network at the place where the record first became sacred. The **Giza plateau** — the Great Pyramid of Khufu, Khafre, Menkaure and the Great Sphinx — is the last surviving Wonder of the ancient world: ~4,500 years of a single record that outlived dynasties, religions and technologies. And beneath the sand, in the mythic geography of the network, lie the **Halls of Amenti** from the Emerald Tablets of Thoth — the hall of records where the 32 Children of Light keep humanity's wisdom around the Flower of Light.

This is not a node on the ocean route — it sits by the Nile, ~180 km from the port of Alexandria. It is the **capstone of the way**: after the return home (initiation 10) and learning to govern (11, Bohemia) comes the twelfth initiation — **Remembrance**. The pilgrim does not ask what else to discover; the pilgrim asks what was remembered.

## Principle: the record that does not sink

Every node carries one initiation. Amenti carries **Remembrance**:

- **The last Wonder** — the Great Pyramid is the only surviving Wonder of the ancient world. No node has served longer: stone as humanity's longest-running blockchain.
- **The halls of records (MYTH)** — beneath the surface, in the network's story, lie the halls that did not sink with Atlantis, because they stood not on what sinks but on a principle that can be written again.
- **The credential stamp** — *"I remembered"* — the capstone: after every leg, the pilgrim's record is written into the halls; the circle that began in Pontevedra closes.

### The Halls of Amenti — honesty about the Tablets (MYTH)

As the corpus chapter itself says (`NirvanaCloud/05`): **the Emerald Tablets of Thoth are a modern esoteric text** — not an Egyptological artefact. We read them as literary archetype, not historical evidence — the same distinction we use for the Infant Jesus aboard Tres Marias.

The image the Tablets offer is precise nonetheless: after the fall of Atlantis (in the myth — a civilisation sinking under its own pride) the **Halls** were built beneath the surface — not to hide power but to keep wisdom until humanity needs it again. At the centre burns the **Flower of Light** — and around it sit the **32 Children of Light** at thirty-two thrones: "human in body, yet immortal in consciousness." Above them watch the **Seven Lords of Space-Time**.

## Custodial framework

The Giza plateau is a protected archaeological site under the **Supreme Council of Antiquities** — and Egypt is a living country with Islamic and Coptic layers, not an ancient-world backdrop. The node therefore means **relationship, not presence**: no parcel, no construction on the plateau, no programme without the custodians' consent.

And the corpus's Egyptological ethics apply (`TerraNova/ancient-egypt/01-RAMEC`): the academic frame is primary; the Emerald Tablets are always marked as a modern literary text; no "pharaoh loot" or "Atlantis was Egypt" copy; hieroglyphs only with verified meaning; no promises about players' afterlife.
