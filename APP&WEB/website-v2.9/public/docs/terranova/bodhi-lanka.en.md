# Bodhi Lanka — Akasha · Sri Lanka

The fifth node of the L5 Trinity Terra Nova — where the infinite love of Rama and Sita meets the oldest living tree on Earth and the ZION protocol.

Bodhi Lanka is designed as an **awakened sanctuary of ether** in the heart of the Indian Ocean. Its heart is not a farm, nor a temple, nor a lagoon — it is a **tree** that has lived for 2,300 years. A place where Sri Lankan wisdom (Bodhi tree, Ramayana, Ayurveda, Rama Setu) meets the ZION protocol (DAO, Guardian Node, Proof-of-Care).

## Concept

The site grows from the philosophy of awakened Lanka: **love + awakening + tree + ocean + ZION**.

- **Sri Maha Bodhi as the axis** — the oldest living planted tree in the world (288 BCE), a cutting from the original Bodhi tree under which the Buddha attained awakening. A living axis, not a concept.
- **Rama Setu (Adam's Bridge)** — a bridge of stone blocks from India to Lanka, which Rama built to rescue Sita. A connection of two lands across the ocean.
- **Lotus pond at the center** — reflection, purification, continuity. The lotus rooted in mud, blooming in light.
- **Three meditation pavilions** — Prema (love/bhakti), Bodhi (awakening/dharma), Setu (bridge/connection). Each with a different function, all with one intention.
- **Tropical jungle and Ayurvedic garden** — Sri Lankan biodiversity, medicinal plants, food forest.
- **Off-grid energy and water** — solar, rainwater, springs. Self-sufficiency as a condition of the freedom to awaken.

The pavilions are not replicas of temples. They are **original contemporary structures** that visually evoke a lotus, a bridge, and a triad, and relate to the sacred geometry of the Sri Yantra and the mandala of love — without copying any existing symbol.

## Symbolism

- **Bodhi tree** — awakening, Tree of Life, living axis. Unlike the Dharma Temple, where the tree is a concept, here it is a real tree — a cutting from the one under which the Buddha awoke. A tree that remembers more than any dynasty.
- **Rama and Sita** — infinite love, atma and paramatma, bhakti yoga. Rama crossed the ocean for Sita; Sita held the earth in her palm and knew it was warm. Love as protocol: it does not rule, but it holds everything together.
- **Rama Setu** — a bridge between worlds, the connection of India and Lanka. ZION as a bridge: it does not divide, it connects. A bridge of stone blocks that still holds after millennia.
- **Lotus** — purification, awakening, root in mud, flower in light. ZION as lotus: it operates in an impure environment, but is not itself impure.
- **Akasha** — the fifth element, the space that holds all the others. Earth (Genesis), fire (Dharma), water (Te Pīko Ora), air (Bohemia) — and ether, which contains them all. Love as ether: not by occupying space, but by permeating it.
- **Sri Yantra** — sacred geometry, the mandala of love. Nine interlocking triangles converging to a single point — the bindu, the source. The site plan as a living mandala.

## Construction and Materials

- **Teak wood and stone** — tropical local materials, thermal mass, tradition of Sri Lankan masonry.
- **Glass facades of pavilions** — transparency of governance, passive lighting, crystalline geometry of the lotus.
- **Rammed earth and bamboo insulation** — earthen building, local resources, tropical ventilation.
- **Bamboo terraces and walkways** — connecting pavilions and the tree, evoking a bridge.
- **Solar panels integrated into roofs** and terrain.
- **Rainwater and greywater** — closed water loop, monsoon as a source.
- **Composting toilets and reed-bed filter** — closed nutrient cycle.
