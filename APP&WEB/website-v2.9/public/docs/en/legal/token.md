# Token Not Security — ZION TerraNova

**Statement on the nature of ZION tokens**

---

## Summary

ZION tokens **are not securities** under the Howey test or any other regulatory framework known to us. They are native Proof-of-Work mining rewards, which makes them the payment token of the blockchain.

---

## Howey test — ZION does not meet the criteria

The Howey test (USA SEC) defines a security as an investment of money in a common enterprise with an **expectation of profit derived solely from the efforts of others**. ZION tokens do not meet this definition because:

| Criterion | ZION |
|-----------|------|
| Investment of money | ❌ ZION is not sold; it is mined through Proof-of-Work |
| Common enterprise | ❌ The network is decentralized and has no central issuer |
| Expectation of profit | ❌ ZION value depends on the miner's own work, electricity, and hardware, not solely on others |
| Efforts of others | ❌ Every miner controls their own performance |

---

## Characteristics of ZION tokens

ZION tokens are:

- ✅ **Mining rewards** — the result of Proof-of-Work computation
- ✅ **A means of payment** on the ZION network for transaction fees
- ✅ **A utility token** for access to the L2 wZION bridge, L3 DAO voting, and L2 DeFi
- ✅ **Decentralized** — no central issuer and no admin key

ZION tokens are **not**:

- ❌ Shares, bonds, or other investment instruments
- ❌ A deposit or savings product
- ❌ Collateral guaranteed by any institution
- ❌ A security under EU MiCA, the U.S. Securities Act, or UK FSMA

---

## EU MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets)

Under the EU MiCA regulation, effective from 2024, utility tokens and PoW-mined tokens fall into the **crypto-assets** category outside the strictest regime when they:

- are not pegged to fiat currency,
- are not issued by a central institution.

ZION satisfies both conditions.
