# Risk Disclosure — ZION TerraNova

**Important notice:** Before participating in any activity related to the ZION blockchain, please read this document carefully.

---

## Risks of crypto assets

### 1. Technical risks

- **Code defects:** Although ZION has 780+ automated tests and has undergone a security audit, no software is free of bugs. A critical bug could lead to loss of funds or network outage.
- **Private key loss:** Losing your wallet private key is irreversible. The ZION team cannot recover lost funds.
- **Smart contract risks (L2):** The wZION ERC-20 bridge and DAO contracts are experimental. Bugs in smart contracts on Ethereum or Base may lead to loss of funds.

### 2. Market risks

- The value of ZION tokens may fall to zero.
- Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile.
- Past performance does not predict future value.
- Liquidity may be low, especially in the early post-mainnet phase.

### 3. Regulatory risks

- Cryptocurrency regulation varies by jurisdiction and changes over time.
- In some countries, mining or holding cryptocurrencies may be restricted or prohibited.
- Tax consequences of owning and mining cryptocurrencies are the responsibility of each user.

### 4. Operational risks

- Mining hardware wear and electricity costs may exceed mining rewards.
- Network hashrate changes over time, affecting mining profitability.
- Pool outages may cause temporary loss of rewards.

### 5. Protocol risks

- Hard forks may change network rules.
- The CHv4 upgrade requires an updated mining software stack.
- The Decade Decay model reduces block rewards every 10 years.

---

## Who should not participate

Do not proceed if:

- you cannot afford to lose the full amount committed,
- you require a guaranteed return on investment,
- you are unable to understand the technical aspects of PoW mining,
- you are in a jurisdiction where cryptocurrency is prohibited.

---

## User responsibility

Participation in the ZION network is **voluntary**. Every user is fully responsible for:

- securing their private keys,
- understanding the risks before participating,
- complying with applicable laws in their jurisdiction,
- handling tax reporting for mining and trading.
