## 2. Phases

### Phase 1 — Stabilisation (Feb–Mar 2026)

CHv3 single PoW, memory-hard fork, parallel dual-mining, VerusHash ARM64, unified pool validator, docs.

### Phase 2 — Audit & stress (Apr–Jun 2026)

Third-party or deep self-audit, bug bounty, large miner soak, reorg simulations, more seeds, explorer, desktop wallet.

### Phase 3 — Exchange & ecosystem (Jul–Sep 2026)

Listings prep, CoinGecko/CMC, whitepaper EN, mobile wallet, third-party pools, community growth.

### Phase 4 — Historical mainnet window (Oct–Dec 2026)

Genesis finalisation, premine distribution, seeds, mainnet config, DAO vote on emission, **historical target 31 Dec 2026**.
