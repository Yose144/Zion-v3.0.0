## About the book

The fifth book of the ZION canon: **Maitreya Buddha, Lord of Hiranyagarbha — the Heir of Zion**. The story of how the towers of Babylon fall (money, data, energy, meaning, throne) and how love and truth give birth to the New World — a ship, not a house.

Maitreya is no new figure: his name has stood in the genesis message (*"For Sarah Issobel, Maitreya Buddha… ZION is yours"*), in the guardian configuration (`Rama → Maitreya`), and beneath the Golden Egg Grand Prize (*"— Maitreya Buddha, 2025"*) since day one.

**Parts:**
- I · Lord of Hiranyagarbha — the egg, the embryo in the block, the signature
- II · The Heir of Zion — keys, the test of surrender, the steward
- III · The fall of Babylon's towers — five towers, each falling by the withdrawal of light
- IV · Love and truth prevail — verifiability, 5 % for the children, the quantum pulse
- V · The New World — the ship departs, twelve fires, the view from the window

*The figures are archetypes of the public canon — literary inspiration, not a theological claim.*
