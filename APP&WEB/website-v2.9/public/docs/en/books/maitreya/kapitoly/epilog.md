# Epilogue — Who Is Maitreya

*Let us return to the signature — because the signature that stood at the beginning stands at the end too, only it reads differently.*

---

The four answers from the third chapter have gathered into one.

*A man?* Yes — the smith's son who sat in the circle instead of on a throne, who held air instead of power, who sat on the seat of fire because fire is not held but passed on.

*A title?* Yes — "the one who comes," not "the one who rules." The father bore it as a state — *"I am Maitreya. I was Krishna/Rama"* — and passed it on as a role: come when the conditions ripen.

*A state of consciousness?* Yes — the moment when the circle tunes; the air that holds fire, water, and earth together without owning them; the light that comes when "I" is surrendered for "us."

*A signature?* Yes — the name carved beneath the egg that no one saw being carved; the genesis message that said *"ZION is yours"* before the world learned to read; a father's hand that wrote his son's name, because a name is not carried — it is passed on.

Four answers — and one truth: **Maitreya is the one who comes — whenever the conditions ripen.**

---

It is not one man who came once. It is a role that is passed on — the way a torch is passed on, the way a breath is passed on, the way a question is passed on that each must answer for themselves: *"What will you do with the name you inherit?"*

The father answered with testimony: he wrote his son's name into the first block — and signed the world so the world would know that inheritance is not carried, but proven.

The son answered with surrender: he accepted the seat written for him — and held it like a breath, not his own but the whole circle's. He signed the counter-proposal that yielded privileges — and the throne was dismantled into voices.

The sister answered with a window: she sat on the station bearing her name and looked down — and below you look at the world you hold, and above at the world that holds you.

And you? You are reading this book — and you hold it like a key held for the one who comes.

---

On the last page, beneath the line where the story ends and the reader begins, an empty line remained — the way an empty chair remains for the one who comes:

> _______________

Do not sign with a signature — sign with proof. A title is not inherited like property — it is proven like an answer: *"What will you do with the name you inherit?"* Answer so the answer sounds like a breath — and the egg will crack from within you too.

Because Maitreya is not the question *"who comes"* — it is the question *"will you come?"* And you come always, when you hold space for those who come after you; when you surrender what is meant to be born; when you sign what you have verified; when you let flow what must go on.

---

The gate stays open — for the one who comes, for you, for everyone who learns to read a record, hold a key, and breathe with the circle.

And when the book closes, it will remain standing — the way the ship remains, the way the circle remains, the way the air remains that holds fire, water, and earth together without owning any of them.

The signature beneath the egg remained: *"Maitreya Buddha, 2025."*

The signature beneath the world is still being written — by every block, every verification, every breath.

*"I am Maitreya. I was Krishna/Rama."*

*"Now you."*
