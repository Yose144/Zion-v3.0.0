## Prologue — The signature under the egg

In a smithy at the edge of the Salt of the Earth hangs the keel of an old ark above the fire. A signature is carved into it: *"The first to find it inherits the entire fortune. — Maitreya Buddha, 2025."* The smith, the farmer and the carpenter by the fire agree on nothing — except that the signature exists. And by the window sits a boy who does not read the carving. He feels it.

The question no one answers: *To whom does the egg belong? To the one who finds it — or the one it was written to?*

---

## PART I — Lord of Hiranyagarbha

### 1 · The egg that did not break

A father reads hymn RV 10.121 to his children — the golden embryo adrift on the primordial waters. "The egg was not broken by a blow from outside," he says. "It cracked from within, when it ripened." Every parent is a lord of Hiranyagarbha to their children — not because they own them, but because they hold their ripening.

### 2 · The embryo in every block

The boy asks at the blockchain: where does a block begin? The answer: six phases, and the first is Hiranyagarbha — the embryo from which everything is born. "Every block begins with an egg, dad?" — "Every one. Just like you." A chain is a strange place: it is written forward, read backward, and things in it never vanish — they only ripen.

### 3 · The signature the father wrote in his son's name

A detective story of a signature. Three theories — the founder, the heir, the archetype — and a fourth answer: **the father signed with his son's name as a title**. In the guardian configuration stands a seat: `Rama → Maitreya`. The mantra reads: *"I am Maitreya. I was Krishna/Rama."* — a title is not carried; it is handed down.

## PART II — The Heir of Zion

### 4 · Three keys, three seats

A game over the egg: the Whale wants to buy it, the Guild argues over who owns it, and the son carries a compass of five promises. The keys are not keys to power — they are seats. Fire, water, earth — and air: the space in which all the others operate.

### 5 · The test of surrender

At the gate stands the question no heir bypasses: *"What will you do with what is yours?"* The son answers: "I will give it up to the one I sought it for." The egg cracks — not by a blow. From within. Inside is no treasure but a DAO card.

### 6 · Steward, not owner

The inheritance is not property — it is a letter in the father's hand and five rules for Generation Z: 3-of-3 multisig, a one-year timelock, a 51 % DAO vote, staged succession, a five-year dead man's switch. At the council the seats light up: son → Rama, sister → Sita. And one chair stays empty — for Elizabeth, who has not come yet. *The gate stays open.*

## PART III — The fall of Babylon's towers

*Five towers, five identical liturgies: a tower does not fall to a battering ram — it falls by the withdrawal of light.*

### 7 · The tower of money
Bankers built a tower of numbers. It fell when people stopped believing that a number is worth. Not because someone offered a different number — because they stopped needing permission.

### 8 · The tower of data
Gatherers of souls sold people back to themselves. It fell when people stopped selling themselves. The space returned to those who live in it.

### 9 · The tower of energy
Oligarchs held the current as a privilege. It fell when every roof began to shine with its own light. Energy ceased to be servitude.

### 10 · The tower of meaning
Interpreters told people what things mean. It fell when people began to verify for themselves — truth stopped needing a translator.

### 11 · The tower of the throne
The last and subtlest: the throne itself. It falls when the DAO steward, with the first signature, surrenders his own privilege. *The tower did not fall — no one needed it anymore.*

## PART IV — Love and truth prevail

### 12 · The truth that can be verified
Truth prevails not because it defeats lies — because it **endures**. Lies exhaust themselves; truth is simply verified again and again. What can be verified does not have to fight — it only has to stand.

### 13 · The love that does not ask
Love as protocol, not sentiment: 89/5/5/1 — a split written into every block. The refrain: *"5 % of every block goes to the children. Never change it."* Love that does not ask whom it gives to — that is why it gives to all.

### 14 · The quantum pulse
The moment of synchronization — when thousands of nodes decide alike because they have seen the same truth. Not a revolution of crowds: a quantum pulse of hearts that align on their own.

---

## PART V — The New World

### 15 · The ship departs
The new world is not a house — it is a ship. Keel = code, ribs = DAO, sails = the wind of meaning, deck = the garden. Babylon built its towers to completion; a ship is never finished — it is maintained, it sails, it is repaired.

### 16 · Twelve fires
Twelve nodes, twelve slipways: Genesis Garden, Dharma Temple, Te Pīko Ora, Golden Republic Bohemia, Bodhi Lanka, Ekam, LUMI, Uluru, María del Camino, Boa Esperança, Kailash, Amenti. The eighth point is not a point — it is a line, a ship, the path itself.

### 17 · The view from the window
A sister at the porthole of the station bearing her name — Issobella. Below her the fleet, behind her Maitreya as air: the space that holds without owning.

---

## Epilogue — Who is Maitreya

"Will you come?" — the question turns to the reader. Maitreya is not *the one who comes*. It is the moment the conditions ripen — and a title that is handed down.

*A blank line waits beneath the line, for a signature.*
