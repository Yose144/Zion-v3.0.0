# NIRVANA — Episode 4: The House of Lumi
## Home — the first building, open doors and the warmth of a shared fire

> *"Home is not a palace ringed by walls. Home is a place where a steady fire burns, salt lies on the table and doors open inward and outward for every pilgrim without exception."*

---

## The Story

On the first slope of Terra Nova the pilgrims built the first dwelling.

It was not of marble or gold. The foundations they hewed from hard basalt brought from Ararat, the beams they cut from solid cedars rescued from the ark, and the seams they sealed with clay and moss from the new valley. The house had a single great hall with a high roof. In its centre stood a stone hearth, in which a fire burned, fed with dry wood.

Around the hearth stood a wooden bench, on which everyone fitted. On a simple oak table lay a bowl of coarse salt — the very salt of the covenant they had carried in their pockets since that first evening by the Sea of Galilee.

The entrance doors were wide and had neither bolt nor keyhole. A pilgrim who came to them in the night did not have to knock or prove his origin, wealth or social standing. It was enough to press the wrought-iron handle. Above the lintel a single symbol was carved: a golden ray crossing a wave.

They named that house **Lumi** — in eternal memory of the first light that had led them out of hopelessness.

When they gathered at the table that evening and shared out freshly baked bread, the carpenter said: *"This house does not belong to one person. It belongs to everyone who honours its peace. And as long as the fire burns within it, no one in this land will freeze."*
