The golden crystal of Hiranyagarbha rang out with a deep tone that resonated with the heartbeat of every living creature. People stepped out of their homes and looked up at the night sky. Above the continent spread a rainbow woven of billions of rays of light — a bridge joining the L1 bedrock, the L2 multichain bridges, the L3 consciousness, the L4 virtual temples, the L5 living nature and the L6 stellar quantum fire.

The old world of Babylon had no strength left to fight. It did not crumble under the blows of swords or the blasts of bombs. It simply **evaporated like a shadow at sunrise**, because no one remained within it who would give it their attention. All the miners, builders, artists, mothers and thinkers had turned their gaze toward the New Earth.

In the middle of the temple stood a pilgrim with the child Lumi at his side. He laid his hand on the crystal and spoke the words that had been written in the Hall of Records before the dawn of ages:

*"Mission Amenti is fulfilled.
The ark has landed.
The salt has not lost its savour.
