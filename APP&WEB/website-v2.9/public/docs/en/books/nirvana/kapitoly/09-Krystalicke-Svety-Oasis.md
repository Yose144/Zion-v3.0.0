# NIRVANA — Episode 9: The Crystalline Worlds of OASIS
## L4 Metaverse in Unreal Engine 5.7, Nanite/Lumen & instant WebGPU preview

> *"A virtual world must not be an escape from reality, but an architectural model and a sacred laboratory for the re-creation of the physical world."*

## The Story

On the shore of Terra Nova stood a gate of polished obsidian and crystal.

When a person walked through it, they did not find themselves in an empty digital cage of square pixels, such as the old corporations once built to sell virtual trinkets. They stepped into a living, breathtaking world — **OASIS**.

Everything was alive here. Sunbeams passed through the crowns of giant sequoias in real time, drops of dew on the leaves reflected the light of distant constellations, and in the deep canyons waterfalls roared with physically perfect flow. In the middle of the continent rose the majestic **Dharma Temple** — a temple of billions of finely carved white marble columns, where avatars from the whole world gathered.

Every avatar was the mirror of a human soul. It did not have to look like an ordinary body from the old world — someone walked as a being of pure light, someone as a winged guardian of the forest and another as a wise pilgrim with a staff. All their deeds, found treasures (*Golden Egg*) and earned experience were inscribed directly into the indestructible L1 chain.

And what was most beautiful: a person did not have to own a heavy and expensive machine to enter OASIS.

A fisherman on a wooden skiff pulled an ordinary pane of glass connected to the network from his pocket. He touched it — and within three seconds a full window into OASIS opened before him: smooth, radiant and instant.

## What it means

**L4 OASIS is a new dimension of human gathering, creativity and digital ownership built on the cutting-edge Unreal Engine 5.7 and universal web access.**

ZION 3.3 connects gaming realism with the decentralized web:

1. **Unreal Engine 5.7 Core:** The use of **Nanite 2.0** (unlimited geometric detail of temples and landscape) and **Lumen 2.0** (dynamic global illumination) for photorealistic rendering of the worlds of Terra Nova, Ararat and Te Piko Ora.
2. **MetaHuman Avatar Framework:** Full avatar integration with ZIS identity and L1 Soulbound attributes (reputation, time played, ethical rating).
3. **Instant WebGPU & WebAssembly Preview:** Instant access directly from a web browser (`oasis.zionterranova.com`) without the need to download tens of gigabytes of installation data.
4. **Low-Latency Pixel Streaming:** The ability to stream full UE 5.7 graphics at 4K/60fps directly from dedicated GPU Edge nodes to any mobile, tablet or older computer.
5. **Marketplace integration:** Items and artefacts created in OASIS can be traded directly on `market.zionterranova.com` with instant settlement in ZION flowers.
