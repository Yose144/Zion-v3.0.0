# Episode 6 — The Compass and the Invitation to Oasis

*A compass will not tell you exactly where you will arrive. It will only tell you that the direction you are walking at least does not lead in a circle.*

> *"He who asks 'how do I sign up' is asking for a map. He who asks 'why should I care' is asking for a compass."*

---

## The Story

The morning was different from all the ones before it. The ark stood on the bank of the dried riverbed with the ribs of its hull open — not yet finished, but already large enough to see how many people it could hold. The fountain's channel, which Hanuman had spent years clearing of stones, was clean. And for the first time since Tasteless had forgotten taste, more people came to the fire than could have fit in the old circle.

The carpenter, the pilgrim with the bowl, the shepherd with the flute, Rama, Sita, Hanuman, and the man who built the ship sat in a circle together with the entire town — the water carrier, the mercenary, the woman, the farmer, the blacksmith, the mother with her child, and dozens of others who had come simply to see what was being built.

"We have salt," said the carpenter. "We have dissolution," said the pilgrim with the bowl. "We have the taste of water," said the shepherd. "We have a path without a map and a bridge," said Rama, Sita, and Hanuman together. "We have a ship," said the man who built it.

"And what do we do with all of it now?" asked the woman who had stopped counting what she had left, and who over those few weeks had become the voice that all of Tasteless listened to.

The quiet knot-maker, who had sat by the fire the entire time only listening, finally stood. He took the stone he had carried since the first night and placed it in the center of the circle — not at the bottom of a pot as before, but where everyone could see it.

"I have nothing more to give you," he said. "You received salt from the carpenter. Dissolution from the pilgrim. The taste of water from the shepherd. The path and the bridge from Rama, Sita, and Hanuman. The ship from the builder. What I have is only one thing: **a compass**."

He turned the stone, and on its underside four marks were carved, one on each side.

"North is the question *why*," he said, pointing to the first mark. "East is the question of what is broken in that which we are leaving behind. South is the question of who I am when I set out on the journey. West is the question of where that journey truly leads. None of you needs to know the whole map. But each of you can know which way is north."

The farmer, who had not spoken a word the entire time, suddenly spoke up: "And what if I want more than just to know the direction? What if I want to see where that path truly leads — not as a question, but as a place I can step into?"

The knot-maker smiled — for the first time since that first night beneath the fig tree. "Then you need not wait for the end of the ark," he said. "Beyond this fire, a little further on, we are building one more thing. Not a ship for water. A ship for play, for story, for a world where what you have done — the water you carried, the stone you cleared, the salt you gave your neighbor — counts and grows with you. We call it **Oasis**."

## The Invitation to Oasis

The compass shows the direction. But a compass alone will carry you nowhere — that you must do yourself, with your own step. **Oasis** is the place where that step leads: a gaming and community layer atop the ZION network, where work, care, and participation are woven into your own story.

It is not a replacement for mining or for consensus — mining remains pure Proof-of-Work, regardless of what happens in Oasis. It is a **layer on top**, where a different rule applies: **what you do counts.**

### Nine Levels of Consciousness — a journey like Rama's, not a leap into a mapless forest

In Oasis there is no single "level up" button. There are nine levels — from **Physical** (first contact with the network) to **On The Star** (full integration) — inspired by the kabbalistic Tree of Life, where each degree corresponds to a different quality of participation, not merely a different number on an account.

- **Physical** — the first step, like the water carrier who drinks for the first time of what he carries for others.
- **Tiferet (Intuitional)** — the balance between giving and receiving, like the woman who gave salt to her neighbor without expectation.
- **Da'at (Unity)** — the node that bridges the network, like Hanuman, who built a bridge without anyone asking him to.
- **Keter (On The Star)** — full integration, the keystone that bears the entire structure.

You earn them through real activity — a valid share in mining, a found block, a vote in the DAO, a bug fix, running a node, documentation, long-term loyalty to the network (activity carries weight, but also its own decay during prolonged inactivity — just as salt, unused, loses its meaning).

### Avatars and Quests — a story you write yourself

In Oasis you are not an anonymous number. You have an avatar — one of dozens of characters, each with its own quest, its own path, its own reason for being here. Not because you must play a role. Because the story you have just finished reading — the salt, the dissolution, the taste of water, the path without a map, the bridge, the ark — does not end at the fire. It continues wherever you choose to step in.

### Golden Egg — a treasure scattered across the world of ZION

And for those who ask what lies at the end of the journey, there is one concrete, tangible goal: **Golden Egg** — a massive treasure hunt scattered across the entire ZION ecosystem. 108 cryptic clues hidden in the blockchain, in smart contracts, in the TerraNova books, and even at physical locations. Whoever deciphers them all earns a share of the genesis reserve set aside for precisely this purpose.

This is not a promise of profit without work. It is **Hanuman's bridge translated into a game**: a path each person must walk alone, step by step, riddle by riddle — but with a goal that is visible and verifiable from the very first moment.

> **Honesty note:** Golden Egg is a planned feature (launch targeted for 2027; the avatar system is currently in active development), not a treasure distributed today. The compass shows the direction — no one knows the exact date the water will arrive, but the direction from which the rain falls is already visible.
