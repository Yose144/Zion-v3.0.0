# I. Morning Mist Over the Valley of the First Step

After the night when Rádha brought joy and the tone of a bamboo flute to the fire, the sun rose above the cliffs.

When the mist dissolved, the landscape beneath the overhang no longer looked like an ordinary forest. Before the travelers opened a vast, luminous space — **the First World of Oasis**, known in ancient texts as **the Garden of Hiranyagarbha (The Realm of the Golden Womb)**.

At the center of the entire landscape towered the **Ancient Tree of Life**, its roots drinking water from the L1 blockchain and its branches forming nine heavenly tiers (9 levels of consciousness from Malkuth to Keter). Around the Tree lay **8 Genesis Territories**, each holding one of the key elements of a new civilization.

"This is not a virtual escape," said Morpheus, gesturing toward the valley. "This is a **trainer for a new reality**. Here we learn to govern land, found guilds, distribute resources, and search for the 108 traces of the Golden Egg without destroying the world around us."

# III. Geography of the First World of Oasis — 8 Genesis Territories

The First World of Oasis is not an endless wasteland. It is precisely structured into **8 Genesis Territories**, each with its own meaning, its own rule, and its own reward pool:

```
                         [ 09. KETER — The Stellar Horizon ]
                                       │
                         [ 05–08. Fifth through Eighth Tier ]
                                       │
               ╭───────────────────────┴───────────────────────╮
               │      01. MALKUTH — Valley of the First Step    │
               ╰───────────────────────┬───────────────────────╯
                                       │
     ╭─────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────╮
     │                                 │                                 │
[ 02. Zone of Code & Mining ]    [ 03. Garden of Service (Humanitarian) ]   [ 04. Guild City ]
 (Neo / Trinity / Miners)           (Hanuman / Sítá)                  (Morpheus / Guilds)
```

1. **Valley of the First Step (Malkuth Gateway):** The entry zone for all newcomers (Level 1 — Dormant / Physical). Here the first onboarding takes place — wallet verification and obtaining the first Avatar.
2. **Crystal Mines (Mining & Code Realm):** Territory dedicated to miners (Stream 1, 2, 3) and developers. Mining uptime and code contributions generate bonus XP here.
3. **Garden of Service (Humanitarian Sanctuary):** The place where the 5% Humanitarian Tithe from every block is visibly gathered. Players with the Giver Avatar decide here on specific grants and aid.
4. **Guild Citadel (Guild Keep):** The zone where the first Guilds are formed (max 100 members per guild). Guilds compete here for territory governance through fair auctions and contributions to the network (10 000 ZION claim cost).
5. **Temple of Hiranyagarbha (The Golden Womb Temple):** The central temple at the roots of the Tree of Life, where the first of the **108 traces of the Golden Egg** lies.

# IV. The Mechanics of Growth: From XP to the Golden Egg

How does life in the First World of Oasis work?

1. **Earn XP for real actions:**
   - Launching a node or miner → **+500 XP / day**
   - Reporting a bug or helping with documentation → **+1 000 XP**
   - Joining a Guild and participating in a community task → **+2 000 XP**
   - Contributing to a humanitarian project → **+1 500 XP**
2. **Progress through 9 Levels of Consciousness:**
   - From *Level 1 (Physical)* through *Level 5 (Cosmic)* to *Level 9 (OnTheStar)*.
   - With each higher level, your Avatar gains a new appearance, access to deeper quests, and the right to vote in DAO governance.
3. **Search for the 108 traces of the Golden Egg:**
   - 8.25 billion ZION from the genesis reserve is locked in 5 reward pools (Player Pool, Guild Pool, Territory Pool, Golden Egg Pool, Winners Pool).
   - The traces are hidden in blocks of the L1 chain, in smart contracts on Base/EVM, in the books of TerraNova, and in real geographic locations.
